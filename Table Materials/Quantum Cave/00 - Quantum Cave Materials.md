## Version notes

The Session 4 packet guide previously shared the filename `Quantum_Cave_Session_3_GM_Guide.md` with a different, longer standalone guide. It now has a distinct Session 4 title and path. Session 4 links to this revised packet guide; Session 3 links to the [[Archive/Preparation/The Last Grin/Quantum Cave Session 3 Guide|earlier standalone guide]]. Both texts are retained.

The November 9 handoff records newer pawn artwork in the associated project's `artifacts/quantum-cave-session4/` and delivered ZIPs. This restructuring preserves the vault's existing versions. Compare the project artwork before the next pawn revision; the move establishes locations, while content authority follows the current notes and explicit GM decisions.
