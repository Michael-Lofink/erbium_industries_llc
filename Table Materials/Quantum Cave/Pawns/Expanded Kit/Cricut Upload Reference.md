# Cricut Upload Reference

Expanded kit: eight electrovore pawns, three upright quantum stones, and eight reversible vent tokens. All dimensions below apply to the **complete uploaded image or arrangement**.

[[Table Materials/Quantum Cave/00 - Quantum Cave Materials|Quantum Cave materials]] · [[Table Materials/Quantum Cave/Pawns/Expanded Kit/ASSEMBLY|Full assembly instructions]] · [[Table Materials/Quantum Cave/Pawns/Expanded Kit/Pawn-Assembly.pdf|Assembly diagram]]

## 1. Upload the artwork

In Design Space, upload each linked PNG and choose **Flat Graphic** for Print Then Cut. Preserve the white areas inside the pieces; the surrounding background is already transparent. On the canvas, keep proportions locked and enter the width below; check the resulting height.

Each pawn sheet contains the upright bodies and matching base labels. The two vent sheets supply active and spent faces for the same eight tokens.

## 2. Print and cut

Choose **Make** and print through Design Space on Letter cardstock so Cricut’s sensor marks appear on each sheet. Use single-sided printing at **100% scale**, with Letter paper and portrait orientation selected. Place the printed sheet on the mat as shown in Design Space, select the setting for your cardstock, and cut.

## 3. Cut the backings

Upload the SVGs below with **Basic Cut** as the operation. Select all shapes belonging to one uploaded arrangement, check its locked dimensions, then **Attach** them to preserve their placement on the mat. Use the backing material and cutting setup that worked for your map.

The pawn bases finish at one inch across. Each vent backing measures **2″ × 1″**. The optional cores stiffen the upright pawn bodies.

## 4. Assemble

Glue each pawn’s base label to its matching backing. Score the body along the printed dashed lines and fold it so the artwork faces outward on both sides, enclosing the optional core if used. Fold the two feet outward and glue them onto the base. Follow the [[Table Materials/Quantum Cave/Pawns/Expanded Kit/Pawn-Assembly.pdf|illustrated assembly guide]] for the folds; make one pawn first to check your stock.

For each vent, glue matching **ACTIVE** and **SPENT** faces onto opposite sides of one backing. Match the identifiers so each token can be flipped during play.

## Optional position markers

Upload [[Table Materials/Quantum Cave/Pawns/Expanded Kit/Print-Then-Cut/Optional-Position-Markers.png|Optional-Position-Markers.png]] as **Flat Graphic / Print Then Cut** and set the complete image to **3.75″ × 0.5″**. This produces six half-inch A/B markers, which can stay as thin paper or cardstock.
