## Cricut workflow

1. Upload **Cricut/All-Pieces-Print-Then-Cut.png** as a Print Then Cut image. Set its locked dimensions to **4.75 × 3.5 inches**. The PNG has a transparent background and 13 separate opaque pieces.
2. Print and cut through Design Space on Letter cardstock. Keep all pieces at their supplied size. Enable bleed if desired.
3. Upload **Cricut/Required-Seven-Backings.svg** as Basic Cut. Its complete arranged size is **4.75 × 2.25 inches**. Attach the shapes to retain the arrangement. Cut the seven backing shapes, then glue on the matching artwork.
4. Use **Maps/Quantum-Cover-Placement.png** to place J, H1 and H2 at A. Keep the alternate B locations available for their shifts.

The whole set fits a 12 × 12 mat and your 11 × 14 stock; the longer mat also works. The 4.75 × 3.5-inch artwork fits comfortably within Letter Print Then Cut limits. Your completed room pieces and jigsaw connections stay as built.

**Individual pieces:** Artwork contains transparent 600-ppi PNGs; Cuts contains one matching silhouette SVG per piece. Set creature/stone widths to **1 inch**, marker widths to **0.5 inch**. **All-Pieces-Basic-Cut.svg** includes all 13 outlines if you want backed position markers too.

**Printing by hand:** Manual-Cut-Letter.pdf is an ordinary Letter print sheet. Print at **Actual size / 100%** and measure the one-inch square. For sensor-guided Cricut cuts, print through Design Space using the PNG.

**Material:** use the backing and blade setup that worked for your finished cave. Explore 4 supports Fine-Point and Deep-Point blades; Cricut's Knife Blade is a Maker tool. If a material setting requires Knife Blade, choose an Explore-compatible backing or cut that backing by hand. Stock dimensions alone do not determine blade compatibility.

## Rules in this revision

The cave begins Dark-Side. Q1–Q3 retain their independent observation cycles. J/H1/H2 use a round-start shift during combat, with **Fix Your Gaze** holding one through that shift. Fixed printed pillars and vents stay in place.

Deliberate interior Q1 cycling remains immediate; the **1d4+1-day** delay governs natural exterior resurfacing. OutCry IV requires settlement components for full repair. The maintenance case and abandoned cable support partial repairs.

Music links and cue timing are in **Session-4-Music.md**. Existing cave, anomaly and vision cues are retained. **Robot Getaway** is the first-choice electrovore combat cue.
