# Grinrunner Hunting Truck
## Vehicle 2

==Huge== ==Tech==

**Source** Homebrew
**Price** 1,200 credits
**Description** A patched six-wheeled utility truck with a two-seat cab, rear bench seats, and a reinforced cargo bed. Its mismatched panels bear electrical scorch marks. Hooks, restraint cables, and a folding loading ramp accommodate electrovore carcasses and recovered salvage.

---

**Space** 20 feet long, 10 feet wide, 8 feet high
**Crew** 1 pilot; **Passengers** 6
**Piloting Check** Piloting (DC 16)

---

**AC** 13; **Fort** +8
**Hardness** 5; **HP** 40 (BT 20)
**Immunities** object immunities

---

**Speed** 35 feet (motorized)
**Collision** 2d8 bludgeoning (DC 16)

---

**All-Terrain Suspension** The truck ignores difficult terrain caused by loose gravel and shallow sand. It treats greater difficult terrain from these surfaces as difficult terrain. This ability does not function while the truck is broken.

**Passenger Layout** One passenger rides beside the pilot. Five passengers fit on folding benches in the open cargo bed. The bed's side panels provide standard cover against attacks from creatures on the ground outside the truck, but no cover against attacks from above.

**Salvage Bed** With its rear benches folded away, the truck can carry up to 100 Bulk of cargo instead of its five rear passengers. Each occupied rear passenger seat reduces this capacity by 20 Bulk.

**Loading Winch** A powered winch can drag an unattended object of up to 100 Bulk up the loading ramp and into the cargo bed over 1 minute. It cannot restrain an unwilling creature or function while the truck is broken.

**Exposed Transport** The truck is not airtight and provides no breathable air or environmental protection. Occupants must rely on their own equipment.

**Equipment** Mounted headlights, planetary-range comm unit, loading winch, and cargo restraints. The truck has no mounted weapons.
