**Source** [Battlecry!](/Sources.aspx?ID=268) pg. 60

Eschewing straightforward clashes of armies on the fields of war, you favor covert, hit-and-run techniques that make your environment your battlefield. You specialize in using your surroundings to your advantage, striking from the shadows and disappearing before your foes realize what hit them.

Your need to avoid notice means you are likely to develop particular deftness with weapons that allow you to strike from a distance, using whatever ammunition the environment provides. Blowguns and slings are weapons often favored by guerrillas in particular, their simple efficacy enhanced with easily crafted poisons.

You're skilled in subtle tactics and using the environment against your enemies. You become trained in your choice of  [Deception](/Skills.aspx?ID=38) or [Thievery](/Skills.aspx?ID=50); if you are already trained in both, you become an expert in one instead. When you take this dedication, choose a single, discrete urban or wilderness location, such as “Mzali” or “the Verduran Forest”; this becomes your favored location. When you are in this location and [undetected](/Conditions.aspx?ID=96) by all enemies, you can [Sneak](/Actions.aspx?ID=2405) without attempting a Stealth check as long as you move no more than half your Speed. During exploration, this also allows you to automatically approach within 15 feet of other creatures while Avoiding their Notice, as long as they aren't actively [Searching](/Actions.aspx?ID=2630) or on guard.  <br /> <br />You can change your favored location by spending 1 week of downtime in the new location you would like to set as your  favored location.

You can capably deliver toxins with a [blowgun](/Weapons.aspx?ID=424). Your blowgun [Strikes](/Actions.aspx?ID=2306) can apply [injury](/Traits.aspx?ID=635) [poisons](/Traits.aspx?ID=669) even if they deal no damage due to a creature's resistance. If you critically succeed at a blowgun Strike using a poisoned dart, the target's initial save against the poison is one degree of success worse than the creature rolls; this is a [misfortune](/Traits.aspx?ID=654) effect.

 In addition, if you make a blowgun Strike while [hidden](/Conditions.aspx?ID=79) or [undetected](/Conditions.aspx?ID=96), you don't automatically become [observed](/Conditions.aspx?ID=84). Instead, immediately attempt a [Stealth](/Skills.aspx?ID=48) check against the Perception DC of the target. If you succeed, you don't become observed, and are hidden (if you were undetected, you still become hidden rather than remaining undetected).

You are especially skilled in transforming simple weapons that can be easily assembled from readily available materials into deadly weapons. In your hands, a blowgun's base damage increases from 1 to a 1d4 damage die, and it gains the deadly  d4 trait. In addition, you gain the following action.  

**Guerrilla Assault** <actions string="Single Action" /> **Frequency** once per round; **Effect** You Interact to reload a sling or blowgun and then Strike. If the Strike is successful and you were undetected or unnoticed by the target when you made the attack, you are now [hidden](/Conditions.aspx?ID=79) from the target after the attack, as they cannot tell  where the attack came from.

You prepare your sling stones with small, edged grooves to enable them to deliver poison, and you have learned other  techniques for poisoning your weapons. You can apply injury poisons to sling bullets, allowing them to deliver such poisons  on a successful Strike. 

During your daily preparations, you can prepare a number of simple injury poisons equal to your level that can only be applied to sling bullets or blowgun darts. These follow the rules for [injury](/Traits.aspx?ID=635) poisons, except that they deal 1d4 poison damage with no saving throw. Only you can apply these poisons properly, and they expire at your next daily preparations. 

**Special** If you later gain the [Poison Weapon](/Feats.aspx?ID=4933) feat (such as from the poisoner archetype or the rogue multiclass archetype), you can apply your injury poisons to any weapon that is normally a valid receptacle for injury poisons, but you do not gain additional simple injury poisons during your daily preparations.

You know methods of sabotaging your enemy's war machines and weaponry. You can attempt to sabotage a siege weapon or  vehicle with a [Thievery](/Skills.aspx?ID=50) check against the standard DC of the weapon or vehicle's level.  <br />**Critical Success** The next time the weapon is Launched or the vehicle moves, it targets or moves to a square of your  choice instead of its intended target.  <br />**Success** As critical success, but the weapon or vehicle targets or moves to a random square.  <br />**Failure** The sabotage fails.  

Additionally, you can use Battlefront Sabotage to  sabotage a weapon an enemy is wielding. Choose a creature wielding a melee or ranged weapon within your reach and  attempt a Thievery check against their Reflex DC. On a  success, the weapon's wielder must succeed at a DC 11 flat check the next time it attempts a Strike with that weapon or the action is disrupted, as part of the weapon's structural integrity falters. The creature is then temporarily immune to Battlefront Sabotage of their weapons for 10 minutes.

Your diminishment of your foes' ranks damages their morale. Whenever you reduce a creature to 0 HP with a Strike or spell, all enemies within 30 feet of the downed creature who witnessed the attack must attempt a Will saving throw against your class DC. A creature that didn't see you directly (such as if you're invisible or remained hidden after the attack) takes a –2 circumstance penalty to this save. Regardless  of the result, each creature is temporarily immune to your Frightful Attrition for 10 minutes.  <br />**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  <br />**Success** The creature is [frightened](/Conditions.aspx?ID=76) 1.  <br />**Failure** The creature is frightened 2.  <br />**Critical Failure** The creature is frightened 3 and fleeing for 1 round.

You move and attack with deadly silence, unbelievable speed, and unlimited ferocity. Strike with a [blowgun](/Weapons.aspx?ID=424) or [sling](/Weapons.aspx?ID=430); if the Strike is successful, the enemy cannot tell where it came from and you remain hidden. Immediately after the successful Strike, you can [Sneak](/Actions.aspx?ID=2405) to a new location, Interact to reload a blowgun or sling, and Strike again, remaining hidden on a successful Strike. If the second Strike is successful, you can Sneak, Interact to reload, and then Strike with a sling or blowgun a third time, remaining hidden after a  successful Strike.  

After using Lonely Army, you are fatigued for 1 minute.

Your carefully prepared and placed shot brings instant death to your target. Attempt a Strike with a [blowgun](/Weapons.aspx?ID=424) or [sling](/Weapons.aspx?ID=430) using ammunition that you have poisoned with your simple injury poison. If the Strike is successful and you are hidden from the target, or unnoticed or undetected by them, the target must succeed at a Fortitude save against your class DC or die; this is a [death](/Traits.aspx?ID=571) and [incapacitation](/Traits.aspx?ID=631) effect. A creature that survives is temporarily immune to the instant death effect of Deathblow for 1 day.
