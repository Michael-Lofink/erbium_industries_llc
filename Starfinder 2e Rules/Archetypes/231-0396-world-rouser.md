Life abounds from the highest mountain peaks to the driest deserts to the deepest seas, worlds that nurture plants and shelter animals. But you know worlds are wonders beyond merely sustaining life. A world like Golarion can cage a god, can recover from 1,000 years of darkness, can destroy a civilization like Tar Khadurrm.

You know what worlds can truly do—and why they don’t. You know they sleep, as they have since almost the moment they were created. But more importantly, you know how to wake a world, if only for a few moments. You call to them, and they stir from their slumber. And during those brief moments where they wake, these worlds speak back to you, whispering in a language only you can hear. Alert, they take actions to sustain you, working their will via the animals, plants, and terrain that call it home. 

Those who share this secret are known as world rousers. On Golarion, they are most common among those who live in the wild—[druids](/Classes.aspx?ID=34) and [rangers](/Classes.aspx?ID=36) especially—where the voice of the world isn’t drowned out by the noise of civilization. [Gnomes](/Ancestries.aspx?ID=61), with their roots in the restless [First World](/Planes.aspx?ID=35), often learn to bestir the world, as do those who live along [ley lines](/Rules.aspx?ID=1531) and where the barrier between [the Universe](/Planes.aspx?ID=26) and the First World is thin. In those places, the world seems to rest more fitfully.

You know how to wake the world around you from its slumber, whether through your natural affinity for nature or hard-earned knowledge. You gain the Rouse the World action. 

As your ability to converse with the world grows, you can call on it to help you in other ways, some of which require a saving throw. The DC for an ability gained via this archetype is either your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher, and is called your world rouser DC.

**Rouse the World** <actions string="Single Action" /> ([concentrate](/Traits.aspx?ID=561), [primal](/Traits.aspx?ID=676)) The flora, fauna, and elements in a 10- foot burst within 30 feet stir, aiding you and your allies and whispering in your ear. This area is known as your waking world. While in your waking world, you and your allies gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [Athletics](/Skills.aspx?ID=36) and [Acrobatics](/Skills.aspx?ID=34) checks, and you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [Nature](/Skills.aspx?ID=43) checks to [Recall Knowledge](/Actions.aspx?ID=2367) about creatures and hazards in the area. The effect lasts for 10 minutes, but you can [Dismiss](/Actions.aspx?ID=2311) it. Once per round on subsequent turns, you can [Sustain](/Actions.aspx?ID=2317) this ability to increase the burst’s size by 10 feet. You can Rouse the World in only one area at a time; if you use this ability again, the first area returns to normal.

When you Point Out the creatures in this way, the action does not have the [visual](/Traits.aspx?ID=727) or [manipulate](/Traits.aspx?ID=645) traits, but still requires your allies to be able to hear and understand you.

Your waking world returns to slumber, and lays the peace of deep sleep on all within it. You [Dismiss](/Actions.aspx?ID=2311) your waking world, and all creatures in the area must attempt a Will save against your world rouser DC. [Animals](/Traits.aspx?ID=531), [beasts](/Traits.aspx?ID=547), and [plants](/Traits.aspx?ID=668) native to this world treat the result of their save as one degree worse. Creatures in the area then become temporarily immune to this ability for 1 hour.

**Success** The creature is unaffected.<br />
**Failure** The creature can’t use reactions for 1 round.<br />
**Critical Failure** As failure, but the creature is also [slowed](/Conditions.aspx?ID=92) 1.

Animals, plants, and the earth itself rush to protect you from harm, interposing themselves between you and danger. You and your allies in your waking world gain [cover](/Rules.aspx?ID=2559) from enemies you are aware of until the start of your next turn. The cover is based on the number of actions you spend. If you use one action, it is lesser cover; if you spend two actions, it is standard cover.

Dust and pollen fill your waking world, obscuring creatures from sight. All creatures within your waking world become [concealed](/Conditions.aspx?ID=62), and all creatures outside the area become concealed to creatures within it until the start of your next turn. In addition, a creature that ends its turn in the area must attempt a Fortitude saving throw against your world rouser DC as it inhales the particles. On a failure, it is [dazzled](/Conditions.aspx?ID=65) until the end of its next turn or [blinded](/Conditions.aspx?ID=59) on a critical failure.

Sound is absorbed in your waking world, covering everything in a shroud of quiet until the start of your next turn. Creatures in your waking world become hidden to [imprecise hearing](/Rules.aspx?ID=2407) or [concealed](/Conditions.aspx?ID=62) to [precise hearing](/Rules.aspx?ID=2406). A creature in your waking world attempting to use spell or effect with the [auditory](/Traits.aspx?ID=541) or [sonic](/Traits.aspx?ID=697) trait must succeed at a DC 4 [flat check](/Rules.aspx?ID=333); on a failure, the spell or effect is [disrupted](/Rules.aspx?ID=2236). Anytime a creature in the area is affected by an auditory or sonic effect, they must attempt a DC 16 flat check. On a success, they are not affected by the auditory or sonic effect, though they still may be affected by other aspects of an ability (for example, a creature hit by a weapon with a [_thundering_](/Equipment.aspx?ID=2850) rune would not take sonic damage but would still take the normal damage from the weapon).

A willing ally on the ground in your waking world is swaddled in earth. For each source of [persistent damage](/Conditions.aspx?ID=86) it is taking, the creature can immediately attempt a [flat check](/Rules.aspx?ID=333) to recover, with the DC reduction from appropriate assistance. While [swallowed](/MonsterAbilities.aspx?ID=77), the creature becomes [paralyzed](/Conditions.aspx?ID=85) and cannot be targeted or affected by any new effects (ongoing effects, such as [afflictions](/Rules.aspx?ID=2765) or persistent damage, continue to affect it as normal). At the start of your next turn, the creature regains 6d8 Hit Points and emerges in its previous location. If another creature is in that location, it is pushed to the nearest available space. The target creature then becomes temporarily immune to this ability until your next daily preparations.

The ground in your waking world opens to consume a creature standing on it, which must attempt a Reflex save against your world rouser DC. The creature must be Medium or smaller; if your waking world is a 20-foot [burst](/Rules.aspx?ID=2385), the creature can be Large or smaller, or Huge or smaller if your waking world is a 30-foot burst or larger. If you use this ability while your waking world already has a creature [grabbed](/Conditions.aspx?ID=77) or devoured, that creature is automatically freed. If your waking world ends, any creature affected by this ability is automatically freed.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.<br />
**Success** The creature takes 3d6 bludgeoning damage.<br />
**Failure** The creature takes 6d6 bludgeoning damage and is [grabbed](/Conditions.aspx?ID=77) by the earth. The creature can attempt to [Escape](/Actions.aspx?ID=2296) against your world rouser DC.<br />
**Critical Failure** The creature is pulled into the earth, which closes above it. It takes 6d6 bludgeoning damage immediately and is devoured. While devoured, it becomes grabbed and [slowed](/Conditions.aspx?ID=92) 1, and takes 6d6 bludgeoning damage at the end of each of its turns, and cannot be targeted by any effect. A creature can attempt to Escape against your world rouser DC; in addition to the normal checks to Escape, it can attempt a [Nature](/Domains.aspx?ID=85) check. If it Escapes, it is returned to the area where it was when grabbed. A creature with a [burrow Speed](/Rules.aspx?ID=2348) can Escape automatically and move up to its burrow Speed as part of that action.

When you Rouse the World, it awakens with a mighty yawp that stuns the unsuspecting. All enemies in the area must attempt a Will save against your world rouser DC. A creature that attempts this save or observes this ability becomes temporarily immune to it for 24 hours.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.<br />
**Success** The creature is [frightened](/Conditions.aspx?ID=76) 1.<br />
**Failure** The creature becomes frightened 2 and [stunned](/Conditions.aspx?ID=93) 1.<br />
**Critical Failure** The creature becomes frightened 3 and stunned 1.
