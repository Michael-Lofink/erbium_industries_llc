A promising youth growing up in a Chelaxian noble house often receives specialized training to master the house’s traditional arts, fight effectively, and survive court intrigue. Such a Chelaxian scion can select from the archetype’s feats. Each house also provides access to additional feats that function as archetype feats, with levels that sometimes differ from those of the original feats.

A noble house has invested in your training, imparting special techniques the family has honed over generations. You gain [Additional Lore](/Feats.aspx?ID=5114) as a skill feat, choosing from among Cheliax Lore, Devil Lore, Hell Lore, or a Lore associated with your house. In addition, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [Deception](/Skills.aspx?ID=38) checks, [Diplomacy](/Skills.aspx?ID=39) checks, [Intimidation](/Skills.aspx?ID=40) checks, and your Will DC against Chelaxian citizens and [devils](/MonsterFamilies.aspx?ID=389).

Through consistent (and often punishing) practice, you’ve learned how to use your house’s preferred weapons. You have familiarity with either one martial weapon of your choice; two simple or martial weapons of your choice from your house’s preferred weapons list; or one advanced weapon of your choice from your house’s preferred weapons list. For the purpose of proficiency, treat any of these selections that are martial weapons as simple weapons and any that are advanced weapons as martial weapons. If you’re at least an expert in one of the selected weapons, you get the critical specialization effect of that weapon when you get a critical hit with it. 

**Charthagnion**: Weapons with the [disarm](/Traits.aspx?ID=577) or [parry](/Traits.aspx?ID=667) trait <br />**Elliendo**: [Brawling](/WeaponGroups.aspx?ID=4) weapons and [knives](/WeaponGroups.aspx?ID=9) <br />**Henderthane**: [Hammers](/WeaponGroups.aspx?ID=8) and [picks](/WeaponGroups.aspx?ID=10) <br />**Jeggare**: One-handed [swords](/WeaponGroups.aspx?ID=15) <br />**Leroung**: [Clubs](/WeaponGroups.aspx?ID=5) and [shields](/WeaponGroups.aspx?ID=12) <br />**Narikopolus**: [Bows](/WeaponGroups.aspx?ID=3) <br />**Sarini**: [Flails](/Weapons.aspx?ID=374) <br />**Thrune**: [Clubs](/WeaponGroups.aspx?ID=5) and [crossbows](/WeaponGroups.aspx?ID=17) 

In addition, you gain the For My House! action. 

**For My House!** <actions string="Free Action" /> **Frequency** once per minute; **Effect** You announce your house affiliation, either by declaring it aloud (giving this ability the [auditory](/Traits.aspx?ID=541) trait) or openly displaying your house heraldry (giving this ability the [visual](/Traits.aspx?ID=727) trait). Until the beginning of your next turn, your weapon Strikes and spells deal an additional 1 damage to any creature that witnessed the display. If you’re an expert in [Cheliax Lore](/Skills.aspx?ID=41), Genealogy Lore, Heraldry Lore, [Performance](/Skills.aspx?ID=45), or Warfare Lore, the additional damage increases to 2. The damage increases to 3 if you’re a master in the skill or 4 if you’re legendary in the skill.

A true aristocrat knows how to encourage their best hirelings, ensuring loyalty and future excellence. You praise the triggering ally, granting them one of the following benefits. The ally is then immune to this ability for 1 day. 

**Eagerness:** Invigorated by praise, the ally can [Feint](/Actions.aspx?ID=1434), [Interact](/Actions.aspx?ID=2297) with a nearby object, [Leap](/Actions.aspx?ID=2298), [Reload](/Rules.aspx?ID=2196), or [Step](/Actions.aspx?ID=2304) as a reaction. 

**Persistence:** For 1 minute, the ally gains [temporary Hit Points](/Rules.aspx?ID=2321) equal to half your level plus your Charisma modifier. 

**Validation:** If the ally has the [panache class feature](/Classes.aspx?ID=63), they gain panache.

Like the [archdevil](/DeityCategories.aspx?ID=4) [Dispater](/Deities.aspx?ID=397), you maintain an unshakable poise even under impossible stress. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to the triggering saving throw or to your Will DC against the triggering effect. If you critically succeed at the saving throw (or if the source of the effect critically fails its skill check, such as to [Demoralize](/Actions.aspx?ID=2395) you), you gain [temporary Hit Points](/Rules.aspx?ID=2321) equal to half your level until the end of the effect’s source’s next turn. If the effect originated from a creature, you can Demoralize that creature once as a free action on your next turn; if the creature was temporarily immune to your attempts to Demoralize it, it loses that immunity.
