Warriors can draw powers from the joy and the sadness in their hearts, spiritual gifts and burdens they can share and give to others. They cultivate experiences from their adventures to nurture and grow their spiritual power, learning to channel it through martial arts techniques tailored to their style and personality. Once they’ve created a martial art that combines a favored weapon and specialized unarmed techniques, spirit warriors learn to channel spirit energy through their limbs and weapons. Spirit warriors are typically found in the Forest of Spirits, the Chuyokai Forest, and the Valashmai Jungle.

You’re a warrior who trains your spirit and body to work in perfect harmony, enhancing your attacks with your spiritual energy while fighting with a ferocious martial technique that combines blade and fist. The damage die for your fist changes to 1d6 instead of 1d4, and your fist gains the [parry](/Traits.aspx?ID=667) trait. You don’t take the normal –2 circumstance penalty when making a lethal attack with your fist or any other unarmed attacks. You gain the Overwhelming Combination action.

**Activate—Overwhelming Combination** <actions string="Single Action" /> ([flourish](/Traits.aspx?ID=606))   **Requirements** You’re wielding a one-handed melee weapon or a melee weapon with the agile or finesse trait; **Effect** Make two [Strikes](/Actions.aspx?ID=1167) against a target within your reach, one with the required weapon and one with your fist [unarmed](/Traits.aspx?ID=719) attack. If both hit the same target, combine their damage for the purposes of its [resistances](/Rules.aspx?ID=2893) and weaknesses. Apply your multiple attack penalty to each Strike normally.

You’ve sworn an oath to defend the helpless from dangerous titanic beasts, including [kaiju](/Traits.aspx?ID=383) that roam the surface of Golarion. Attacks made as part of your Overwhelming Combination ability gain a +4 circumstance bonus to damage against a creature at least 2 sizes larger than you, or +6 if you have master proficiency with the weapon you used. You also gain a +2 circumstance bonus to saving throws and DCs against kaiju hazards. You gain the following edict. 

**Edict** You must do everything within your power to protect others from massive creatures they can’t defend themselves from; in the event the victims are evil or actively trying to harm you or other innocents, you don’t have to save them.

You’ve sworn an oath to protect the holy places within nature and the spirits that dwell within, from ancient trees to primordial wild guardians. You can use [Diplomacy](/Skills.aspx?ID=39) to [Make an Impression](/Actions.aspx?ID=2392) on animals and to make very simple [Requests](/Actions.aspx?ID=2393) of them. In most cases, wild animals will give you time to make your case. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to Diplomacy checks to Make an Impression when interacting with a [beast](/Traits.aspx?ID=547), [fey](/Traits.aspx?ID=599), or [kami](/Traits.aspx?ID=354). You gain the following edict. 

**Edict** You must aid any animal or nature spirit in need unless it violates your other tenets or puts someone else at risk of immediate harm.

You’ve sworn an oath to ferret out and destroy malevolent shapechangers who pose as mortals with evil intent. You gain a +4 circumstance bonus to Perception checks to detect a shapechanged creature’s disguise, and a +2 circumstance bonus to attempts to [Recall Knowledge](/Actions.aspx?ID=2367) about shapechangers. Whenever you use Overwhelming Combination against a shapechanged creature, you attempt to counteract one polymorph effect on that creature. The counteract rank is half your level rounded up, and the [counteract](/Rules.aspx?ID=2250) check modifier is your Charisma modifier + your proficiency bonus with the weapon used to [Strike](/Actions.aspx?ID=2306) as part of your Overwhelming Combination. You gain the following edict.

**Edict** You must reveal and slay evil or predatory shapechangers you discover or encounter, as long as you have a reasonable chance of success.

Your spiritual power pierces both body and soul, pinning your foe in place. Make a melee [Strike](/Actions.aspx?ID=2306) with a one-handed, [agile](/Traits.aspx?ID=526), or [finesse](/Traits.aspx?ID=602) weapon, or your fist unarmed attack. On a successful hit, the target must succeed at a Fortitude save against the higher of your class DC or your spell DC, or become [immobilized](/Conditions.aspx?ID=81). The target can end this immobilization with a successful [Escape](/Actions.aspx?ID=2296) attempt using the higher of your class DC or your spell DC as the Escape DC.
