<title level="3">Multiclass Barbarian Characters</title>

The barbarian archetype is a great choice for characters who emphasize Strength and melee attacks more than usual for their class, as long as they can handle Rage's spellcasting restriction. It's especially good for characters looking to add more damage. 
<ul><li>Alchemist barbarians can enhance themselves with potent mutagens before releasing their fury.</li><li>Champion barbarians might enter a zealous rage when their deity is offended and diplomacy is no longer an option.</li><li>Druid barbarians transform into mighty beasts and monsters before entering a rage.</li><li>Monk barbarians can use the enhanced emotional state of rage to flow with their stance.</li><li>Rogue barbarians are ruffians who thrive in a scrap and are happy to let their anger take control.</li><li>Spellcaster barbarians might wait until they're out of better options, using rage as a last resort.</li></ul>

You become trained in Athletics; if you were already trained in [Athletics](/Skills.aspx?ID=36), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. You become trained in barbarian class DC. You can use the Rage action. While raging, you take a –1 penalty to AC. Choose an instinct as you would if you were a barbarian. You have that instinct for all purposes, but you don’t gain any of the other abilities it grants. If that instinct has anathema, you’re bound by them.
