You can create natural remedies. You gain the [advanced alchemy benefits](/Rules.aspx?ID=3282). You can use advanced alchemy to create 4 [alchemical](/Traits.aspx?ID=528) [consumables](/Traits.aspx?ID=564) with the [healing](/Traits.aspx?ID=623) trait, though the number is reduced by 2 if you didn’t make your [daily preparations](/Rules.aspx?ID=2575) in the wilderness. These consumables are called your herbal items. You remember herbal item [formulas](/Rules.aspx?ID=2214) and don’t need a [formula book](/Equipment.aspx?ID=2724) for them.

You become an expert in [Nature](/Skills.aspx?ID=43) and can use Nature instead of [Crafting](/Skills.aspx?ID=37) to [Craft](/Actions.aspx?ID=2385) alchemical consumables with the healing trait. You don’t need to be trained in Crafting to do so, and you can use [healer’s tools](/Equipment.aspx?ID=2727) instead of [alchemist’s tools](/Equipment.aspx?ID=2702).

When using [Natural Medicine](/Feats.aspx?ID=5182) to [Treat Wounds](/Actions.aspx?ID=2399), you gain the +2 circumstance bonus from having fresh ingredients, even if not in wilderness. If you do this in the wilderness, you gain a +4 circumstance bonus instead.

When you create an [elixir](/Traits.aspx?ID=589) as an herbal item, you can prepare it as a poultice instead. A poultice gains the [oil](/Traits.aspx?ID=663) trait (but remains [alchemical](/Traits.aspx?ID=528), not [magical](/Traits.aspx?ID=644)) and loses the elixir trait. You can apply a poultice by [Interacting](/Actions.aspx?ID=2297) with one hand. When you apply a poultice, in addition to the item’s normal effects, the recipient can attempt an immediate flat check to recover from a single source of [persistent](/Conditions.aspx?ID=86) acid, bleed, or fire damage, with the DC reduction from appropriate assistance.

Your herbalist talents continue to grow with use and you have become potent at making even more remedies from less. You can create up to 6 herbal items per day with advanced alchemy.

**Special** At 10th level or higher, you can select this feat a second time to increase the number to 8.

The herbal items you create use medicinal plants endemic to your location. When a creature consumes one of your herbal items, that creature gains the benefit matching the location where you created that item. If the benefit is not immediate, it lasts for 1 minute unless otherwise stated. 
<ul><li>**Aquatic** Gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Fortitude saves.</li><li>**Arctic** For 1 hour, treat [environmental cold effects](/Rules.aspx?ID=2769) as if they were one step less severe.</li><li>**Desert** For 1 hour, treat environmental heat effects as if they were one step less severe.</li><li>**Forest** Gain a +2 circumstance bonus to saves against [disease](/Traits.aspx?ID=578) and [poison](/Traits.aspx?ID=669) effects.</li><li>**Mountain** Gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Reflex saves.</li><li>**Plains** Gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Will saves.</li><li>**Swamp** Remove one source of [persistent](/Conditions.aspx?ID=86) bleed damage.</li><li>**Underground** Gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [Perception](/Rules.aspx?ID=2298).</li></ul>
