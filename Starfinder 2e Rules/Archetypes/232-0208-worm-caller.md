You follow the ancient [hryngar](/MonsterFamilies.aspx?ID=38) tradition of the worm caller—mystics who can call upon the strength and destructive force of the mighty [cave worms](/MonsterFamilies.aspx?ID=21) that inhabit the Darklands. You can manifest worms to attack your enemies, draw upon their senses and resistances, summon one to do your bidding and, eventually, become a worm yourself.

You pledge yourself to understanding and emulating one of the most powerful creatures of the Darklands: the mighty [cave worm](/MonsterFamilies.aspx?ID=21). You gain the following action.

 **Shake It Off** <actions string="Single Action" /> ([fortune](/Traits.aspx?ID=76), [primal](/Traits.aspx?ID=134)) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You fail or critically fail a saving throw against a condition or adverse effect (such as [_baleful polymorph_](/Spells.aspx?ID=17)); **Effect** You reroll the triggering save and use the better result.

**Special** You can't select another dedication feat until you have gained two other feats from the Worm Caller archetype.

Your flesh grows thick and bloated, protecting you against harm. You gain resistance equal to half your level versus one of the following types of damage, chosen when you take the feat: fire, cold, or bludgeoning. This choice cannot be changed.

 When you're unarmored, your flesh gives you a +2 item bonus to AC with a Dexterity cap of +3. You can apply with armor potency runes and property runes directly to your flesh, though excruciatingly painful.

**Special** You can take this feat up to three times, each time selecting a new damage type from the list.

 When using [Worm Form](/Feats.aspx?ID=4368) you are immune to penalties to your Speed and the immobilized condition, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to your Fortitude or Reflex DC against attempts to [Shove](/Actions.aspx?ID=38) or [Trip](/Actions.aspx?ID=40) you, and you ignore [difficult terrain](/Rules.aspx?ID=453) and greater difficult terrain.

You call forth a brood of infantile [cave worms](/MonsterFamilies.aspx?ID=21) to feast on your enemy's flesh. Choose a creature within 30 feet; the creature must attempt a Fortitude save. At 8th level and every 2 levels above that, the [persistent piercing damage](/Conditions.aspx?ID=29) taken on a failure increases by 1d6.

**Critical Success** The worms writhe away harmlessly.<br />
**Success** The target takes 2d6 piercing damage and 2d6 persistent piercing damage.<br />
**Failure** The target takes 6d6 piercing damage and 2d6 persistent piercing damage.<br />
**Critical Failure** As failure, plus the target is [flat-footed](/Conditions.aspx?ID=16) as long as it takes persistent piercing damage.

Furthermore, while swimming on the surface of a liquid, you can [Leap](/Actions.aspx?ID=81) out of the liquid and do not fall until the end of your turn, allowing you to spend the rest of the turn above the surface.

You call forth a ghostly manifestation of the worm spirit. Choose an intersection on the ground within 60 feet; the ghostly maw of a worm emerges from the ground, attacking all creatures in a 10 foot burst. Each creature in the area must attempt a Reflex save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.<br />
**Success** The target takes 1d10+13 bludgeoning damage as it is battered by the worm's body.<br />
**Failure** The target takes 3d10+15 piercing damage as it is bitten by the worm. The target is [immobilized](/Conditions.aspx?ID=24) until the end of its next turn.<br />
**Critical Failure** As failure, but the damage increases to 6d10+30 and the target is knocked [prone](/Conditions.aspx?ID=31).

You take on the form of a purple worm or transform into a humanoid-worm hybrid, retaining your own head but with a worm's body, transforming into a Huge battle form. You must have enough space to expand into or the ability is lost. While in this form, you gain the [animal](/Traits.aspx?ID=9) trait. You can Dismiss the form.

 You gain the following statistics and abilities regardless of which battle form you choose: 
<ul><li>AC = 20 + your level. Ignore your armor's check penalty and Speed reduction.</li><li>40 temporary Hit Points.</li><li>[Darkvision](/Rules.aspx?ID=415).</li><li>One or more [unarmed](/Traits.aspx?ID=199) melee attacks specific to the battle form you choose, which are the only attacks you can Strike with. You're trained with them. Your attack modifier is +25, and you use the listed damage. Melee attacks are Strength based (for the purpose of the [enfeebled](/Conditions.aspx?ID=13) condition, for example). If your unarmed attack modifier is higher, you can use it instead.</li><li>[Athletics](/Skills.aspx?ID=3) modifier of +30, unless your own modifier is higher.</li></ul> You also gain specific abilities based on the type of monster you choose: 
<ul><li>**Purple Worm** Speed 40 feet, burrow 30 feet, swim 20 feet; **Melee** <actions string="Single Action" /> jaws ([deadly 2d10](/Traits.aspx?ID=174), [reach 15 feet](/Traits.aspx?ID=192)), **Damage** 3d10+15 piercing; **Melee** <actions string="Single Action" /> stinger ([agile](/Traits.aspx?ID=170), [poison](/Traits.aspx?ID=126), [reach 15 feet](/Traits.aspx?ID=192)), **Damage** 2d12+15 piercing plus 2d6 [persistent poison](/Conditions.aspx?ID=29); **Melee** <actions string="Single Action" /> body ([reach 15 feet](/Traits.aspx?ID=192)) **Damage** 1d10+13 bludgeoning</li><li>**Hybrid** Speed 40 feet; **Melee** <actions string="Single Action" /> stinger ([agile](/Traits.aspx?ID=170), [poison](/Traits.aspx?ID=126), [reach 10 feet](/Traits.aspx?ID=192)), **Damage** 2d12+15 piercing plus 1d6 [persistent poison](/Conditions.aspx?ID=29); **Melee** <actions string="Single Action" /> body ([reach 10 feet](/Traits.aspx?ID=192)), **Damage** 1d10+13 bludgeoning; **Hybrid Caster** You retain the ability to speak, and can cast spells as long as they don't have somatic or material components.</li></ul>
