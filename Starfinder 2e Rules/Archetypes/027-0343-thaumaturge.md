<title level="3">Multiclass Thaumaturge Characters</title>

Thaumaturge suits characters who want to glean strange knowledge. It's especially useful for those who tend to fight with one-handed weapons, like swashbucklers or rogues, since they have their other hand free for an implement.

You've uncovered basic thaumaturgy. You become trained in thaumaturge class DC. Choose an [implement](Implements.aspx); you can use it to Glimpse Vulnerability, but don't gain its benefits. You also gain a few esoterica, allowing you to use [esoterica](/Traits.aspx?ID=899) actions. You become trained in your choice of [Arcana](/Skills.aspx?ID=35), [Nature](/Skills.aspx?ID=43), [Occultism](/Skills.aspx?ID=44), or [Religion](/Skills.aspx?ID=46); if you were already trained in these, you become trained in a skill of your choice. You gain the Glimpse Vulnerability action. 

 **Glimpse Vulnerability** <actions string="Single Action" /> ([esoterica](/Traits.aspx?ID=899), [manipulate](/Traits.aspx?ID=645)) **Frequency** once per round; **Requirement** You are holding your implement; **Effect** You glimpse a hidden vulnerability and then strengthen it to aid you. Select a creature you can see. Until you Glimpse Vulnerability again, that target gains weakness 2 against your [unarmed](/Traits.aspx?ID=719) and weapon Strikes.
