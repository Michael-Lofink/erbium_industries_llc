<title level="3">Multiclass Commander Characters</title>

<ul><li>[Bard](/Classes.aspx?ID=32) commanders are the ultimate leaders, enhancing and coordinating their allies to achieve peak effectiveness.</li><li>[Investigator](/Classes.aspx?ID=59) commanders are highly intelligent and highly educated, capable of evaluating their opponents and their battlefield with unrivaled efficiency while determining the best way to secure a victory.</li><li>[Wizard](/Classes.aspx?ID=39) commanders often studied at a school of battle magic that included practical combat training alongside their more specialized magical training.</li><li>The [exemplar](/Classes.aspx?ID=65) commander grants access to great options for characters who want to help their party work together and maneuver around the battlefield effectively. It’s particularly useful for characters looking to coordinate with their allies to set up powerful attacks.</li></ul>

You gain the tactics class feature like a commander and gain your own folio; this folio contains two common mobility or offensive [tactics](Tactics.aspx) of your choosing. You can prepare one of these tactics whenever a commander would be able to prepare  tactics. You gain a commander's banner that grants you a 30-foot [aura](/MonsterAbilities.aspx?ID=53) for the purposes of using your tactics, but the banner does not grant the commander's banner bonus to Will saves and DCs against fear effects. You become trained in commander class DC and Warfare [Lore](/Skills.aspx?ID=41); if you were already trained in Warfare Lore, you become trained in another Lore skill of your choice.
