You gain the services of a [young animal companion](/Rules.aspx?ID=151) that travels with you and obeys simple commands as best as it can. This [trained animal](Companions.aspx) is trained in [Performance](/Skills.aspx?ID=12) instead of the skill listed for its type. 

**Special** You can’t select another dedication feat until you’ve gained two other feats from the animal trainer archetype.

Your animal companion grows up, becoming a [mature animal companion](/Rules.aspx?ID=155). Increase its proficiency rank in [Performance](/Skills.aspx?ID=12) to expert instead of one of the three skill increases for mature. It gains the Mesmerizing Performance advanced maneuver, rather than the normal advanced maneuver for its type.
