<title level="3">Multiclass Alchemist Characters</title>

The free alchemical items from the alchemist archetype are extremely useful to characters of any class. The following are just some of the possible combinations. 
<ul><li>Barbarian alchemists can mix mutagens with their rage to ferocious effect. </li><li>Champion alchemists can focus on alchemical items that boost defenses and heal others, allowing their champion's reaction and _lay on hands_ to go further. </li><li>Fighter alchemists can use their alchemy to gain additional options in situations where their usual tactics don't work.</li><li>Ranger alchemists often focus on alchemy and snares, getting extra use out of their Crafting skill and supplying bombs for the bomb snare.</li><li>Rogue alchemists can combine a rogue's poison feats with free daily poisons, and bombs present an interesting way to sneak attack with various types of energy damage.</li><li>Spellcaster alchemists often use their alchemical items to take pressure off their repertoire or prepared spells. For instance, a wizard alchemist who can brew up darkvision elixirs to grant vision in darkness can prepare another spell instead of _darkvision_.</li></ul>

You put your alchemical interest into practice. You become trained in alchemical bombs, the alchemist class DC, and Crafting; if you were already trained in [Crafting](/Skills.aspx?ID=37), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice.

You gain the [Quick Alchemy](/Rules.aspx?ID=3282) benefits, creating up to 4 versatile vials during your daily preparations. Add the formulas for four additional common alchemical items to your formula book, in addition to those you gain from [Alchemical Crafting](/Feats.aspx?ID=5117).
