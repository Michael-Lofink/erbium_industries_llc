**Source** [Impossible Magic](/Sources.aspx?ID=355) pg. 96

There are places in the world where the essences of magic coalesce and build on each other, creating churning wells of power and streams of the energies. These forces can make up the fabric of the planes or produce entropic fields where magic is simply nullified, consumed before it can be brought into being. Your body is one such place for these powers. Whether by exposure to magical radiation, experimentation, happenstance of birth, or intense training, the essences of magic have warped themselves around you, creating in you a living nexus of power. You have trained yourself to sense with your very being the thrum and pulse of energy that permeates all things. As you absorb the magical energy around you, your soul filters it into pure potential. This power, however, cannot be held for long, as it urges to be released. For others, magic is a formula to be memorized or the will of a deity channeled through them. For you, magic is as easy as breathing.

On Golarion, the accidental transformation into a living nexus usually occurs in places where magic is wild and unpredictable, often suffusing the very air people breathe. Certain parts of the Mana Wastes fit this criteria as the ancient conflict between Geb and Nex scarred this once-beautiful land with stray bolts of energy and clouds of magical radiation. While not many settlements exist in the Mana Wastes outside of Alkenstar, adventurers undertaking explorations into the wastelands have been known to return with the uncanny ability to absorb any magic around them and control it as a living nexus.

---

Whether through happenstance or hours of rigorous training, you have discovered within yourself the ability to breathe in the magic around you.

You have an invisible, internal nexus of energy and have learned how to control your absorption of others’ magical energy and store it in this internal nexus. You gain the Absorb Magic reaction and the Overflowing Blade action. The DC for an ability gained via this archetype is either your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher, and is called your nexus DC. 

**Absorb Magic** <actions string="Reaction" /> ([concentrate](/Traits.aspx?ID=561), [magical](/Traits.aspx?ID=644)) **Requirements** You are not overflowing; **Trigger** Another creature within 30 feet [Casts a Spell](/Actions.aspx?ID=3521) or uses an action, activity, or reaction with the [arcane](/Traits.aspx?ID=534), [divine](/Traits.aspx?ID=579), [magical](/Traits.aspx?ID=644), [occult](/Traits.aspx?ID=662), or [primal](/Traits.aspx?ID=676) trait; **Effect** You breathe in a small fraction of the magic produced by the triggering effect, storing it in your internal nexus. You become overflowing. You remain overflowing for up to 1 minute before the stored magical energy fades. While overflowing, you gain a +1 status bonus to saving throws against effects with the arcane, divine, magical, occult, and primal traits. This bonus becomes +2 at 15th level.

**Overflowing Blade** <actions string="Single Action" /> ([concentrate](/Traits.aspx?ID=561), [force](/Traits.aspx?ID=610), [magical](/Traits.aspx?ID=644)) **Requirements** You are overflowing; **Effect** You conduct the magic in your internal nexus to coat your weapon or unarmed strike, empowering it with magical energy for a devastating attack. Make a melee [Strike](/Actions.aspx?ID=1167). If the Strike hits, you deal an additional 1d4 force damage. At 10th level, the additional damage increases to 2d4 force damage. At 18th level, the additional damage increases to 3d4 force damage. You are no longer overflowing.

Each working of magic has a unique scent and frequency of vibration. You have trained yourself to recognize the minute differences in magics, as if distinguishing between different odors. You gain [scent](/Rules.aspx?ID=3375) as an [imprecise sense](/Rules.aspx?ID=2407) with a range of 30 feet. This scent can only detect creatures, items, and effects that are currently under the effect of a spell or have the [arcane](/Traits.aspx?ID=534), [divine](/Traits.aspx?ID=579), [magical](/Traits.aspx?ID=644), [occult](/Traits.aspx?ID=662), or [primal](/Traits.aspx?ID=676) trait. In addition, you may use [Arcana](/Skills.aspx?ID=35), [Occultism](/Skills.aspx?ID=44), [Nature](/Skills.aspx?ID=43), or [Religion](/Skills.aspx?ID=46) instead of [Survival](/Skills.aspx?ID=49) to [Track](/Actions.aspx?ID=2408) creatures you could sense with this ability.

The altered ability loses the force trait and gains the trait of the new damage type.

Regardless of the result, you create a 30- foot [cone](/Rules.aspx?ID=2386) of magical energy dealing 3d6 force damage with a [basic](/Rules.aspx?ID=2297) Fortitude save against your nexus DC. You choose the cone’s direction, but its first square must be a square in the target of your strike’s space. A creature that critically fails the save is also knocked [prone](/Conditions.aspx?ID=88). The force damage increases by 1d6 at 8th level and every 2 levels thereafter. You are no longer overflowing.

Your internal nexus has grown to an immense size and can absorb workings of magic even as they form, sometimes even swallowing them entirely. You become overflowing as if you had Absorbed Magic. Additionally, you reduce the amount of damage the triggering spell or ability deals by 6d6 for all creatures targeted by the spell or ability. This reduction is applied before any [weaknesses](/Rules.aspx?ID=2317) or [resistances](/Rules.aspx?ID=2893) are taken into account. If you completely mitigate the damage to a creature, the triggering spell or ability has no effect on them.

The amount of damage reduced increases by 1d6 at 14th level and every 2 levels thereafter.
