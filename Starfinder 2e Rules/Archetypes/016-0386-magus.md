<title level="3">Multiclass Magus Characters</title>

The magus archetype grants you the ability to cast a limited amount spells, though you can learn more. It’s a good choice for martial characters who want to add a little magic to their repertoire. After some study, a magus multiclass character can learn the ability to combine their magic and their weapon strikes into a single devastating blow.

You cast spells like a [magus](/Classes.aspx?ID=17), gaining a spellbook with four common [arcane](/SpellLists.aspx?Tradition=1) cantrips of your choice. You gain the [Cast a Spell](/Actions.aspx?ID=2734) activity. You can prepare two cantrips each day from your spellbook. You’re trained in the spell attack modifier and spell DC statistics. Your key spellcasting attribute for magus archetype spells is Intelligence, and they are arcane magus spells. You become trained in [Arcana](/Skills.aspx?ID=35); if you were already trained in Arcana, you instead become trained in a skill of your choice.
