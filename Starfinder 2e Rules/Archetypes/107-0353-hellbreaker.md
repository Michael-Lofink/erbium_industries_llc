Founded in Isger to protest against the injustice and apathy of their faraway rulers in Cheliax, the Hellbreakers League is a young but growing rebel organization determined to make its mark. Members of the League swear themselves as agents of rebellion to topple the Chelaxian regime and free its vassal states from its oppressive rule, starting with Isger.

 The Hellbreakers League knows it's at a heavy disadvantage in its war against Cheliax as a still- developing group challenging one of the world's most powerful nations. Hellbreaker tactics rely on wit and daring as well as knowledge of the fiendish forces their enemies might use against them. Through sword, spell, and subterfuge, they work tirelessly to liberate the oppressed and remove tyrants from power.

<title level="2" noclass="true" icon="">Hellbreakers' Regimen</title>

Hellbreakers work around the clock, as they know their opposition won't be taking a moment off either. Training is vigorous and frequent, constantly testing Hellbreakers' abilities and almost always bringing them to the brink of exhaustion. That being said, discipline in a Hellbreaker station is certainly less painful than in their Chelaxian counterpart's.

By joining the League, you've taken on the responsibilities of a Hellbreaker. You gain the [Additional Lore](/Feats.aspx?ID=5114) general feat for both Devil Lore and Hellknight Lore. If you were already trained in one or both of those Lore skills, you also become trained in a Lore skill of your choice. When you roll initiative, you can attempt a Devil Lore, Hellknight Lore, or Society check to [Recall Knowledge](/Actions.aspx?ID=2367) about one enemy you can see as a free action, if that skill would be applicable. The GM is the final arbiter of whether or not one of those skills would be appropriate to Recall Knowledge about the enemy.

**Special** If you have the [Dirty Trick](/Feats.aspx?ID=6472) skill feat, you can attempt a [Thievery](/Skills.aspx?ID=50) check instead of a Deception check; if you do, this action loses the mental trait.

**Success** The target is off-guard against the triggering attack.<br />
**Critical Failure** You lose your balance in your attempt to assist your ally and fall [prone](/Conditions.aspx?ID=88).

If the attack is a critical hit, the target can’t Fly, Leap, levitate, or otherwise leave the ground until the end of your next turn.
