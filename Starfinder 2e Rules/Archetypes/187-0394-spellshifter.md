**Source** [Impossible Magic](/Sources.aspx?ID=355) pg. 102

You may or may not cast spells, but you’ve always possessed an uncanny knack for attuning yourself to the ebb and flow of the mystical currents that power them. Thanks to specialized training or natural aptitude, you’ve honed this gift into the ability to exert a modicum of control over magical spells cast nearby, allowing you to reshape and repurpose their energies to suit your own designs. You might use this talent for any number of purposes: to enhance the spells cast by you or your allies, to weaken and confound magic-wielding enemies, or even just to covertly humiliate rival spellcasters foolish enough to cross you.

As a spellshifter, you carry a special implement called a conduit, a seemingly mundane tool or object that you use to tap into the magical vibrations that suffuse the world around you. Only you can use this conduit, and every spellshifter’s conduit is unique. Yours might represent something distinctive about your personality, your relationship to magic, or the circumstances under which you first discovered your powers.

Spellshifters aren’t widely known in many parts of the world, and individuals with the innate potential to become one are sometimes regarded by distrustful spellcasters as jinxes whose mere presence might cause magic to malfunction or behave unpredictably. Past experience with these sorts of superstitions might drive you to actively conceal your powers from those who might fear them, or it might motivate you to seek out any opportunity to flaunt them openly, refusing to be cowed by the prejudice of others.

You can exert your will over magical spells cast in your vicinity, potentially altering or controlling their effects. You accomplish this through the use of a spellshifting conduit, a magical object of your choice that has light Bulk and can be held in one hand. If your conduit is lost or destroyed, you can train yourself to use a new one by spending 10 minutes attuning yourself to it. You gain the Shift Spell action and the Share the Burden spellshift.

**Shift Spell** <actions string="Single Action" /> ([concentrate](/Traits.aspx?ID=561), [manipulate](/Traits.aspx?ID=645)) **Requirements** You are within the affected area of an ongoing spell, or you can physically touch an object or creature under the effects of a spell, and you are holding your conduit; **Effect** Choose a spellshift and attempt an [Arcana](/Skills.aspx?ID=35) check against the DC of the spell (or the spell DC of the spellcaster if the spell does not normally have a DC). The caster immediately becomes aware that you’re trying to shift their spell and intuitively knows the potential effects of the shift, but not the result of your skill check. A willing caster can allow you to achieve one degree of success higher than the result of your check. The effects of the check are dependent on the chosen spellshift. Regardless of the result, the spell becomes immune to Shift Spell for 24 hours. 

**Spellshift—Shift Burden** You share the burden of a spell, allowing you to share control with the caster. If [Sustaining](/Actions.aspx?ID=2317) a spell with this spellshift causes a creature to attempt a saving throw, it is attempted against the higher of your class DC or spell DC. If the spell requires a spell attack roll, make the attack using your Arcana modifier instead of the spell’s normal spell attack modifier.

**Critical Success** If the spell is sustainable, you can [Sustain](/Actions.aspx?ID=2317) the spell as a free action as though you had cast the spell. For the remainder of the spell’s duration, you can Sustain the spell as though you were the caster.<br />
**Success** If the spell is sustainable, you can Sustain the spell as a free action as though you had cast the spell.

Your intuitive understanding of magic allows you to read the energy of a spell in the air. You gain the Analyze Magic spellshift.

**Spellshift—Analyze Magic** Once you’ve homed in on a spell or magical effect, you can study its unique magical signature to immediately understand exactly what it does.

**Critical Success** You learn information about the spell or effect as if you had critically succeeded at a skill check to [Identify Magic](/Actions.aspx?ID=2365) using the appropriate skill for its magical tradition.<br />
**Success** You learn information about the spell or effect as if you had succeeded at a skill check to Identify Magic using the appropriate skill for its magical tradition.

You can tamper with the elemental energy of a spell. You gain the Elemental Substitution spellshift.

**Spellshift—Elemental Substitution** Raw elemental energy is the fuel powering some of the most iconic spells of destruction ever devised, and you’ve discovered a method for realigning the magical pathways through which that energy flows into the material world.

**Critical Success** If the targeted spell inflicts [acid](/Traits.aspx?ID=524), [cold](/Traits.aspx?ID=555), [electricity](/Traits.aspx?ID=586), [fire](/Traits.aspx?ID=604), or [sonic](/Traits.aspx?ID=697) damage, you change its damage type to your choice of one of acid, cold, electricity, fire, or sonic damage. If the effect has a duration, it inflicts damage of its new type for 1 minute, then returns to normal.<br />
**Success** As critical success, except if the effect has a duration, it inflicts damage of its new type until the beginning of your next turn, then returns to normal.

Your innate power can shut down other magic. You gain the Dismantle Spell spellshift.

**Spellshift—Dismantle Spell** You attempt to [counteract](/Rules.aspx?ID=2250) a spell or at least suppress its effect for a time.

**Critical Success** The spell is counteracted if its rank is no more than 1 higher than half your level rounded up.<br />
**Success** The effects of the spell are suppressed until the start of your next turn. If the spell has a duration that is measured in rounds, its duration is extended by 1 round. If the spell does not have a duration, it targets the same creature or area as though it had just been cast at the start of your next turn.

You can reflexively shift a spell even as it’s being cast. You attempt to Shift the triggering Spell even if you do not meet the other requirements to Shift Spell. On a success, the spellshift applies to the spell’s effects as appropriate for the chosen spellshift. After you choose your spellshift, but before you attempt your [Arcana](/Skills.aspx?ID=35) check, the caster can voluntarily let their spell be [disrupted](/Rules.aspx?ID=2236). Regardless of the outcome, the caster then becomes immune to your Reactive Spellshift for 10 minutes.

You can alter the flow of magic around you, moving a spell effect from one target to another. You gain the Redirect Magic spellshift.

**Spellshift—Redirect Magic** Through sheer force of will, you can direct a spell’s energies away from its caster’s intended target.

**Critical Success** You change a single target of the spell to another viable target within 30 feet of you (even if it would be outside of the spell’s normal range). You can’t use this spellshift to affect a creature twice with the same spell. If the spell had an initial saving throw, the new target must attempt it, possibly achieving a different result than the original target of the spell. The DC for the spell becomes your [Arcana](/Skills.aspx?ID=35) DC instead of the original caster’s spell DC.<br />
**Success** As critical success, but if the target succeeds at any required saving throw, they are unaffected, and the spell does not change targets.
