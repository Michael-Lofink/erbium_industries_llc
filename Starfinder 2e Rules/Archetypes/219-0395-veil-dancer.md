**Source** [Impossible Magic](/Sources.aspx?ID=355) pg. 104

There are gaps in reality, minute interstices like the spaces between threads in a cloth. These hidden pockets of [the Universe](/Planes.aspx?ID=26), though not extraplanar, are imperceptible and inaccessible to most, who go about their daily lives without realizing they’re metaphorically stepping over thousands of tiny cracks per day. Time and space act oddly in these gaps, providing hiding places, instantaneous transportation, and escape routes for those who know where to look. 

You know where to look.

You’ve studied these small fissures, imbuing yourself with the power to make use of them. Though you may or may not be a formal spellcaster, you use this innate magic to conceal yourself or maneuver into advantageous positions. In a skirmish, you rely on the element of surprise, with your hit-and-run tactics keeping your foes uncertain where the next blow might come from. You’re skilled at sneaking about unseen, getting in and out of places before anyone even notices your infiltration. Thieves and assassins often learn the ways of the veil dancer, but anyone who wishes to rely on stealth and short-range teleportation in combat and exploration might make use of such techniques. 

The art of veil dancing was first developed in Nex. Some sources say it was developed by the archmage Nex himself, while others claim it was honed at [Nethysian](/Deities.aspx?ID=288) temples by sacred guardians of the faith. From there, the techniques rapidly spread throughout the Inner Sea and the rest of Golarion. Due to the tradition’s association with thievery and assassinations, some are wary of those who openly profess to be veil dancers, though most veil dancers would claim that the practice is merely a tool, one that can be put to either benevolent or malevolent ends. 

Veil dancers are more common in places where a large proportion of the population has magical training or exposure to magical energies, such as Nex, Jalmeray, New Thassilon, and the city-state of Nantambu in the Mwangi Expanse. You might have learned the magic formally or developed your abilities on your own. In either case, you pride yourself on your ability to vanish and reappear where people least expect you. For you, distances and heights are no barrier, merely a puzzle to be solved.

You’re able to shift yourself into the spaces between to avoid notice. You gain the Part the Veil action.

**Part the Veil** <actions string="Two Actions" /> ([concentrate](/Traits.aspx?ID=561), [magical](/Traits.aspx?ID=644)) **Requirements** You are not observed by any enemies; **Effect** You phase yourself into the interstices, veiling yourself from physical reality. You become [concealed](/Conditions.aspx?ID=62). You can use this concealment to [Hide](/Actions.aspx?ID=2404) or [Sneak](/Actions.aspx?ID=2405) as your disassociation from reality makes you difficult to focus on. If you use a [hostile action](/Rules.aspx?ID=2251), this effect ends after the action is complete; otherwise, it lasts for 1 minute.

In the blink of an eye, you transport yourself and any items you’re wearing and holding from your current space to an unoccupied space within 60 feet that you can see. If this would bring another creature with you—even if you’re carrying it in an extradimensional container—you cannot Dart Between. When you arrive at your destination, you can Part the Veil as a free action, even if you wouldn’t normally meet its requirements. If you are [hidden](/Conditions.aspx?ID=79), this action does not cause you to become [observed](/Conditions.aspx?ID=84) provided you still have [cover, greater cover,](/Rules.aspx?ID=2372) or [concealment](/Rules.aspx?ID=424) from the creatures you were hidden from after the teleportation.

You leave behind an afterimage of yourself in your previous square. This afterimage looks like you and can perform simple behaviors to make the deception more plausible but otherwise cannot move or take actions. A creature can distinguish between you and your afterimage easily. Your afterimage automatically fails all checks and DCs and is destroyed if it would take any damage; otherwise, it lasts for 1 minute. Whenever you take the [Step](/Actions.aspx?ID=2304) action, you can instead teleport to an afterimage instead. If you do, the action gains the [teleportation](/Traits.aspx?ID=710) trait, and the afterimage you teleport to is destroyed.

You teleport to a square within 60 feet and make a [Strike](/Actions.aspx?ID=1167) against a creature; if you hit, it must attempt a Fortitude save against your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher. **Failure** The target takes 10d6 force damage, and the target is affected by a 2nd-rank [_silence_](/Spells.aspx?ID=1674) spell for 1 round.<br />
**Critical Failure** The target takes 15d6 damage, and the target is affected by a 2nd-rank _silence_ spell for 1 minute.
