**Source** [Player Core 2](/Sources.aspx?ID=227) pg. 208

You've studied countless techniques for providing medical aid, making you a peerless doctor and healer. While many healers happily practice the art of medicine in the safety and serenity of stationary buildings, few are in more dire need of medicinal aid than adventurers. Such an occupation comes with frequent injuries that need constant attention. Few parties would ever turn down help from a practicing medic. In fact, the need for medicine is so great that many medics started as adventurers and took up medicine as a necessity. While patching up injuries is the most common practice of medics, they are also invaluable for their ability to help fight diseases and ailments inflicted by all kinds of creatures and traps. The adaptability and utility of the medic are undeniable. Whether you studied medicine for most of your life or picked it up on the road, you are who people turn to for aid.

You become an expert in [Medicine](/Skills.aspx?ID=42). When you succeed with [Battle Medicine](/Feats.aspx?ID=5125) or [Treat Wounds](/Actions.aspx?ID=2399), the target gains a circumstance bonus to the number of Hit Points they regain equal to 5 HP at DC 20, 10 HP at DC 30, or 15 HP at DC 40. <br /> Once per day, you can use Battle Medicine on a creature that’s temporarily immune due to having already been treated with Battle Medicine. If you’re a master in Medicine, you can do so once per hour.

You treat an adjacent creature in an attempt to reduce the [clumsy](/Conditions.aspx?ID=61), [enfeebled](/Conditions.aspx?ID=71), or [sickened](/Conditions.aspx?ID=91) condition. If a creature has multiple conditions from this list, choose one. Attempt a [counteract](/Rules.aspx?ID=3280) check against the condition using your [Medicine](/Skills.aspx?ID=42) modifier as your counteract modifier and the condition’s source to determine the DC. You can’t treat a condition that came from an [artifact](/Traits.aspx?ID=537) or effect above 20th level unless you have [Legendary Medic](/Feats.aspx?ID=5169); even if you do, the counteract DC increases by 10. Treating a Condition that is continually applied under certain circumstances (for instance, the enfeebled condition a [holy](/Traits.aspx?ID=517) character gains from carrying a weapon with the [_unholy_](/Equipment.aspx?ID=2851) rune) has no effect as long as the circumstances continue.

**Critical Success** Reduce the condition value by 2.<br />
**Success** Reduce the condition value by 1.<br />
**Critical Failure** Increase the condition value by 1.

You resuscitate the dead. Attempt a DC 40 [Medicine](/Skills.aspx?ID=42) check to revive a dead creature who has been dead for no more than 3 rounds and whose body is mostly intact. If you succeed, the target returns to life with the effects of [_raise dead_](/Spells.aspx?ID=1645), except it still has the [wounded](/Conditions.aspx?ID=99) condition it had before dying, increased by 1 (or wounded 1 if it wasn’t wounded before dying). Whether you succeed or fail, the target is temporarily immune to Resuscitate for 1 day.
