<title level="3">Multiclass Necromancer Characters</title>

The necromancer archetype allows you to conjure thralls to fight for you on the battlefield. It allows you to support multiple fronts of a battle and is most useful to those who like to fight in or near the front lines. Additionally, further necromantic power allows you to control large areas at a time and take advantage of your enemy’s weakest angle.

You can cast spells like a necromancer, gaining a dirge with four common occult cantrips of your choice. You gain the [Cast a Spell](/Actions.aspx?ID=4180) activity. You can prepare two cantrips each day from your dirge. You're trained in the spell attack modifier and spell DC statistics. Your key spellcasting attribute for necromancer archetype spells is Intelligence, and they are occult necromancer spells. You become trained in [Occultism](/Skills.aspx?ID=44); if you were already trained in Occultism, you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. 

Once per turn you can use a single action to create a single thrall within 30 feet. This action has the [concentrate](/Traits.aspx?ID=561), [manipulate](/Traits.aspx?ID=645), [necromancer](/Traits.aspx?ID=950), [occult](/Traits.aspx?ID=662), and [thrall](/Traits.aspx?ID=955) traits. You can use the Command a Thrall action as described in the thrall trait.
