<title level="3">Multiclass Swashbuckler Characters</title>

The swashbuckler archetype is a great fit for martial characters who want to be a bit flashier or for Charismabased spellcasters who want to add a bit of dramatic flair to their routine. Multiclass swashbucklers work especially well for characters who already want to incorporate skill-based action and seek out daring deeds to do in the heat of battle. 
<ul><li>Barbarian swashbucklers love to show off their strength and brutality with devastating finishers.</li><li>Champion swashbucklers can show off the powers of their deity to inspire awe in potential converts and fear in enemies of the faith.</li><li>Investigator swashbucklers might love to solve big cases with an audience to show off their prowess.</li><li>Monk swashbucklers can take full advantage of their perfectly trained bodies to perform impressive feats.</li><li>Rogue swashbucklers might hide in the shadows before emerging to put on a flashy show.</li><li>Spellcaster swashbucklers might want to show off their mastery of both martial and magical arts.</li></ul>

Choose a swashbuckler's style. You gain the [panache and stylish combatant](/Classes.aspx?ID=63) class features, and you apply the bravado trait to [Tumble Through](/Actions.aspx?ID=2370) and any actions indicated in your swashbuckler style, allowing you to gain panache. You become trained in [Acrobatics](/Skills.aspx?ID=34) or the skill associated with your style. If you were already trained in both skills, you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. You also become trained in swashbuckler class DC. You don't gain any other effects of your chosen style.
