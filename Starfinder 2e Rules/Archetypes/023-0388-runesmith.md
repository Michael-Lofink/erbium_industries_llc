You become trained in runesmith [class DC](/Rules.aspx?ID=2051) and [Crafting](/Skills.aspx?ID=37). You gain a [runic repertoire](RunesmithRunes.aspx) with two 1st-level runes of your choice. You choose these from the common runes on the rune list as well as uncommon or rarer runes you have access to. 

You can etch runes. An etched rune is carved, inked, or branded; this application does not damage the creature or item. Etching a rune requires an artisan's toolkit. Your magic can sustain up to one etched rune at a time. If you're at least 9th level, increase the maximum number of etched runes you can sustain at a time to 2, and if you're at least 17th level, increase it to 3. You can etch any number of runes up to your maximum during your daily preparations or by spending 10 minutes in exploration mode. Your etched runes remain indefinitely until they're expended or removed or until you etch more runes than your maximum, which causes your oldest rune to fade. Additionally, you gain the Solitary Invocation action. 

**Solitary Invocation** <actions string="Single Action" /> ([invocation](/Traits.aspx?ID=956), [magical](/Traits.aspx?ID=644), [runesmith](/Traits.aspx?ID=952)) You utter the name of one of your runes within 30 feet, which blazes with power, applying the effect in its Invocation entry. An invoked rune then fades away, its task complete.

You can now create runes in the midst of combat. You gain the Trace Rune action.

**Trace Rune** <actions string="Single Action" /> or <actions string="Two Actions" /> ([concentrate](/Traits.aspx?ID=561), [magical](/Traits.aspx?ID=644), [manipulate](/Traits.aspx?ID=645), [runesmith](/Traits.aspx?ID=952)) **Requirements** You're holding or wearing an [artisan's toolkit](/Equipment.aspx?ID=2703); **Effect** You apply one rune to an adjacent target matching the rune's usage entry. You can have any number of runes traced at a time, but each rune remains only until the end of your next turn. If you spend 2 actions to Trace a Rune, you draw the rune in the air, causing it to appear on a target within 30 feet.
