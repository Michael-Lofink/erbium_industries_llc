_[**PFS Note**](/pathfinder-society) All Pathfinder Society agents have access to the Inventor archetype._

You revel in the creation of clever inventions, deploying your brilliance to defeat your foes. Explosives, gadgets, and innovative technologies? You have ideas for all of them, and you're eager to test your theories.

<title level="3">Multiclass Inventor Characters</title>

Inventor is a great fit for intelligent characters looking to branch out and learn new tricks, especially [alchemists](/Classes.aspx?ID=1), who are already good at [Crafting](/Skills.aspx?ID=4) and may appreciate unstable options as an alternative to daily alchemical resources. If they meet the Intelligence requirements, [clerics](/Classes.aspx?ID=5) of deities who encourage invention and experimentation like [Brigh](/Deities.aspx?ID=33) are an amazing fit, both because it makes narrative sense and because such characters can usually [Refocus](/Actions.aspx?ID=71) while fixing their innovation after using an unstable ability.

You become trained in [Crafting](/Skills.aspx?ID=4) and inventor class DC. You gain the [Inventor](/Feats.aspx?ID=798) skill feat, even if you don't meet the skill feat's requirements. Choose an [innovation](Innovations.aspx). You gain that innovation, though you don't gain any other abilities that modify or use that innovation, such as modifications or [Explode](/Actions.aspx?ID=902).
