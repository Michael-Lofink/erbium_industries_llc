You’ve formed a bond with an eidolon, an entity that manifests in a physical body only through their link to your life force. You gain an [eidolon](Eidolons.aspx) as well as the [Manifest Eidolon](/Actions.aspx?ID=757) activity. Due to your tenuous link, you can’t gain or use [tandem](/Traits.aspx?ID=395) actions. Because you don’t have [Act Together](/Actions.aspx?ID=758), only you or your eidolon can perform an [exploration](/Rules.aspx?ID=2006) activity at one time. 

Your eidolon is trained in unarmed attacks and unarmored defense, and shares your proficiency rank for [Perception](/Rules.aspx?ID=2040), saving throws, and skill checks. Choose an eidolon type. You become trained in your eidolon’s listed skills. For each of those skills that you’re already trained in, you become trained in a different skill of your choice. Your eidolon’s initial attributes are reduced. Instead of starting with a +4 attribute modifier in Strength or Dexterity and +2 in the other, your eidolon starts with a +3 in Strength or Dexterity and +2 in the other attribute. They otherwise gain the statistics listed for an eidolon of that type. 

At 5th level, the eidolon’s attribute modifier that was reduced to +3 increases to +4, before applying attribute boosts. At levels 5, 10, 15, and 20, your eidolon also gets four attribute boosts, which follow the same rules as yours.

You gain the [basic spellcasting benefits](/Rules.aspx?ID=2132). You gain the [Cast a Spell](/Actions.aspx?ID=3521) activity. Your key spellcasting attribute for summoner archetype spells is Charisma, and they are summoner spells of your eidolon’s tradition. You become trained in the spell attack modifier and spell DC statistics. You gain a spell repertoire and two cantrips. Each time you gain a spell slot of a new rank from the summoner archetype, add a spell of the appropriate spell rank to your repertoire.
