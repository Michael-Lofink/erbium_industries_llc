Quantum caves contain formations that shift when observation ends. Distinctive mineral seams, fractures, and embedded fossils identify the same formation across its different configurations.

Each formation follows the [[Quantum]] trait. Its **Extent** identifies what shifts together. Features grouped under this family can be placed separately or combined into a larger cave.

## Quantum Arch

```sf2e-stats
# Quantum Arch
## Environmental Feature

==Quantum==

---

**Description** A broad stone arch with a forked white mineral seam across its crown. Its flattened upper surface is wide enough to walk across.
**Extent** The arch and its two footings.

---

**Fallen** The arch lies on its side, providing a low walkway onto its crown.
**Upright** The arch stands beside an elevated passage. Its crown rests level with the entrance.
**Spanning** The arch bridges a fissure between two ledges.

---

**Transition** Fallen → Upright → Spanning → Fallen.
**Passengers** Creatures standing on the arch travel with it, arriving on its upper surface.
**Placement** Mark the arch's footprint in each configuration. Place the elevated passage and fissure where the arch can provide access.
**Rules** [[Quantum]]

---
```

## Quantum Cliff

```sf2e-stats
# Quantum Cliff
## Environmental Feature

==Quantum==

---

**Description** An orange mineral band crosses a cliff between the cavern floor and an upper gallery.
**Extent** The cliff face and its projecting ledges.

---

**Sheer** Narrow cracks provide handholds. Reaching the upper gallery requires Climbing.
**Stepped** Broad shelves form a walkable staircase to the upper gallery.
**Split** A cleft opens through the cliff's base, exposing a passage to the chamber beyond.

---

**Transition** Sheer → Stepped → Split → Sheer.
**Passengers** Creatures climbing or standing on the cliff travel with its surface. Occupied ledges retain enough space to support their passengers.
**Placement** Choose the cliff's height and Climb DC. The cavern floor, upper gallery, and passage beyond remain fixed.
**Rules** [[Quantum]]

---

Source: Erbium Industries LLC
```

## Quantum Tunnel

```sf2e-stats
# Quantum Tunnel
## Environmental Feature

==Quantum==

---

**Description** A curved tunnel with pale mineral veins across its floor. The bend conceals its far opening.
**Extent** The tunnel's interior and both mouths.

---

**First Connection** Entrance cavern ↔ Lower gallery.
**Second Connection** Lower gallery ↔ Upper gallery.
**Third Connection** Upper gallery ↔ Deep chamber.

---

**Transition** First → Second → Third → First.
**Passengers** Creatures entirely inside travel with the tunnel as its mouths reconnect.
**Placement** Assign each mouth a fixed landing at every connected chamber. Use the same recognizable interior for all three connections.
```

## Quantum Stepping Stone

```sf2e-stats
# Quantum Stepping Stone
## Environmental Feature

==Quantum==

---

**Description** A flat slab protrudes from the cavern floor. A dark spiral fossil marks its upper surface.
**Extent** One slab. Each additional stepping stone is a separate quantum feature.

---

**Near Bank** The slab rests beside the edge of a fissure, within stepping distance of solid ground.
**Midpoint** The slab occupies the center of the fissure.
**Far Bank** The slab rests beside the opposite edge.

---

**Transition** Near Bank → Midpoint → Far Bank → Near Bank.
**Passengers** Creatures and loose objects entirely supported by the slab travel with it.
**Placement** Choose the slab's size and mark its three positions. Space additional slabs so their positions can form a crossing.
```

## Quantum Pillar

```sf2e-stats
# Quantum Pillar
## Environmental Feature

==Quantum==

---

**Description** A thick stone pillar bears an exposed vein of violet crystal. Matching circular scars mark its possible positions.
**Extent** One pillar.

---

**First Position** The pillar occupies its first marked footprint.
**Second Position** The pillar occupies its second marked footprint.
**Third Position** The pillar occupies its third marked footprint.

---

**Transition** First → Second → Third → First.
**Cover** The pillar provides standard cover when it partially obstructs a line of attack. Its solid stone blocks sight through its full width.
**Placement** Use a footprint at least 5 feet across. Position its three sites to change cover along firing lanes or obstruct different approaches.
```

## Quantum Chamber

```sf2e-stats
# Quantum Chamber
## Environmental Feature

==Quantum==

---

**Description** A small round chamber has a single doorway and a continuous silver seam running around its walls.
**Extent** The chamber's floor, walls, ceiling, and doorway.

---

**Lower Landing** The doorway opens onto the cavern floor.
**Upper Landing** The doorway opens onto a high gallery overlooking the cavern.
**Deep Landing** The doorway opens into an enclosed chamber farther underground.

---

**Transition** Lower Landing → Upper Landing → Deep Landing → Lower Landing.
**Passengers** Creatures and loose objects entirely inside travel with the chamber.
**Placement** Mark three locations large enough to contain the chamber. Establish the lighting at each landing; occupants need a way to release observation while inside.
```

## Using the Family in an Encounter

Start with two or three formations. A quantum pillar can provide cover across an exposed cavern floor, while a cliff offers access to an elevated firing position. A tunnel can connect that gallery to a chamber containing the encounter's objective.

Give each formation a recognizable marking and describe that marking whenever it reappears. Once the party discovers its configurations, record the cycle where the players can reference it.

Creatures familiar with the cave can watch a formation to hold a useful position, carry an image to preserve a route, or deliberately release observation to travel. Establish their method visibly so the party can understand and interfere with it.

For an anomaly site, place the anomaly beyond a quantum chamber's Deep Landing.
