# Nostalgic

> [!summary]
> **Nostalgics** preserve the equipment, techniques, customs, and practical knowledge of earlier eras by continuing to use them.
>
> Some restore weapons whose designs predate the [[Star Dream]]. Others reproduce traditional clothing, practice historical crafts, study obsolete machinery, cook from handwritten recipes, navigate from physical charts, or maintain customs that have survived only in archives and museums.
>
> A nostalgic can appreciate modern technology perfectly well. Their expertise lies in understanding older things deeply enough to keep them useful.

The infosphere makes historical information easy to retrieve. Practical knowledge is harder to preserve. A technical diagram can explain how a black-powder firearm was assembled without teaching someone how it feels when the mechanism is beginning to foul. A museum archive can preserve a dead language without preserving the gestures, jokes, etiquette, and habits that once accompanied it.

Nostalgics learn those missing details.

The archetype is most commonly associated with equipment carrying the **archaic** trait, though many of its abilities also cover old languages, customs, ruins, historical technologies, and other remnants of earlier eras.

## Nostalgic Modifications

A **nostalgic modification** is an archaic item restored and adapted using modern materials and precision while preserving its original construction and operating principles.

A nostalgic-modified item retains the **archaic** trait. It doesn't automatically gain the analog or tech trait.

Visible modifications vary by item. An old firearm might use precisely manufactured replacement springs, improved metallurgy, modern propellant produced to an obsolete specification, or concealed reinforcement in its frame. A sword might use a historically accurate blade geometry produced with modern material tolerances. A bow might use improved laminates hidden beneath traditional finishing.

### Weapon Grades

A nostalgic-modified archaic weapon can use the normal Starfinder weapon improvement system.

| Grade | Level | Upgrade Price | Total Grade Value | Upgrade Slots | Damage Dice | Tracking |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Commercial | 0 | — | — | 0 | 1 | — |
| Tactical | 2 | 350 credits | 350 credits | 0 | 1 | +1 |
| Advanced | 4 | 650 credits | 1,000 credits | 1 | 2 | +1 |
| Superior | 10 | 9,000 credits | 10,000 credits | 1 | 2 | +2 |
| Elite | 12 | 10,000 credits | 20,000 credits | 2 | 3 | +2 |
| Ultimate | 16 | 80,000 credits | 100,000 credits | 2 | 3 | +3 |
| Paragon | 19 | 300,000 credits | 400,000 credits | 3 | 4 | +3 |

The price of the original archaic weapon is separate from the cost of its grade improvements.

A nostalgic-modified weapon can accept weapon upgrades even though it lacks the analog or tech trait. A tech upgrade remains a tech item after installation without giving the weapon itself the tech trait.

### Runes and Grades

Fundamental and property weapon runes become dormant while a weapon uses nostalgic modifications. Their effects return if the nostalgic modifications are removed.

The [[Runic Preservation]] feat allows property runes to function alongside the Starfinder improvement system.

### Optional Archaic Equipment Rules

If the campaign uses the optional archaic equipment rules, a nostalgic-modified weapon you wield uses its normal damage die against non-archaic armor.

Its Broken Threshold is also calculated normally.

These benefits do nothing in a campaign where the optional archaic equipment rules aren't being used.

## Archaic Weapons and Class Abilities

For you, an archaic firearm counts as a **gun** when determining whether you can use an ability that requires a gun. The weapon retains the archaic trait and doesn't gain the analog or tech trait.

You can use [[Aim]] with any archaic ranged weapon. An archaic ranged weapon also counts as a gun for an ability that includes Aim as part of its effect.

Other abilities requiring a gun use an archaic firearm unless another Nostalgic feat says otherwise.

# Nostalgic Feats

```sf2e-stats
# Nostalgic Dedication
## Feat 2
==Uncommon== ==Archetype== ==Dedication==

**Prerequisites** trained in Crafting or Society

You have spent enough time studying earlier eras to reproduce their equipment and practices with more than academic accuracy.

You gain [[Additional Lore]] for History Lore. If you already have Additional Lore for History Lore, choose another Lore skill associated with a historical period, culture, technology, or tradition.

You gain access to common equipment with the archaic trait.

You can apply **nostalgic modifications** to archaic weapons. You can improve these weapons using the normal Starfinder weapon-grade progression. You must meet the normal level and cost requirements for improving equipment.

While you wield a nostalgic-modified archaic weapon, its Broken Threshold is calculated normally, and it doesn't reduce its damage die against non-archaic armor if the campaign uses the optional archaic equipment rules.

An archaic firearm counts as a gun for your abilities. You can use Aim with any archaic ranged weapon, and an archaic ranged weapon counts as a gun for abilities that include Aim as part of their effect.

**Special** You can't select another dedication feat until you've gained two other feats from the Nostalgic archetype.
```

# Using the Archetype

A Nostalgic's interests can come from almost any part of history.

A character might restore pre-PES machinery, study a culture destroyed during the early intersystem era, practice an ancestral martial tradition, collect obsolete consumer electronics, reproduce historical music, maintain ancient religious observances, cook extinct regional cuisines, or carry weapons that most people encounter only in museums.

An item's age doesn't determine whether it has the archaic trait. A newly manufactured weapon built according to an archaic design can still be archaic. Black-powder firearms, bows, traditional blades, and similar equipment continue to be produced by specialists and enthusiasts.

Likewise, an old item isn't automatically archaic if it was built using technology comparable to modern Settled Systems equipment.

## Nostalgic Characters in the Settled Systems

The Settled Systems contain enormous archives of preserved information, though access to the original objects and traditions represented in those archives varies considerably. Museums, universities, private collectors, ancestry communities, religious organizations, isolated settlements, historical societies, and commercial manufacturers all preserve different pieces of the past.

Erbium Industries also maintains substantial historical collections. Corporate archives tend to value provenance, ownership documentation, technical relevance, and intellectual-property status alongside historical importance.

Some Nostalgics make a living restoring museum pieces or authenticating collections. Others reproduce archaic equipment commercially or practice traditions maintained within their families and communities.

There is no requirement that a Nostalgic consider the past better than the present. Their defining skill is knowing how to make the past work.
