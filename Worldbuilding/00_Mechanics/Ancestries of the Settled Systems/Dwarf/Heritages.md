# ==Citadel Dwarf==
You're part of a renowned mining clan that tunnels into planets, moons, and asteroids for rare minerals. You become trained in [Survival](https://2e.aonsrd.com/skills/18-survival). If you would automatically become trained in Survival (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. When you critically succeed at a Survival check to [Earn Income](https://2e.aonsrd.com/actions/47-earn-income) by mining, you uncover a vein of highly valuable minerals. The next time you use Survival to Earn Income in the same location, you treat the task level as 1 higher. If you're a master in Survival, you treat the task level as 2 higher, and if you're legendary in survival, you treat the task level as 4 higher. The DC for the task remains the same.

# ==Deep Space Hyrngar==
You have an attribute bonus to Charisma and a flaw to Wisdom rather than the other way around (unless you choose two free attribute boosts instead of the usual boosts and flaw). You become trained in [Deception](https://2e.aonsrd.com/skills/6-deception). If you would automatically become trained in Deception (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. If a creature owes you money or has caused you significant financial loss, you have a +1 circumstance bonus to Deception checks to [Lie](https://2e.aonsrd.com/actions/79-lie) to them, to Perception DCs against attempts to Lie to you, and to [Intimidation](https://2e.aonsrd.com/skills/8-intimidation) checks to [Coerce](https://2e.aonsrd.com/actions/84-coerce) or [Demoralize](https://2e.aonsrd.com/actions/85-demoralize) them.

# ==Industrial Dwarf==
Whether working vast ale vats or overseeing robotics factories, your clan has long honed its trade through technology. You gain the [Additional Lore](https://2e.aonsrd.com/feats/755-additional-lore) feat for your clan's primary industry (such as [Warfare Lore](https://2e.aonsrd.com/skills/9-lore)). When you [Craft](https://2e.aonsrd.com/actions/76-craft) a common item of your level or lower that's associated with this industry, you automatically know the formula and can use the Lore skill in place of [Crafting](https://2e.aonsrd.com/skills/5-crafting). Your proficiency in this Lore makes you eligible for Crafting skill feat prerequisites, but only while you're interacting with items related to your clan's industry.

# ==Questbound Dwarf==
Your clan has dedicated itself to galactic exploration aboard a dwarven generation ship.  Decades in space have left you more familiar with navigating stars than tunnels. You become trained in [Piloting](https://2e.aonsrd.com/skills/14-piloting). If you would automatically become trained in Piloting (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. If you succeed at a Piloting check to [Navigate](https://2e.aonsrd.com/actions/162-navigate) or [Plot Course](https://2e.aonsrd.com/actions/94-plot-course), you get a critical success instead. You also gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Piloting checks made to [Recall Knowledge](https://2e.aonsrd.com/actions/50-recall-knowledge) about starships.
