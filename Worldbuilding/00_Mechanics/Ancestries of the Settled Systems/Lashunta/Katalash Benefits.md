# Basic Katalash Benefits

```sf2e-stats
| ==Katalash==
```

Every heritage with the Katalash trait grants the following benefits.

#### Hit Points
You gain 8 Hit Points from your ancestry instead of 6.

#### Attribute Boosts
Strength  
Constitution  
Free

#### Attribute Flaw
Charisma

These replace the attribute boosts normally granted by your lashunta ancestry.

#### Katalash
You gain the Katalash trait. You count as a Katalash lashunta for prerequisites, equipment, abilities, and effects that distinguish between lashunta expressions.
You retain your Limited Telepathy and Emotional Context ancestry abilities.

#### Psionic-Pathos Predator
You can detect the emotions of others, though you lack the ability to sort out the complexities of these emotions. You gain emotion sense as an imprecise sense with a range of 30 feet. This means you can detect and determine the location of creatures that possess emotions. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to Perception checks when using the Seek action to find hidden or undetected creatures within the range of your emotion sense who's under an emotion effect, such as rage, the frightened condition, or any spell with the emotion trait. 

#### Alien Bearing

Your layered eyes, mandibles, predatory posture, abrupt movements, and emotionally intense telepathy are difficult for most Settled Systems peoples to interpret without fear or discomfort.

You take a –2 circumstance penalty to Diplomacy checks to [Make an Impression](https://2e.aonsrd.com/actions/29-make-an-impression) or [Request](https://2e.aonsrd.com/actions/30-request) something from a creature that is not a Katalash lashunta.

This penalty doesn’t apply if the creature is already Helpful toward you. The GM can also waive this penalty for a creature whose background, community, or personal experience has made them meaningfully familiar with Katalash bodies and communication.

Alien Bearing doesn’t affect Gather Information, Deception, Intimidation, or any other use of Diplomacy.

# Running Katalash Social Friction

Alien Bearing represents the immediate difficulty Katalash experience when attempting to gain trust or voluntary cooperation from people unfamiliar with their bodies. Wider institutional prejudice should usually appear through attitudes, procedures, and material barriers rather than additional universal numerical penalties.

Within Erbium facilities, government offices, major ports, corporate housing, medical institutions, and other human-standard environments, an unknown Katalash may begin one attitude step worse than an equally situated Damaya, Korasha, or human visitor. This usually means beginning Unfriendly rather than Indifferent. It should rarely make a creature automatically Hostile.

Common forms of discrimination include additional security screening, refusal to accept telepathic communication, demands that the Katalash restrain their antennae or mandibles, denial of equipment on compatibility grounds, insistence upon a Damaya or Korasha intermediary, and ordinary anger being documented as threatening conduct.

These reactions shouldn’t apply universally. Individual relationships, recognized rank, sponsorship, reputation, and demonstrated familiarity can overcome personal prejudice. Katalash-majority communities and institutions designed around Katalash bodies impose none of these disadvantages.

A Katalash character should feel the effects of refusing assimilation, but their social treatment should be capable of changing through play.
