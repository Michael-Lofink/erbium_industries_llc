# ==Gold-Blooded Madrosarai==
Aurum blood fills your veins, giving your skin a metallic sheen that seems to glow under light. Your bloodline is often associated with leadership and the ability to coordinate others. You become trained in [Intimidation](https://2e.aonsrd.com/skills/8-intimidation). If you would automatically become trained in Intimidation (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in another skill of your choice. You gain the [Group Coercion](https://2e.aonsrd.com/feats/811-group-coercion) skill feat. If you roll a critical failure on an Intimidation check to [Coerce](https://2e.aonsrd.com/actions/84-coerce) a [humanoid](https://2e.aonsrd.com/traits/101-humanoid), you get a failure instead.

# ==Lime-Blooded Madrosarai==
Madrosarais with lime blood have often produced impressive innovations and enacted great changes throughout the Virtuum and beyond. The blood in these veins is seen as a path into many professions, but especially those where one works with objects. You become trained in [Crafting](https://2e.aonsrd.com/skills/5-crafting). If you would automatically become trained in Crafting (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in another skill of your choice. You gain the [Quick Repair](https://2e.aonsrd.com/feats/865-quick-repair) skill feat.

# ==Red-Blooded Madrosarai==
Often considered the greatest warriors within madrosarai culture, red-blooded madrosarais have a strong association with unleashing their emotions in acceptably controlled bursts. The crimson-red blood grants you a connection with great madrosarai warriors of the past. You struggle to control your temper but can unleash it in moments of pure aggression at the pitch of battle. You gain the Controlled Rage action.

# ==Stone-Blooded Madrosarai==
One of the muted madrosarai bloodlines, stone-bloods are often employed as medics or philosophers. You always take time to analyze a situation rather than reacting rashly. You can cast the _[analyze target](https://2e.aonsrd.com/spells/9-analyze-target)_ cantrip as an arcane innate spell at will. A cantrip is heightened to a spell rank equal to half your level rounded up. Increase the status bonus granted by _analyze target_ by 1.

# ==Teal-Blooded Madrosarai==
Of the bloodlines, teal is the most commonly found among madrosarais. This bloodline is known to represent the most dedicated of workers and those seeking to serve their species and the wider needs of the Virtuum. With this blood in your veins, you gain access to a greater resilience that you can employ while performing your duties. You gain the Madrosarai Tenacity reaction.![[Madrosarai Tenacity]]

# ==Umber-Blooded Madrosarai==
A relatively new bloodline that appeared following the Gap, those of the umber bloodline possess powerful psychic abilities that seem linked with events that not even they can remember. You gain one cantrip from the [occult](https://2e.aonsrd.com/spells/occult) spell list. You can cast this cantrip as an innate occult spell at will. A cantrip is heightened to a spell rank equal to half your level rounded up. Replace all instances of [arcane](https://2e.aonsrd.com/traits/22-arcane) or [Arcana](https://2e.aonsrd.com/skills/2-arcana) in all madrosarai ancestry feats you gain with [occult](https://2e.aonsrd.com/traits/132-occult) and [Occultism](https://2e.aonsrd.com/skills/12-occultism) respectively, including the tradition of all innate spells you gain, the traits of the feats, and the feat's prerequisites.
