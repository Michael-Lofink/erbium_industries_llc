# Pressure-system maintenance records

**Citation:** `A1%8&14-7` · **Status:** established

**Formal title:** Pressure-Envelope Inspection Record Standard

## What it does

Requires compatible stamped maintenance histories for certified pressure-envelope and structural safety systems.

## Application

Introduced after the Lapse 7 disaster on Theosis. Shared records let inspectors compare parts and repairs across manufacturers. Work recorded outside recognized stamp systems may be rejected as unverified.
