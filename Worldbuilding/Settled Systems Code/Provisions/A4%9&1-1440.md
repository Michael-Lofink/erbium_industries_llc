# Wages during civic service

**Citation:** `A4%9&1-1440` · **Status:** established

**Formal title:** Wage Ineligibility During Designated Civic Service

## What it does

Personnel assigned to designated civic service are ineligible for ordinary wages during that service. The rule applies to the Great Volunteering.

## Application

The original payroll clause covered 1,440 standard minutes. Later amendments expanded its application without changing the number. Equipment leases remain separate obligations; the campaign’s employment offer continues charging them during service.
