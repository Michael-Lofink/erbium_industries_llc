# Android assembly rights

**Citation:** `A2%13&1-3` · **Status:** established

**Formal title:** Exclusive Android Assembly Authority

## What it does

Android communities have the exclusive right to assemble new androids. Non-android interests may not independently manufacture them.

## Application

The law protects against renewed manufacture of synthetic servants. Android communities nevertheless depend on Erbium-controlled foundries, components, firmware, and industrial licenses.
