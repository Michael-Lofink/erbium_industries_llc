# Legal status of cradle colonies

**Citation:** `A2%8&3-11` · **Status:** established

## What it does

Classifies Fonqugon cradle colonies as sapient-supportive reproductive ecologies. They receive ecological and cultural protection, but no independent legal standing, right of refusal, ownership, or representation.

## Application

Authorities can act on a colony through ecological and emergency powers without following the procedures required for action against a person. The source does not give this provision a formal title.
