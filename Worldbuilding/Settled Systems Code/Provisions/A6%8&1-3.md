# Closure proceedings

**Citation:** `A6%8&1-3` · **Status:** provisional

**Formal title:** Closure Eligibility, Sentencing, and Final Order

> [!note] Provisional
> This citation is reserved in an existing note. Its standalone law has not been completed.

## What it does

The continuity-node law reserves this citation for capital eligibility, sentencing, review, appeal, and execution procedures. Closure means execution.

## Application

The parent law requires conviction of a qualifying offense, separate sentencing, tribunal confirmation, completed or exhausted review, and a final order. A referral alone does not authorize execution. The complete standalone procedure remains unwritten.
