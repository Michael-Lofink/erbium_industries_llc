# Entry to recover integrated property

**Citation:** `A6%1&8-5` · **Status:** example

**Formal title:** Entry and Physical Recovery of Integrated Corporate Assets

> [!note] Worked example
> This was a drafting example in the old guide. It is not established law.

## What it does

The original guide suggests this citation for a separate power authorizing entry and physical recovery of leased integrated components.

## Application

It is a worked example paired with [[A3%4&7-2]]. No established warrant requirements, exemptions, or enforcement procedure have been supplied.
