# Emergency water requisition

**Citation:** `A9%8&7-9` · **Status:** established

**Formal title:** Emergency Water and Conveyance Requisition Provision

## What it does

Allows recognized governments to temporarily direct privately controlled water supplies and transport during declared drought, flood, fire, or public-health emergencies.

## Application

Owners are entitled to compensation based on authenticated use records and existing public obligations. Small operators often wait longer than corporate providers for payment. Independent community supplies can be requisitioned early.
