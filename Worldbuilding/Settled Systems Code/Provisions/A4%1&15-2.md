# Independent settler classification

**Citation:** `A4%1&15-2` · **Status:** established

**Formal title:** Independent Charter Operator Classification

## What it does

Generally treats settlers as independent operators when they control their land use, production, and commercial decisions. Financing or buying their output does not ordinarily make an institution their employer.

## Application

The classification limits claims to wages, employment protections, and benefits. It can apply even where contracts leave settlers with little practical choice of equipment or customer.
