# Land records after a disaster

**Citation:** `A1%1&17-4` · **Status:** established

**Formal title:** Authenticated Cadastral Record Priority Provision

## What it does

Recognized survey and title records are presumed accurate when local evidence has been lost. The presumption can be challenged.

## Application

On Agrio Dysis IV, Charter House Six supplies much of the accepted evidence. Ground books, witnesses, and proof of occupation can rebut a survey, but doing so takes time and legal assistance.
