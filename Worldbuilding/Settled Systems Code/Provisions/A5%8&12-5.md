# Taking financed property offworld

**Citation:** `A5%8&12-5` · **Status:** established

**Formal title:** Encumbered Equipment Transport Verification Provision

## What it does

Requires ports and carriers to verify permission before leased or financed property leaves the jurisdiction where its lien can be enforced. This includes livestock, equipment, medical devices, and implants.

## Application

Debt alone does not prohibit a person from leaving Agrio Dysis IV. The property check can nevertheless prevent a family from taking what it needs to live or work elsewhere.
