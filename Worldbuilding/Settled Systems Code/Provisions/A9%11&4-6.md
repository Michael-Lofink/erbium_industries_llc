# Void Calling precautions

**Citation:** `A9%11&4-6` · **Status:** established

**Formal title:** Extended-Voyage Cognitive Exposure and Quarantine Standard

## What it does

Classifies Void Calling as vacuum-associated cognitive exposure syndrome and establishes minimum prevention, reporting, quarantine, airlock-security, and treatment procedures for registered long-voyage vessels.

## Application

Vessels must document watch rotations and an approved grounding protocol, restrict airlock access, remove symptomatic personnel from flight-critical work, record unexplained exterior alarms, and provide treatment or diversion toward a natural planetary atmosphere. Report Exposure stage-3-or-greater incidents and every episode of true Void Calling. Medical records distinguish dormant infection from confirmed cure.

Natural planetary atmosphere suppresses symptoms and transmission while the infection continues progressing. Recovery there can clear the disease; examinations remain inconclusive. Outside atmosphere, recovery stops at stage 1 unless an effect counteracts or removes the disease. Transmission begins at stage 1 while the infection is active. Drift travel prevents new exposure; suppression of an existing infection remains a common crew belief with uncertain evidence.

The actual protocol requires supervision and sensory grounding. Corporate security often substitutes sedation and solitary restraint. Operators use completed logs to argue individual noncompliance; incomplete records can jeopardize insurance, docking, and survivor compensation.
