# Void Calling precautions

**Citation:** `A9%11&4-6` · **Status:** established

**Formal title:** Extended-Voyage Cognitive Exposure and Quarantine Standard

## What it does

Classifies Void Calling as vacuum-associated cognitive exposure syndrome and establishes minimum prevention, reporting, quarantine, airlock-security, and treatment procedures for registered long-voyage vessels.

## Application

Vessels must document watch rotations and an approved grounding protocol, restrict airlock access, remove symptomatic personnel from flight-critical work, record unexplained exterior alarms, provide treatment or diversion toward an atmosphere, and report stage-3 or greater incidents.

The actual protocol requires supervision and sensory grounding. Corporate security often substitutes sedation and solitary restraint. Operators use completed logs to argue individual noncompliance; incomplete records can jeopardize insurance, docking, and survivor compensation.
