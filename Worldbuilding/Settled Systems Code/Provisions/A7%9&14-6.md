# Redrawing flooded parcels

**Citation:** `A7%9&14-6` · **Status:** established

**Formal title:** Post-Inundation Parcel Reconciliation Provision

## What it does

Authorizes boundary reassessment after flooding makes a parcel’s existing legal description unusable.

## Application

Agrio Dysis IV’s Office of Survey and Record administers reassessment using substantial amounts of Charter House Six data. New boundaries may divide occupied property or transfer valuable access before competing evidence can be heard.
