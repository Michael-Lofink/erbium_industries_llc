# Port closures during the Shredder

**Citation:** `A5%11&9-4` · **Status:** established

**Formal title:** Periastron Port Suspension and Atmospheric Docking Restriction

## What it does

Suspends ordinary atmospheric arrivals, departures, and external intercity docking during declared Shredder conditions.

## Application

The restriction reduces collision and structural risks. It also strands travelers, delays essential deliveries, and can prevent displaced employees from reaching registered housing.
