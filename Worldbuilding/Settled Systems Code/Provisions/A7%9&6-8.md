# Keeping a settlement charter

**Citation:** `A7%9&6-8` · **Status:** established

**Formal title:** Beneficial Settlement Occupancy Requirement

## What it does

Allows authorities to reclaim chartered land when a holder fails requirements for verified residence, productive use, environmental stewardship, or improvements.

## Application

The rule discourages speculative claims that leave land unused. Illness, failed infrastructure, and evacuation can also prevent residents from meeting its conditions.
