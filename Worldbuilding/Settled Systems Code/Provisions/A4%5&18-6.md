# Staffing during the Shredder

**Citation:** `A4%5&18-6` · **Status:** established

**Formal title:** Periastron Continuity Labor Designation

## What it does

Allows governments and certified operators to reassign qualified staff, extend coverage, adjust rest schedules, and restrict departures during a declared recurring hazard.

## Application

The provision requires medical monitoring, replacement crews, and hazard compensation. Erbium classifies many Shredder duties as ordinary infrastructure work and pays standard wages for them.
