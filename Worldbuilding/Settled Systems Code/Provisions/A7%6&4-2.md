# Offworld cradle-colony permits

**Citation:** `A7%6&4-2` · **Status:** established

## What it does

Requires biological-continuity and invasive-ecology permits for offworld Fonqugon cradle colonies.

## Application

Improper habitats can kill colonies, harm surrounding ecosystems, or disrupt healthy formation. Authorities can use those risks to deny independent communities permits while approving Erbium facilities. No formal title is supplied.
