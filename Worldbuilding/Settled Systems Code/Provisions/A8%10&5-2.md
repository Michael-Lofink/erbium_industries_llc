# Infrastructure representation

**Citation:** `A8%10&5-2` · **Status:** established

**Formal title:** Essential Infrastructure Stewardship Representation Provision

## What it does

Guarantees representation to the recognized operator of shared relay synchronization, strategic erbium reserves, PES administration, and transport standards.

## Application

Erbium holds a permanent vote through the Infrastructure Stewardship Seat. The stated purpose is technical participation by the institution responsible for implementation; the seat also lets Erbium vote on laws governing its own operations.
