# Monitoring aquifer extraction

**Citation:** `A7%6&11-3` · **Status:** established

**Formal title:** Confined Aquifer Withdrawal and Subsidence Monitoring Standard

## What it does

Requires high-volume operators to report extraction and its effects on pressure, recharge, ground movement, and downstream users.

## Application

The Planetary Water Bureau uses the reports for allocation and can suspend dangerous extraction. Large operators can afford compliant monitoring and legal staff, giving their records more weight than smaller communities’ observations.
