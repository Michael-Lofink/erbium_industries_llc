# The Vuddar volunteering exemption

**Citation:** `A4%9&4-7` · **Status:** established

**Formal title:** Vuddarian Cultural Sanctuary Exemption

## What it does

Exempts qualifying residents, clan institutions, ecological custodians, and facilities physically located on Vuddar from ordinary Great Volunteering selection and hosting requirements.

## Application

The exemption followed clan campaigning in late Lapse 4 PES. Offworld dwarves remain eligible. Residence and synchronization disputes can cause qualifying residents to be selected.
