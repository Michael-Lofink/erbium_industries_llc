# Custody of personal anchors

**Citation:** `A4%7&6-2` · **Status:** established

**Formal title:** Personal Stabilization Article Custodial Accommodation

## What it does

Permits designated employers and civic-service authorities to retain objects needed for an employee’s emotional regulation during designated service. It is ancestry-neutral but primarily affects brenneri anchors.

## Application

Anchors are catalogued and stored in approved facilities. Substitutes rarely reproduce their sensory familiarity. Supervised access is granted at management’s discretion and used as an incentive or disciplinary measure.

Return is scheduled after all qualifying service obligations are complete. Debt, housing charges, identity disputes, and reviews can delay it. If the owner dies, the anchor should pass to a designated beneficiary, but fees and competing claims can prevent transfer.
