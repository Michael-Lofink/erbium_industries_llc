# Detaching endangered city sections

**Citation:** `A9%6&12-2` · **Status:** established

**Formal title:** Aerostat Compartment Separation Authority

## What it does

Permits designated infrastructure officers to detach compartments that credibly threaten a larger inhabited aerostat system.

## Application

Introduced following the Lapse 18 stabilization disaster on Theosis. Separation can save the remaining city, but lower industrial districts carry much of its weight and are frequently chosen for detachment.
