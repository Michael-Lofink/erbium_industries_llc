# Preserving continuity evidence

**Citation:** `A6%6&3-5` · **Status:** provisional

**Formal title:** Continuity Evidence Seizure and Preservation

> [!note] Provisional
> This citation is reserved in an existing note. Its standalone law has not been completed.

## What it does

The continuity-node law identifies this as authority to seize identification equipment, biological samples, and records involved in a continuity investigation.

## Application

The source does not define a separate warrant standard, retention period, or return procedure.
