# Housing sponsorship

**Citation:** `A4%7&14-3` · **Status:** established

**Formal title:** Pressure-Rated Habitation Eligibility and Transitional Continuity Provision

## What it does

Requires residents of certified compartments to hold a recognized sponsorship category, including employment, dependent, educational, medical, civic, or private sponsorship.

## Application

These records support evacuation and life-support planning. On Theosis, Erbium and affiliated landlords can end housing eligibility when employment ends. The source gives no universal grace period.
