# Temporary identity detention

**Citation:** `A6%4&2-7` · **Status:** provisional

**Formal title:** Temporary Unverified Continuity Detention

> [!note] Provisional
> This citation is reserved in an existing note. Its standalone law has not been completed.

## What it does

The continuity-node law identifies this as authority for detention while an identity discrepancy is investigated.

## Application

The existing parent law requires a stamped incident record and further judicial or administrative authorization after 1,440 standard minutes of a routine hold. This citation remains provisional; its standalone text has not been written.
