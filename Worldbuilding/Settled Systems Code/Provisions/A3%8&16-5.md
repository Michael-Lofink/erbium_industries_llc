# Liability for inspection warrants

**Citation:** `A3%8&16-5` · **Status:** established

**Formal title:** Certified Craft-Warrant Liability Allocation

## What it does

Treats a qualified professional’s craft-warrant as evidence that they accepted responsibility for the condition disclosed at inspection.

## Application

The rule was intended to prevent temporary contractors from approving unsafe work and disappearing. Erbium and Directorate courts may hold engineers or clans liable after management rejects repairs, changes materials or records, or exceeds the disclosed service limit.
