# Continuity Node Integrity

**Citation:** `A2%1&4-13` · **Status:** established

**Formal title:** Continuity Identification Node Integrity and Anti-Falsification Provision

## What it does

Prohibits deliberate interference with an approved continuity node and fraudulent use of its credentials. An unreadable node can lead to temporary restrictions, but does not by itself prove guilt.

The law applies to people required to carry nodes. Astrazoans are its most frequent subjects, though its wording is ancestry-neutral. Accidental damage and necessary emergency medical intervention are protected; reporting and verification duties still apply.

**Closure means execution.** Ordinary node violations do not qualify. An aggravated case must meet the additional capital threshold and pass through an authorized tribunal and review process.

## Authorities

The **Settled Systems Identity Continuity Authority** originated the provision. The **Settled Systems Continuity Registry** administers it, with **Erbium Industries Relay Identity Services** as technical custodian. Enforcement involves local civil authorities, authorized infrastructure security, Erbium Corporate Security, and the **Settled Systems Continuity Tribunal**.

The citation sits under Title 2, Chapter 1 (**Identity and Biological Continuity**), Section 4 (**Continuity Identification Nodes**). The separate requirement to carry a node is reserved under [[A2%1&4-4]].

## Public Purpose

Continuity nodes authenticate people whose bodies can defeat ordinary biometrics. They protect identity and records through changes of appearance, reconstruction, and authorized remembrance work. Erbium argues that reliable identification permits bodily freedom; critics object to the surveillance required to exercise it.

## Statutory Text

The following is a shortened account of the existing subclauses. Their letters and legal distinctions are preserved.

### `A2%1&4-13.a` — Prohibited Interference

Knowingly interfering with a node to conceal identity, evade lawful verification or restrictions, transfer credentials, or obtain access through a false response is prohibited. Physical damage alone does not establish intent.

### `A2%1&4-13.b` — Falsified Continuity Response

Knowingly making, possessing, installing, transmitting, or using means intended to identify a bearer as another person is prohibited. Possession for an authorized medical, technical, archival, security, or remembrance function does not by itself establish a violation.

### `A2%1&4-13.c` — Unauthorized Continuity Transfer

Transferring nodes, keys, remembrance authorizations, or registry credentials between people requires prior Registry approval. A credential remains attached to the legal person through bodily changes and service or remembrance assignments.

### `A2%1&4-13.d` — Duty to Report an Integrity Event

Report known or reasonably suspected compromise within **120 standard minutes** of discovery. Submit to approved validation within **1,440 standard minutes**, or before requesting controlled access, whichever comes first. For an off-stamp bearer, the reporting period begins at the first successful connection to a recognized stamp authority.

### `A2%1&4-13.e` — Unverified Continuity Status

A failed scan or refusal of lawful scanning permits temporary unverified status and restrictions on controlled services, property, and records. This classification does not establish criminal intent, infiltrator status, or closure eligibility. The bearer retains legal personhood.

### `A2%1&4-13.f` — Unverified Infiltrator Designation

Requires unverified continuity plus reasonable evidence of deliberate identity fraud, unauthorized access, evasion of restrictions, or concealment of another person's death or replacement. An unreadable node, refusal to scan, or unregistered bodily change is insufficient alone. The designation permits investigation and detention, not a finding of guilt.

### `A2%1&4-13.g` — Emergency Medical Interference

Licensed professionals may interfere with a node to prevent immediate serious bodily harm or permit necessary emergency surgery. They must preserve recoverable components, document the necessity in a stamped incident record, report at the first medically reasonable opportunity, and help obtain temporary certification. Necessary intervention is not a violation.

### `A2%1&4-13.h` — Accidental Damage

Damage outside the bearer's reasonable control does not itself create criminal liability. Restrictions can remain until verification is restored. Inspection, replacement, and certification costs may be allocated through existing medical, employment, insurance, or equipment agreements.

### `A2%1&4-13.i` — Authorized Variance

The Registry can approve controlled interference for designated protective, medical, research, or security operations, including remembrance testing. Authorization may be sealed from the bearer, local authorities, ordinary query systems, or the public if disclosure would defeat its purpose.

### `A2%1&4-13.j` — Aggravated Continuity Interference

Aggravation covers interference intended to seize critical systems, exercise unauthorized remembrance authority, divert controlled assets, conceal a death or replacement, evade custody or a closure order, or facilitate an organized attack. It permits immediate detention and enhanced prosecution under Title 6.

### `A2%1&4-13.k` — Closure Eligibility

Prosecutors must produce evidence of knowing aggravated interference and at least one qualifying circumstance:

- It directly caused a death or was intended to cause death.
- It created a substantial and imminent risk of multiple deaths through critical infrastructure.
- It concealed a killing in a way that materially obstructed rescue, investigation, or protection of further victims.
- It materially assisted another independently closure-eligible offense.

Referral is not conviction and does not authorize execution.

### `A2%1&4-13.l` — Limitation on Closure

Closure cannot rest solely on node damage, scan refusal, off-stamp status, an unregistered appearance, reporting failure, unauthorized medical treatment, avoidance of ancestry disclosure, nonviolent identity fraud, or infrastructure entry without causing or intending fatal harm.

Execution requires a final judgment under [[A6%8&1-3]] and completion or exhaustion of Title 6 review procedures.

## Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Authorized node** | A Registry-approved, body-bound identification device. It normally transmits a key rather than storing the entire identity file. |
| **Continuity key** | The cryptographic credential linking the bearer to a registered legal person. |
| **Continuity response** | The result of checking the key, device integrity, and registry status. It may confirm identity, impose restrictions, or flag a dispute. |
| **Integrity event** | Any event that could compromise the device or its associated record. |
| **Masking** | Deliberately preventing detection, authentication, or connection to the registry. It can involve signal, software, bodily, or access interference. |
| **Falsification** | Deliberately misrepresenting identity, authorization, device integrity, life status, or legal restrictions. |
| **Continuity suspension** | Temporary restriction of identity-dependent services while a discrepancy is unresolved. It does not end personhood. |
| **Controlled infrastructure** | A designated system, facility, or service requiring verified continuity. |
| **Critical infrastructure** | Controlled infrastructure whose misuse or interruption could threaten life or essential services. |
| **Closure review** | A capital proceeding to determine whether the offense permits a death sentence. |

## Administrative Interpretation

The Registry treats deliberate reductions in a node's availability or reliability as grounds for investigation, even without physical damage. Erbium-maintained relay records normally take precedence, followed by stamped Registry records, approved medical or remembrance authorizations, certified local records, and independently witnessed or off-stamp evidence.

The bearer usually has to resolve discrepancies. All registered skins and service identities remain attached to the same person; a skin has no independent legal standing. This permits tracking across appearances and can disclose ancestry the bearer otherwise keeps private.

Erbium may investigate, detain, preserve evidence, and recommend a capital referral under delegated authority. It cannot formally order execution without an authorized tribunal. Prosecutors often argue that fraud involving critical infrastructure creates the fatal risk required for enhanced charges.

## Procedures

Routine readers request a continuity key, integrity state, registry confirmation, and relevant access restrictions. Queries are described as limited to necessary information, but bearers may not be told that a query occurred or how its record will be retained.

An integrity warning may lead to temporary certification while inspection proceeds. Validation can require medical and identity evidence, witness testimony, and technical examination of the node.

A temporary hold can follow a critical-access request, contradictory credentials, suspected use of another person's node, unauthorized remembrance, or a registry record that places the bearer elsewhere or lists them as dead or missing. The officer must make a stamped incident record. A routine hold requires judicial or administrative authorization to continue beyond **1,440 standard minutes**.

An infiltrator designation requires a recorded account of the targeted identity or infrastructure, evidence of deliberate concealment, and the risk of release. A capital referral must separately identify the aggravating act, fatal consequence or risk, connecting evidence, reasons noncapital prosecution is insufficient, and whether victims or families were consulted. Family support is not required.

Status, detention, record changes, remembrance revocation, and capital eligibility can be challenged. An appeal does not automatically restore services or secure release. Off-stamp evidence is accepted at the Registry's discretion.

## Exemptions

Recognized exemptions cover authorized medical and technical work, approved remembrance activity, and designated protective or research operations. Approved off-stamp protocols and emergencies without verification equipment can also qualify.

Sealed variances may suppress query logs, location information, or ordinary identity details. Intelligence staff, corporate security, continuity personnel, and some executives receive protections rarely available to ordinary employees.

## Penalties

Accidental damage can still require inspection, replacement, and temporary reassignment. Administrative noncompliance may result in fines or loss of access and authorizations. Intentional interference can lead to detention, employment and travel restrictions, reduced service credits, civic service, or prosecution. Falsification can additionally prompt equipment seizure and asset freezing; aggravated cases permit longer or permanent restrictions under Title 6.

A death sentence requires a qualifying conviction, separate sentencing, tribunal confirmation, completed or exhausted review, and a final closure order. This provision does not supply fixed fine amounts or sentences for ordinary violations.

## Amendment History

No amendment stamps have been recorded. The existing account describes an original anti-fraud rule, followed by remembrance and reconstruction amendments, expanded coverage of electronic interference, and explicit limits on capital punishment.

## GM Information

> [!danger] GM only
> These details come from the existing law note and are not public knowledge.

The law expanded after a publicized incident involving an astrazoan remembrance of a deceased relay technician. Public reports blamed unauthorized impersonation for a threatened communications failure. The sealed investigation found that Erbium had left the former employee's remembrance credentials active, deferred maintenance caused the fault, and the astrazoan entered to prevent it. No evidence established impersonation for personal gain.

Erbium systems retrieve more information than the public account admits, including query history, registered skins, remembrance assignments, and private administrative flags. Records are retained beyond the stated period. Administrators can mark a valid key compromised or disputed without altering the device, excluding its bearer from services while making the problem appear to be tampering.

Privileged users receive sealed variances that permit the concealment forbidden to ordinary bearers. Prosecutors also use possible capital charges to obtain pleas, information, surrendered records, waived claims, or long civic-service commitments. They interpret mass-casualty risk especially broadly around corporate infrastructure, including cases where no sabotage occurred.

Closure is publicly justified as finality for victims. Internally, Erbium values its ability to end testimony and disputes over manipulated records. Families opposing execution may be excluded from public messaging. Public opinion and victims' wishes vary; neither referral nor sentencing requires their support.
