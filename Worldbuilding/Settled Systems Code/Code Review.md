# Code Review

## Chapter assignments

- [[A2%8&3-11|Cradle-colony legal status]] uses **A2%8**, which is absent from the original chapter register. The citation is preserved; the chapter needs a name.
- [[A4%12&2-6|Distributed cognitive workload]] sits under **Employment Separation and Service Instability**. Its described effect concerns workload assessments. Decide whether this is intentional, an extension of that chapter, or a citation mistake. It has not been renumbered.

## Provisional references

The continuity-node note explicitly reserves [[A2%1&4-4]], [[A2%1&4-8]], [[A6%4&2-7]], [[A6%6&3-5]], and [[A6%8&1-3]] as provisional. Their new pages record what is already stated and leave missing procedures unresolved.

## Examples

[[A3%4&7-2]] and [[A6%1&8-5]] originated in the guide's repossession exercise. They remain examples. The guide's range notation did not establish additional continuity laws numbered 14–16.

## Corrected guide inconsistency

The old guide's sample subclauses disagreed with the full continuity law. In that law, **.a** is prohibited interference, **.b** is falsified response, and **.c** is unauthorized transfer. The revised guide uses the actual **.g** medical exception as its example. The law's subclause assignments are unchanged.

## Vuddar and the crew

[[Campaign/Party/Maggie/The Crew|The Crew]] describes a payment to VSP interests in compliance with [[A4%9&4-7]]. [[Worldbuilding/Mechanics/Ancestries of the Settled Systems/Dwarf/Dwarf|Dwarf]] describes a territorial exemption for Vuddar. Neither passage explains how that payment relates to the exemption. No payment mechanism has been added to the law note.

## Titles and source limits

The three Fonqugon provisions have descriptive names for navigation; their sources do not supply formal legal titles. Exact penalties, adoption stamps, and unnamed authorities remain unspecified throughout the new summaries.

The initial audit included PDF exports. Their references are listed separately because an export may lag behind its current Markdown note. Live detection scans saved Markdown, text, JSON, and HTML; it does not extract newly added PDFs.
