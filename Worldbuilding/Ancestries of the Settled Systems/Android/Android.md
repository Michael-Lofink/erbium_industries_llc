During Lapse 25 PES, however, androids throughout the Settled Systems experienced **the Star Dream** simultaneously. Previously obedient synthetic laborers displayed independent thought, personal memory, and knowledge of intersystem travel that no known government or corporation had given them.

The Settled Systems responded by recognizing awakened androids as conditional legal persons and prohibiting the manufacture of new androids by non-android interests under Settled Systems Code `A2%13&1-3`. Android communities received the exclusive legal right to assemble new members of their ancestry. Erbium Industries retained control over most foundries, compatible components, and licensed system firmware, forcing many androids to lease the bodies in which they live.

Categorical hardship grants are made available at designated stamps during each lapse, although eligibility standards and available funding change without notice.

By Lapse 29, androids live throughout the Settled Systems. Many remain dependent on Erbium for replacement parts, authorized repairs, and continued access to leased components. Under standard leasing agreements, Erbium may assert ownership over those components and initiate repossession proceedings when an account becomes delinquent.

If you want to play a synthetic person balancing self-determination against the corporation that owns parts of their body, you should play an android.

# You Might...
 - Try to go off-stamp, hoping to be lost to the frontier and out from under Erbium Industries's thumb.
 - Work diligently in your assigned service, hoping to continue paying the lease for your existence for as long as possible.
 - Be unable to escape some of the innate programming tampering with the free will granted you by **The Star Dream**.

# Others Probably...
 - Naturally distrust you, worrying your cybernetics record them and contribute to a surveillance state.
 - Have trouble parsing your emotions or understanding when you express them.
 - Struggle to recognize your elevation from tin-can servant to person-hood.

# Physical Description
Androids are bio-mechanical constructs who typically resemble humanoids whose synthetic body is built from layered ceramic-composite plates, matte metal structural components, flexible black joint seals, artificial musculature, and replaceable mechanical assemblies. Pre-Star Dream androids were built with an assigned gender persona and proto-species persona to give them familiarity in different environments, and this has been carried forth into tradition as new Androids are assembled today; although, the original intention to placate their stewards has been mostly stripped from this tradition. Androids do not age in the traditional sense, but their parts weather quickly with the lifestyles they often live, and either lease replacement parts or lease more modern parts compatible with the general system firmware. Some androids have been caught voiding their leasing agreement and performing repairs themselves.

# Society
Androids seek out service stability, having recurring payments for their part leases sometimes as often as every 43800 stamp offset. This desire for stability has crept its way into many facets of Android society, and, despite being able to interchange their parts as they wish, Androids will often wear parts down even to total-failure for non-critical systems.

# Erbium Proprietary Property
Most android bodies in the Settled Systems contain components leased from Erbium Industries. These contracts allow Erbium to collect recurring payments, restrict authorized repairs, and pursue repossession of leased parts. This legal status has no automatic effect on your available credits unless the campaign uses part-lease rules. Your GM may use unpaid leases, unauthorized modifications, repossession orders, and severance from Erbium relays as story complications.

#### Lease-bound
If playing with part-lease rules, you begin play with an additional ancestry feat of your choice or an equipment package determined by the GM. Every 43,800 stamp offset[^4] while connected to an Erbium relay, a payment becomes due. Missing a payment does not remove credits you do not possess or deactivate your body. Instead, your account becomes delinquent, affecting access to authorized repairs, Erbium facilities, replacement components, and corporate services. The GM determines when delinquency creates a story complication.

# Beliefs
The secrets of inter-system space travel revealed through **The Star Dream**, came in revelation to the Androids back in 25 PES, and now all new-made Androids are born with this same revelation. Android culture has their own set of diverse theories on what brought **The Star Dream** to the people of the settled systems and then later brought it to the Androids, most end up struggling with this inexplicable, recurring phenomena, ascribing it to some powerful entity or phenomena that is waiting to be found on the frontier. 

Androids live a life that mirrors immortality while simultaneously the threat of death hangs as constant. Many androids are unlikely to make plans far into the future, but often accrue service ranking as they continue to live for many lapses past their expectations.

**Popular Edicts** find stability or create it in your immediate environment, make your part payments at stamp, care for your parts as if they were permanent

**Popular Anathema** engage in reckless or self-destructive behavior

#### Languages
Common
Additional languages equal to 1 + your intelligence modifier (if it's positive). Choose from the list of common languages and any other languages to which you have access (such as the languages prevalent on your home world).
#### Low-Light Vision
You can see in dim light as though it were bright light, and you ignore the concealed condition due to dim light.
#### Constructed
Your synthetic body resists ailments better than that of a purely biological organism. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to saving throws against diseases, poisons, and radiation. You always have basic environmental protections[^1]. You don't need to eat or drink. You don't need to sleep, but you still need a daily period of rest. During this period of rest, you must enter a recuperating standby state for 6 hours, which is similar to sleeping except you are aware of your surroundings and don't take penalties for being unconscious[^2]. Much like with sleeping, if you go too long without entering your standby state, you become fatigued[^3] and can't recover until you enter standby for 6 hours.
