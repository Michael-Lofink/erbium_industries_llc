# ==Damaya Lashunta==
Your Second Emergence produced a tall, graceful humanoid body shaped around beauty, fine sensory control, telepathic precision, memory, and sustained intellectual work. Your features were deliberately developed to appear attractive, emotionally readable, and nonthreatening according to the dominant standards of the Settled Systems. Settled Systems institutions frequently presume that a Damaya is educated, respectable, socially competent, and familiar with professional etiquette. This presumption can benefit Damayas who possess none of those qualities and burden those who are expected to remain beautiful, composed, emotionally available, and grateful for the access their expression provides. 

Your appearance conforms closely to the beauty standards promoted throughout the Settled Systems. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to checks to [Make an Impression](https://2e.aonsrd.com/actions/82-make-an-impression) on a creature that can clearly perceive your appearance and whose culture broadly recognizes Damaya presentation as attractive or socially desirable. This bonus applies whether you attempt the check using Diplomacy or another skill. It doesn’t apply if the creature is Hostile toward you, is specifically prejudiced against Damayas, or belongs to a community that rejects Convergence and its beauty standards.

Your developmental education included the etiquette, credentials, terminology, and procedural habits expected within Settled Systems institutions. You become trained in <u>Society</u>. If you would already become trained in Society from your background or class, you instead become trained in another skill of your choice. When you attempt to [Make an Impression](https://2e.aonsrd.com/actions/82-make-an-impression) on or [Request](https://2e.aonsrd.com/actions/83-request) something from an employee, official, administrator, academic, physician, or other representative of a formal Settled Systems institution, you can roll Society instead of Diplomacy. You must be able to present yourself through the institution’s expected language, etiquette, credentials, or procedures. This ability doesn’t allow you to falsify credentials you don’t possess, though it can allow you to recognize which credentials are expected and how they would ordinarily be presented.

You gain an attribute boost to Intelligence and an attribute flaw to Constitution. Your free ancestry boost can’t also be applied to Intelligence.



# ==Korasha Lashunta==
> [!note] Table Note
> Encourage the other players to track their characters’ Bulk. A Korasha’s expanded carrying capacity matters most when someone needs to be dragged out of a wreck, carried away from danger, or relieved of heavy equipment. It takes a village.

Your Second Emergence produced a compact, muscular humanoid body reinforced for physical endurance, rapid reaction, violent impact, and controlled aggression. Your appearance remains conventionally attractive and recognizably humanoid, while heavier chitin, reinforced joints, dense musculature, and a lower center of gravity distinguish you from a Damaya. Settled Systems institutions treat the Korasha body as a promise of useful strength. Employers assume you can carry more, work longer, enter danger sooner, and absorb risks that would be considered unreasonable for another employee. Your physical ability earns respect so long as it remains disciplined and directed toward an approved purpose.

Your reinforced skeleton, dense musculature, and connective tissues are developed for sustained labor and heavy loads. You become trained in <u>Athletics</u>. If you would already become trained in Athletics from your background or class, you instead become trained in another skill of your choice. In addition, you can carry 2 additional Bulk before becoming encumbered and can hold or lift 2 additional Bulk beyond your normal maximum.

You have been trained to apply strength through leverage, balance, and deliberate movement rather than uncontrolled exertion. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Athletics checks to [Force Open](https://2e.aonsrd.com/actions/63-force-open), [Shove](https://2e.aonsrd.com/actions/164-shove), or [Trip](https://2e.aonsrd.com/actions/186-trip). When you critically fail an Athletics check to Force Open, Shove, or Trip, you can treat the result as a failure instead. Once you use this benefit, you can’t use it again for 10 minutes.

You gain 8 Hit Points from your ancestry instead of 6. You also gain an attribute boost to Strength and an attribute flaw to Wisdom. Your free ancestry boost can’t also be applied to Strength.



# ==Unbound Lashunta==
You did not complete a recognized Damaya, Korasha, or Katalash Second Emergence. You might possess a combination of traits from several expressions, have interrupted your development, survived an incomplete metamorphosis, or deliberately pursued a body that existing programs do not recognize.

Your body might be tall and muscular, short and lightly built, asymmetrical, partially chitinous, unusually scaled, or otherwise difficult to classify. Institutions frequently treat Unbound lashuntas as medically unfinished even when their body is precisely what they intended. You can take Lashunta ancestry feats with a prerequisite requiring a specific Lashunta heritage or expression, including Damaya, Korasha, or Katalash, provided you meet all other prerequisites and the feat’s level is no greater than half your level. You count as possessing the required heritage or expression only for selecting and using that feat. You don’t gain that heritage’s traits, attribute modifiers, Hit Points, Basic Katalash Benefits, or any other abilities unless the feat specifically grants them.

You also gain the Unbound Mind reaction.
#### Unbound Mind `pf2:R`
**Trigger** You attempt a saving throw against a mental effect, but you haven’t rolled yet.

You reject the attempt to impose another creature’s will upon your mind. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to the triggering saving throw and to other saving throws against mental effects until the start of your next turn.



# ==Armored Katalash Lashunta==
```sf2e-stats
| ==Katalash==
```

Your Second Emergence developed dense overlapping chitin across your torso, limbs, neck, and skull. These plates flex around your joints while dispersing impacts that would tear or sever softer bodies.

You have [[Katalash Benefits|Basic Katalash Benefits]]. You gain resistance to slashing damage equal to half your level, with a minimum resistance of 1.
Your chitin is a living part of your body. Damage to it can be treated using Medicine and restored through ordinary healing.

# ==Pursuit Katalash Lashunta==
```sf2e-stats
| ==Katalash==
```

You preserve the reinforced hind limbs, forward balance, sudden acceleration, and functional wings of an ancestral Velish pursuit predator. Two or more folded insectoid wings emerge from reinforced structures along your upper back, opening into thin, durable flight membranes supported by chitinous veins. Your body is built to close distances quickly, navigate broken terrain, and pursue prey through Velis’s dense forests, wetlands, and elevated vegetation.

You have [[Katalash Benefits|Basic Katalash Benefits]]. Your Speed is 30 feet instead of 25 feet. You gain a 15-foot Fly Speed. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Athletics checks to High Jump or Long Jump. When you Leap horizontally, increase the distance you Leap by 5 feet.

# ==Faceted-Sight Katalash Lashunta==
```sf2e-stats
| ==Katalash==
```

Your layered eyes process several overlapping fields of vision, supported by secondary sensory structures along your antennae, brow, and jaw. Motion that would escape humanoid sight remains visible to you.

You have [[Katalash Benefits|Basic Katalash Benefits]]. You gain darkvision. When you use the Seek action, you can search a 60-foot cone or a 30-foot emanation instead of the normal area.

# ==Rending Katalash Lashunta==
```sf2e-stats
| ==Katalash==
```

Your Second Emergence preserved and reinforced the mandibles and hooked claws of ancestral lashuntas. These structures remain useful for pursuit, climbing, confined movement, and close combat.

You have [[Katalash Benefits|Basic Katalash Benefits]]. You gain a mandibles unarmed attack that deals 1d6 piercing damage. Your mandibles are in the brawling weapon group and have the unarmed trait. You also gain a claws unarmed attack that deals 1d4 slashing damage. Your claws are in the brawling weapon group and have the agile, finesse, and unarmed traits. Your mandibles and claws are biological parts of your body. Handwraps of mighty blows and similar effects apply to them normally.
