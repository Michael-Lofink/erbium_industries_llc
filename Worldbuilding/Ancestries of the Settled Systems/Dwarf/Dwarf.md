Hailing from the Zeta Hydrae System, on planet [[Zeta Hydrae II]] which they call "Vuddar," Dwarves are a short, squat, and otherwise hardy people. They take pride in tradition and as such have not terraformed much of their home-planet following their revelation from **The Star Dream** but instead established space stations in the night sky to pursue this technology from a safe distance. The dwarves natural inclination for craftsmanship had them adapt to the technological revelations of the dream very quickly; however, the many orbiting bodies in the sky above--whether it be Zeta Hydrae II II, Zeta Hydrae II III, or the planet's ring-- had stalled previous attempts to leave far past the atmosphere prior to the dream.

Being only 5.07 light years away from the Origin system of Wander (or 2.9805 * 10^13 miles), the dwarven people were one of the first to make contact with Wander following the Star Dream. Their pride in tradition made it difficult for the dwarves to advance their own society, but they are more than willing to partake in the advancements of other cultures and foster growth within them, always holding on to a part of home with their more primitive clan crystal.

In 29 PES, many clans of Dwarves have filtered throughout the Settled Systems. Many independent Dwarves find themselves in the triple star system Alpha Leonis 5.65 light years from Vuddar to work directly for Erbium Industries LLC on the buoyant city of the sulfur-planet: "Theosis," where a few times each Lapse, Theosis very briefly passes through the Roche Limit of Alpha Leonis C, a shredder zone for any star, that unless advanced-shielding technology is prepared by the dwarves, the gravity of the star will rip anything near it to pieces. Ships cannot land on Theosis during this time unless properly prepared, but typically travel routes schedule in the lapse to avoid this.

Dwarves have adapted to the long days of Theosis with its 11,760 stamp-offset cycle during its 1,322,496 stamp-offset orbit toiling throughout the daily offset restlessly while the other denizens of the buoyant city are fast asleep.

If you want to play a proud and stubborn artisan who holds their own tradition above the world, but partakes in other's advancements, you should play a dwarf.

# Physical Description
Dwarves typically stand around 4 feet tall, have wide and stocky frames, and generally weigh between 175 and 250 pounds. A dwarf's most noticeable physical feature is their hair. Dwarves of all genders take great pride in their haircare, weaving and braiding phenomenally intricate patterns into their beards and coifs, cleaning them with the finest products, and ensuring they only grow to a certain length (which is determined by their age). The older the dwarf, the longer their beard and hair, and the more respected they are by their clan.

Dwarves reach maturity at around age 25, but clans generally hold additional rites a dwarf must make pilgrimage back to Vuddar to partake in before they're considered an adult. Dwarves typically live up to 350 years old.

# Society

Dwarves revere their home planet Vuddar only slightly higher than they revere their clan. Although the vastness of the settled systems has separated many dwarves from the clans they call family. A dwarf will like make a sacred pilgrimage back to their home planet of Vuddar to reorient themselves with the traditional ways of life every quarter of the current lapse which they sanction well in advance with whatever service institute they are assigned. Many elder clan members live out the last of their days on Vuddar preparing the rites of Dwarven adulthood for those that journey home. Due to the sacred nature of Vuddar to the dwarven people, successful campaigning in late 4 PES following the establishment of **the Great Volunteering** established a sub-clause for Settled Systems Code `[placeholder]` allowing for the exemption of dwarven-interests housed on Vuddar from strictly-encouraged participation in and hosting of **the Great Volunteering** on planet. Dwarves traveling from their home of Vuddar have mistakenly been volunteered by being on a distant planet hosting the volunteering at the time along with a residential credential error misplacing their home-planet residence.

A dwarf can always rely on their clan to care for them, even if they've never met, and most clans have a headquarters, or clanholding on Theosis.

A clan crystal is fragmented from the crystalline core of the trees dotting the jungle-planet of Vuddar. Once carved, these crystals often resemble small statues depicting a notable family leader, and many dwarves strive to have their likeness rendered on a clan crystal someday. To lose or sell a clan crystal is deeply taboo. They're always crafted into a type of weapon valued by the individual clan.

#### Clan Crystal
At birth, you're given a one-handed simple weapon crafted with your clan crystal. The crystal can't be removed, and selling the weapon or crystal is a terrible taboo that earns you the disdain of other dwarves.

#### Vuddarian-Blooded
You or your ancestors hailed from the jungles on Vuddar. The great trunks of trees are planted firmly on underwater shelves, and their immense canopies are one of few places on the planet that remain above the highest tideline. Rope bridges span the gaps between branches. Platform buildings provide stability for housing and other infrastructure, but movement through the canopy cities require regular climbing and scrabbling up ladders.
  
You become trained in [Athletics](https://2e.aonsrd.com/skills/3-athletics). If you would automatically become trained in Athletics (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice.

#### Darkvision
You can see in darkness and dim light just as well as you can see in bright light, though your vision in darkness is in black and white.
