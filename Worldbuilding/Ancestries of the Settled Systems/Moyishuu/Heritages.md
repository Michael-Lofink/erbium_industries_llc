# ==Court of Dance Moyishuu==
Your nature is expressed primarily by movement, and you can seem aloof to many outsiders as you focus more on your movements than on those around you. You become trained in Acrobatics. If you would automatically become trained in Acrobatics, you instead become trained in another skill of your choice. You gain the Crowd Surfing skill feat.

Additionally, once per day, when you use Ready to prepare a single action with the move trait, you can use Ready as a single action instead of a 2-action activity. You must still designate a trigger, but you don’t choose the movement action until that trigger occurs. The action must be a single action with the move trait that you could have used when you Readied and can still use when you take the reaction. Your turn ends as normal when you Ready.

# ==Court of Fields Moyishuu==
Your nature is to enact plans, whether they’re immediate paths to your goal or years-long pursuits. You become trained in Diplomacy. If you would automatically become trained in Diplomacy, you instead become trained in another skill of your choice. You gain the Group Impression skill feat.

Additionally, once per day, when you use Aid, you can prepare to support a developing plan rather than one specific task. You don’t designate a specific ally or action. Until the start of your next turn, when an ally within 30 feet who can perceive you attempts an attack roll or skill check, you can use your reaction to Aid that creature. You can use Diplomacy for your check to Aid regardless of the check you’re assisting. All other rules for Aid apply.

# ==Court of Flames Moyishuu==
Your nature is to tackle your problems immediately and directly, even if it means leaving scorched earth in your wake. You become trained in Intimidation. If you would automatically become trained in Intimidation, you instead become trained in another skill of your choice. You gain the Quick Coercion skill feat.

Additionally, once per day, when you use Ready, you can leave both your action and trigger undesignated. Until the start of your next turn, immediately after another creature completes an action or at the end of its turn, you can use your reaction to use any single action or free action you could have chosen when you Readied and can still use at that moment. You can’t choose a free action that already has a trigger.

If the chosen action has the attack trait, it uses the multiple attack penalty you had when you used Ready.

# ==Court of Song Moyishuu==
Your nature is passionately artistic, and you have an intrinsic connection to the expression of that ability. You become trained in Performance. If you would automatically become trained in Performance, you instead become trained in another skill of your choice. You gain the Impressive Performance skill feat.

Additionally, once per day, when you or an ally within 30 feet who can perceive you is about to attempt a skill check, you can use your reaction to communicate a remembered technique through voice, movement, patterned light, or crystalline resonance. The creature uses your Performance modifier in place of its own modifier for the triggering check. This doesn’t allow the creature to use an action it couldn’t otherwise use or satisfy any proficiency requirement it doesn’t meet.
