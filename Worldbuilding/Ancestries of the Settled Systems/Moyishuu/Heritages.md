# ==Court of Dance Moyishuu==
Your nature is expressed primarily by movement, and you can seem aloof to many outsiders as you focus more on your movements than on those around you. You become trained in [Acrobatics](https://2e.aonsrd.com/skills/1-acrobatics). If you would automatically become trained in Acrobatics (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in another skill of your choice. You gain the [Crowd Surfing](https://2e.aonsrd.com/feats/785-crowd-surfing) skill feat. Additionally, once per day, you can temporarily attune your soulfeel to joy for 1 minute as a free action that has the [concentrate](https://2e.aonsrd.com/traits/46-concentrate) trait.
