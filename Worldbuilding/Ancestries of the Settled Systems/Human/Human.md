If Erbium Industries had its way, humans would be remembered as the only ever denizens of Wander. And to their credit, humans were likely the first. Since Erbium Industries LLC began on Wander, humans still make up much of the higher authorities of Erbium Industries and many work directly for the corporation in the buoyant cities of Theosis. The corporate structure of Erbium Industries is not an aristocracy but as corporate officials grow older, they make sure their children are well-educated and suited for the roles of higher corporate life, positioning their children to qualify for positions higher up the corporate ladder keeping other ancestries or non-affluent humans from joining the higher authorities of Erbium Industries.
