# Void Calling

> [!summary]
> **Void Calling**, also known as the **Star Plague**, is an occult disease associated with long periods spent beyond a planetary atmosphere.
>
> Settled Systems Code `A9%11&4-6` classifies it as **vacuum-associated cognitive exposure syndrome**, usually shortened to **vacuum sickness** in official reports. Spacers call it Void Calling because the afflicted rarely feel sick at first rather they hear someone outside the ship asking to be let in.
>
> Early symptoms include broken sleep, voices beneath engine noise, movement outside viewports, and the persistent feeling that someone is standing just beyond the hull. As the disease advances, familiar people become harder to recognize, the interior of the ship begins to feel off, and open space appears safe, familiar, and occupied.
>
> The lesser exposure syndrome can usually be treated before the victim answers the call. A person who enters vacuum while afflicted, encounters a [[Void-Drawn]], or travels through certain regions of deep space can develop true Void Calling. In its final stage, the victim dies and rises as a creature able to survive in vacuum and spread the disease to others.

## Nature of the Affliction

Void Calling is treated as a disease because it spreads between people, follows recognizable stages, responds to treatment, and can be contained through established shipboard procedures.

No consistent organism has ever been isolated from an afflicted person.

Transmission appears to occur through shared perception. Afflicted crew members often report related hallucinations without having discussed them. One hears knocking from outside the hull. Another sees a missing crewmate responsible for it. A third becomes convinced the airlock has already opened.

The disease does not appear during every long voyage. Recorded risk factors include:

- prolonged driftless travel outside a planetary atmosphere;
- repeated exposure to an unobstructed view of deep space;
- disrupted sleep and watch schedules;
- extended solitary duty;
- malfunctioning artificial horizons;
- unshielded occult or navigational systems;
- proximity to another afflicted person;
- contact with a Void-Drawn creature;
- travel through poorly charted frontier regions.

None of these conditions is present in every outbreak.

A natural planetary atmosphere interferes with the disease, even when that atmosphere is toxic or unbreathable. Artificial atmospheres aboard ships and stations do not provide the same protection.

## Hallucinations

Void Calling draws from the victim's memories, fears, expectations, and unfinished obligations. The result is personal enough to sound convincing.

Common hallucinations include:

- a missing family member knocking on the exterior hull;
- a child or companion drifting beyond a viewport without a suit;
- an emergency beacon broadcasting the victim's own voice;
- a maintenance worker signaling from an impossible exterior platform;
- a familiar settlement visible between the stars;
- a dead friend insisting that the vessel has already depressurized;
- crewmates appearing hollow, artificial, or slightly out of synchronization;
- stars that move when viewed indirectly;
- a corridor that seems to continue through a sealed exterior door;
- the certainty that everyone else has already evacuated;
- the belief that the ship is a replica built around the victim;
- someone outside asking the victim to open the door quietly.

The first hallucinations are rarely threatening. They ask for help, offer reassurance, or provide a plausible reason to leave the inhabited compartment.

## Recognition and Diagnosis

Early Void Calling can be mistaken for sleep deprivation, isolation stress, decompression injury, ordinary fear, hostile telepathy, navigational sickness, or sabotage.

Medicine can identify the recurring symptom pattern. Occultism can recognize the hallucinations as a coordinated influence rather than an ordinary sensory disturbance.

Physical examinations often reveal:

- disrupted sleep without corresponding stimulant use;
- repeated attention toward exterior access routes;
- difficulty recognizing familiar faces;
- contradictory reports of movement outside the hull;
- related hallucinations among people who have not spoken;
- normal pressure readings during reported decompression;
- attempts to disable viewport filters or airlock interlocks;
- unexplained resistance to vacuum during true Void Calling.

No single symptom confirms the disease.

Ship crews are expected to investigate reports of exterior distress until independent sensors rule them out. Some crews have died after dismissing real emergencies as hallucinations. Others have opened airlocks in response to emergencies that never occurred.

## Prevention

Long-voyage precautions usually include:

- paired or rotating watch assignments;
- limits on solitary duty;
- artificial horizon displays;
- scheduled changes in lighting, temperature, scent, and ambient sound;
- regular spoken identity and location checks;
- physical exercise under artificial gravity where available;
- scheduled periods with viewports covered;
- independent confirmation of exterior alarms;
- two-person authorization for airlock operation;
- mechanical locks that function during software failure;
- daily review of sleep, memory, and sensory disturbances;
- immediate removal from piloting, weapons, and airlock duty after credible symptoms;
- plans for diversion toward a planetary atmosphere if symptoms worsen.

Crew members are encouraged to choose **anchors** before departure. An anchor can be a person, object, recording, smell, song, routine, or shared memory that confirms where the person is and why they intend to remain there.

Specific anchors work better than generic reassurance. A sibling describing an embarrassing childhood injury, a tool with a familiar damaged grip, or a meal prepared from a family recipe can interrupt the hallucination long enough for the victim to recognize it.

## Treatment

Ordinary Treat Disease actions can assist recovery.

Medicine addresses exhaustion, malnutrition, injury, and the physical damage caused during an episode. Occult treatment helps the victim separate personal memory from the version of that memory used by the Calling.

Solitary confinement is considered dangerous. Isolation removes contradictory sensory information and gives the disease more control over the victim's perceived surroundings.

Treatment areas usually include:

- at least two consistent caregivers;
- no direct access to exterior locks;
- covered or filtered viewports;
- familiar physical objects;
- stable lighting and gravity;
- repeated orientation to current stamp, location, and personnel;
- manual environmental controls outside the patient's reach;
- independent verification of all reported emergencies.

Returning the victim to a natural planetary atmosphere is the most reliable nonmagical treatment. The atmosphere does not need to be breathable. A sealed habitat inside a toxic atmosphere still provides the beneficial effect.

Effects that counteract or remove diseases can cure either form normally. Recovery does not erase memories of the hallucinations.

## Settled Systems Classification

Settled Systems Code `A9%11&4-6`, the **Extended-Voyage Cognitive Exposure and Quarantine Standard**, establishes the official term _vacuum-associated cognitive exposure syndrome_ and sets minimum response procedures.

Registered long-voyage vessels must maintain:

- documented watch rotations;
- an approved grounding protocol;
- restricted airlock access;
- procedures for removing symptomatic personnel from flight-critical duty;
- records of unexplained exterior alarms;
- access to treatment or diversion toward an atmosphere;
- a formal report after any stage-3 or greater incident.

The law exists because one afflicted pilot, engineer, or security officer can kill an entire crew.

Its paperwork also gives operators a way to limit liability. A company with complete grounding logs and signed crew acknowledgments can argue that an outbreak resulted from individual noncompliance. Ships with incomplete records may lose insurance coverage, docking eligibility, or survivor compensation even when the required equipment failed.

Corporate security often interprets quarantine authority as permission to sedate and isolate afflicted workers. The actual protocol requires supervision and sensory grounding. Solitary restraint remains common aboard ships without medical personnel or spare compartments.

# Discovery

The first recorded outbreak is difficult to identify.

The earliest human and dwarven crews traveling after the [[Star Dream]] reported nothing similar. Their routes remained close to inhabited systems, their voyages were comparatively short, and their crews maintained constant contact while learning unfamiliar technology.

The first surviving accounts appear after expeditions moved beyond the region now recognized as the [[Settled Systems]]. Separate crews reported the same details:

- knocking from outside the hull;
- relatives visible between stars;
- crew members walking willingly into maintenance locks;
- exterior suits found empty and intact;
- survivors insisting that missing crew remained visible;
- recovered bodies moving without atmosphere.

Several expeditions returned before their symptoms became obvious. Later outbreaks occurred aboard vessels that had never traveled as far into the frontier.

By Lapse 2, long driftless voyages commonly used paired watches, mechanical airlock restrictions, covered viewport periods, and spoken grounding checks. Many of those procedures survive as spacer customs even when the crews following them no longer know where they came from.

Experienced spacers still refuse to answer unexplained knocking on an exterior hull.

## Competing Explanations

Medical authorities usually describe Void Calling as an occultly transmissible neuroperceptual disease activated by prolonged environmental stress.

Star Dream theologians have proposed several explanations:

- a corrupted fragment of the original revelation;
- something that heard the Star Dream and began answering;
- punishment for entering regions the Elect were never shown;
- another population attempting contact through incompatible senses;
- evidence that open space possesses some form of awareness.

Moyishuu traditions generally reject the claim that the disease comes from the Drift. Their inherited dreams distinguish the structured movement of the Drift from the stillness associated with the Calling.

No explanation accounts for every outbreak.

## Void-Returned Archetype

Most people who recover from true Void Calling eventually return to their previous physical baseline. A few do not.

Their medical examinations show no active disease. They cannot spread Void Calling through ordinary proximity, and effects that detect disease find nothing to remove. Some stop breathing for minutes without noticing. Some become unnaturally difficult to kill. Others continue hearing voices through sealed hulls and occasionally recognize the presence of living people before seeing them.

Settled Systems medicine describes these cases as persistent post-exposure anomalies. Among spacers, people who develop them are usually called **Void-Returned**.

Taking this archetype does not mean you remain afflicted with Void Calling. You are still a living creature, and you do not gain the undead trait, void healing, or the ability to transmit Void Calling unless an ability specifically says otherwise.

# GM Information

> [!danger] GM Information  
> Void Calling does not spread through air, blood, tissue, or any organism recognized by Settled Systems medicine. It spreads when one mind begins accepting another afflicted mind's version of the void.
> 
> The hallucinations are assembled from information taken from the victim. A Void-Drawn can imitate voices, private phrases, habits, and memories belonging to people it has never physically met.
> 
> The first post-Star-Dream crews were unaffected because the Calling had not yet learned how to interpret the minds of the Elect. The first frontier victims taught it.
> 
> Planetary atmospheres interfere with the disease because they produce too much environmental noise for it to maintain a stable false reality. Weather, gravity, electromagnetic activity, biological life, chemical circulation, and the sensory presence of a world all work against it.
> 
> Erbium Industries has evidence that some frontier regions produce far more outbreaks than others. Those regions overlap with suppressed survey routes and abandoned extraction claims.
> 
> Erbium has not determined whether the Calling is an organism, an intelligence, a location, a form of communication, or a symptom of something larger. Its research divisions use different internal classifications and do not share all of their findings.
> 
> A Void-Drawn retains pieces of its former identity. These fragments do not prove that the original person remains conscious. Some Void-Drawn have guided rescuers toward survivors. Others have used private memories to lure entire crews into space.
> 
> When creating a Void-Drawn, use a creature of approximately the victim's former level. Add the cosmic and undead traits, darkvision, void healing, the standard undead immunities, and the ability to survive in vacuum. A Void-Drawn should also have an auditory or telepathic ability capable of exposing nearby creatures to Void Calling, Exposure.
> 
> Destroying a Void-Drawn ends its immediate influence. It does not cure anyone already afflicted.
