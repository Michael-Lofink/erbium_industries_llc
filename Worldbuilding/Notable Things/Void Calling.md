> [!summary]
> **Void Calling** sometimes called the Star Plague, or Vacuum Sickness by Settled Systems Code `placeholder` , refers to the phenomena and subsequent effect of prolonged exposure to the place between places, outer space. Those who don't prepare for long voyages past planetary atmospheres are subjected to influences of the mind. Subjects are known to experience deeply personal hallucinations, and a call to leave their ships and enter into the void alone.
```sf2e-stats
# Void Calling, Exposure
## Disease 6
==Disease== ==Mental== ==Occult==

Exposure to the near vacuum of space, such as through traveling by starship, can lead to the victim succumbing to a lesser form of Void Calling. This disease attacks the victim's senses and calls them to go into the deeper void. The hallucinations of the victim build and culminate until they attempt to kill their comrades, or escape into the vacuum of space only to later succumb to the true Void Calling. Creatures with the cosmic trait will never progress beyond stage 1, but the disease gains the [[Virulent|virulent]] trait when infecting a creature within 30 ft of another creature already afflicted with any form of Void Calling. When within a planet's atmosphere, this disease loses the virulent trait if it has it, and you reduce its affliciton stage by 2 on a success, and by 3 on a critical success.

**Saving Throw** DC 20 Will

**Onset** 3 days

**Stage 1** carrier with no effects (1 day)

**Stage 2** your companion's eyes stare at you giving you [[Stupefied|stupefied]] 1 (4 hours)

**Stage 3** as stage 2, and you must make a DC 5 flat check to come within 10 ft of an allied creature (4 hours)

**Stage 4** as stage 3, and when you enter a room, and at least 10 minutes have passed since you last made this check, you must succeed at a Will save against the disease's DC  or be unable to detect any creatures until a creature uses a manipulate or move action within your reach. (1 day)

**Stage 5** as stage 4, but you can no longer detect creatures, and begin hearing and seeing anything that would make you go into space such as someone banging on a viewport screaming for help. (1 hour)



```

# Discovery
Who discovered the Star Plague first is hard to pin-down. The disease seems to pick and choose the most vulnerable on a crew to seep into their mind, before trying to spread to the rest of the crew, until seemingly the isolation onset by the disease leads to mass suicide and mutation into something else. The first crews of humans and dwarves that made contact after the revelation of the Star Dream were unaffected. It wasn't until the exploration into the frontier past what is now the Settled Systems that people began experiencing this phenomena. But whatever it was they found out there was brought back with them, and since Lapse 2, precautions are taken on long drift-less voyages to prepare crew to fight off the Void Calling.
