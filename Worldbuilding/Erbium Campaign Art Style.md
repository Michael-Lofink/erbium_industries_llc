# Corporate Frontier Grit

> [!summary]
> A grounded, low-science-fiction visual style centered on blue-collar extraction work at the edge of settled space. Advanced technology is practical, modular, expensive, and frequently repaired. Corporate branding remains clean and reassuring while workers, equipment, and frontier facilities show the physical cost of expansion.

## Core Visual Direction

**Rendering:** Stylized concept-art realism with clear silhouettes, believable materials, restrained saturation, and moderate surface detail.

**World tone:** Industrial competence, institutional neglect, corporate optimism, labor exploitation, frontier isolation, and restrained cosmic unease.

**Technology:** Advanced without appearing effortless. Machines require fuel, maintenance, replacement parts, operators, cables, seals, vents, and access panels.

**Condition:** Worn but functional. Use chipped paint, dust, patched panels, grease near moving parts, faded labels, replacement components, and repairs made with available materials.

## Visual Pillars

### Industrial Practicality

Every object should communicate its function through visible construction.

- Reinforced corners, handles, hinges, bolts, clamps, vents, and maintenance panels
- Modular components designed for repair and replacement
- Heavy equipment with readable weight and mechanical purpose
- Equipment labels, serial numbers, hazard markings, and inspection tags

### Corporate Reassurance

Erbium Industries presents extraction as orderly, safe, scientific, and necessary.

- Clean geometric branding applied to damaged or dangerous equipment
- Polished recruitment material contrasted with harsh working conditions
- Standardized uniforms, signage, packaging, interfaces, and safety language
- Euphemistic slogans and legal disclaimers

### Frontier Labor

Settlements and worksites are built quickly, expanded unevenly, and maintained under limited budgets.

- Prefabricated structures, cargo modules, gantries, tanks, floodlights, and temporary barriers
- Crowded barracks, utilitarian mess halls, equipment lockers, and improvised repairs
- Workers who appear capable, tired, practical, and visibly employed
- Limited personal decoration inside standardized corporate spaces

### Cosmic Unknown

Unsettled systems contain phenomena that resist easy classification without becoming overt fantasy.

- Strange geology, unusual erosion, unfamiliar biology, and anomalous light behavior
- Sparse visual evidence rather than constant spectacle
- Scientific equipment used to study things it cannot fully explain
- Anomalies should feel physically present and dangerous

## Shape Language

**Erbium Industries:** Faceted polygons, chamfered rectangles, trapezoids, hexagonal structures, inset panels, hard diagonals, and controlled symmetry.

**Frontier infrastructure:** Boxy modules, cylindrical tanks, exposed frames, stacked containers, braces, ladders, gantries, and service platforms.

**Natural environments:** Fractured stone, layered strata, mineral shear planes, irregular caverns, asymmetrical formations, and weathered surfaces.

**Unknown technology or structures:** Precise but unfamiliar geometry, limited ornament, ambiguous function, and forms that do not resemble conventional human manufacturing.

## Materials and Surfaces

Primary materials:

- Painted steel and dull aluminum
- Ceramic composite panels
- Matte polymers and rubber seals
- Reinforced glass and scratched visors
- Canvas, webbing, pressure fabric, and insulated padding
- Carbon-black equipment housings

Preferred wear:

- Scratched edges
- Chipped paint
- Dust collected in seams
- Heat discoloration
- Oil residue around mechanical joints
- Faded stencils
- Mismatched replacement panels

Avoid universal rust, excessive grime, mirror chrome, seamless plastics, and pristine showroom finishes.

## Color Palette

### Erbium Industries

- Dark charcoal
- Gunmetal
- Cold silver
- Pale gray
- Muted erbium-magenta or violet-pink

Magenta is a controlled identification color used for logos, interface highlights, uniform piping, division markings, equipment labels, and status indicators.

### Frontier Environments

- Weathered off-white
- Gray-blue steel
- Faded industrial yellow
- Dusty orange-brown
- Olive drab
- Dirty beige
- Desaturated hazard red

### Anomalies

Use sparingly:

- Pale cyan
- Deep indigo
- Muted ultraviolet
- Spectral green
- Pearlescent white
- Dim amber

Avoid saturated rainbow lighting and dominant cyberpunk neon.

## Lighting

Use practical sources:

- Industrial floodlights
- Overhead work strips
- Vehicle lamps
- Helmet and shoulder lights
- Emergency beacons
- Sodium-yellow facility lighting
- Cold planetary or stellar light
- Equipment screens and status indicators

Lighting should reveal dust, depth, machinery, damage, and working conditions. Corporate interiors may be cleaner and more evenly illuminated than frontier installations.

## Character Design

Workers should resemble miners, technicians, surveyors, pilots, logistics personnel, security contractors, and maintenance crews.

Common elements:

- Utility jumpsuits and layered pressure clothing
- Reinforced boots and gloves
- Tool harnesses and tether points
- ID patches, serial markings, and company insignia
- Practical helmets, lamps, air systems, and equipment packs
- Visible wear, repairs, and personalized adjustments

Corporate officials use cleaner materials, better tailoring, fewer tools, and more controlled branding. Security equipment should feel commercially manufactured and workmanlike rather than ceremonial.

## Erbium Industries Brand Rules

The Erbium emblem is the cleanest visual element in the setting.

- Use charcoal, pale gray, silver, and muted magenta
- Maintain faceted mineral geometry
- Use the **Er** monogram and **68** designation consistently
- Apply the detailed logo to recruitment material, headquarters, and formal displays
- Apply the simplified emblem to uniforms, machinery, crates, helmets, doors, and ship hulls
- Keep typography bold, technical, geometric, and highly legible

The anti-corporate tone comes from where the branding appears: malfunctioning equipment, leased survival tools, hazardous worksites, employee contracts, and memorial records.

## Avoid

- Sleek utopian futurism
- Excessive cyberpunk neon
- Cartoon proportions
- Retro-futurist comedy as the default tone
- Fantasy crystals and magical visual effects
- Decorative technology without a visible function
- Universally filthy or ruined environments
- Smooth luxury spacecraft used as ordinary work vehicles
- Generic military styling for all corporate personnel
