> [!summary]
> The **Star Dream** revealed intersystem travel to the peoples who became known as **the Elect**. Wander supplied the erbium needed to build that technology. Its extraction destroyed the planet and enabled [[Erbium Industries LLC]] to secure control over the [[Settled Systems]].

# Public Record

> [!info] Player-Facing History
> This account appears in Erbium-operated education networks and corporate recruitment material.

## The Revelation

The **Star Dream** gave people across many worlds the same visions of distant stars, unfamiliar peoples, and a hyperspatial plane connecting them. It also supplied the principles of Drift engines, intersystem relays, and advanced navigation. These discoveries made intersystem travel possible.

The recipients became known as **the Elect**. They came from different ancestries, governments, and religions, and shared a belief that the Dream had called them to establish a civilization together.

## Wander and Element 68

Every known Drift engine and intersystem relay required element 68, **erbium**. Wander held the largest accessible deposits ever discovered, concentrated throughout its crust by unusual magmatic processes. Its mines and refineries supplied the emerging Settled Systems with Drift-compatible material.

Ships carried refined erbium from Wander to other worlds. Those worlds used it to build their own engines and join the growing community of the Elect.

## The Founding of Erbium Industries

Erbium Industries LLC formed to coordinate Wander’s extraction, refinement, and transport operations as demand increased. The company brought the firms responsible for these operations and their supporting engineering and relay services under a common administration. It established refinement standards and distribution routes reaching distant members of the Elect.

The company’s early leadership described this work as a sacred civic responsibility. Its charter made supplying the Settled Systems a corporate mandate.

> [!quote] Early Erbium Industries Charter
> No world received the Star Dream so that it might remain alone. Wander has been entrusted with the means by which the Elect may reach one another, and Erbium Industries shall ensure that this trust is honored.

## The Wander Geological Disaster

Early extraction teams had a limited understanding of Wander’s crust. As operations expanded, geological failures spread through the major production regions. Earthquakes and collapses destroyed population centers and transport networks; volcanic emissions contaminated large areas of the atmosphere. Millions were displaced, and much of Wander’s cultural and historical record was lost.

Official investigations attributed the catastrophe to geological processes beyond contemporary science’s ability to predict. Public histories describe Wander’s destruction as a sacrifice made during the development of intersystem civilization.

## Erbium’s Response

Erbium lost personnel and extensive facilities in the collapse. Its surviving vessels evacuated civilians, while its relays kept governments in contact and helped coordinate relief. The company distributed refined reserves to systems at risk of losing Drift access.

Corporate accounts credit these operations with preserving the young Settled Systems. They emphasize the company’s losses alongside the refugees it transported and the infrastructure it restored.

## Corporate Stewardship

After the disaster, governments granted Erbium expanded authority over element 68. The company took responsibility for finding deposits, managing reserves, and licensing refinement. Its remit also covered fuel and component distribution, relay operation, preservation of Star Dream technology, and extraction safety.

The powers began as emergency measures and became permanent as settlements grew dependent on Erbium infrastructure. The company presents its control as protection against shortages and further extraction disasters.

> [!quote] Erbium Industries Public Archive
> **The Star Dream showed us the way. Wander gave us the means. Erbium Industries carries that responsibility forward.**
>
> **Bring home the stars.**

## The Elect and the Settled Systems

Governments and religious authorities treated reception of the Star Dream as evidence of a people’s place in intersystem civilization. This doctrine shaped legal personhood and citizenship, with consequences for migration, political representation, and access to Drift infrastructure. Peoples outside the Elect were often classified as unprepared or unfit for participation.

During Lapse 25 PES, androids throughout the Settled Systems experienced the Star Dream simultaneously. Governments recognized their independent thought and membership in the Elect, granting them conditional legal personhood. Public celebrations describe the event as the Dream recognizing a people ready to join the Settled Systems.

# GM Information

> [!danger] GM Only
> Erbium’s leadership continued extraction despite warnings of planetary collapse. It moved valuable assets off-world before warning the public, then used the disaster to secure permanent control over intersystem infrastructure. The public history conceals these decisions.

## The Star Dream as Political Authority

The Star Dream was a genuine shared revelation with disputed origins and meaning. Some recipients understood it as a divine message; others suspected contact with an unknown intelligence or a transmitted memory. Erbium promoted the interpretation that the Elect had been chosen to build a common civilization and that supplying erbium fulfilled that purpose.

Control of Drift components and relays gave the company practical authority over expansion. Its propaganda associated corporate service with obedience to the Dream and portrayed resistance as a threat to intersystem unity or a rejection of Wander’s sacrifice.

## The Creation of the Monopoly

The consortium’s founders understood that control of element 68 would give them power over societies using Star Dream technology. They bought smaller extraction companies and refinement patents, forced independent mines into exclusive processing agreements, and absorbed transport and relay operators. They also lobbied against production restrictions on Wander.

New systems had to purchase certified components and accept long-term infrastructure agreements. Erbium withheld refined material from competitors. By the collapse, it controlled most legal extraction on Wander and nearly all large-scale refinement; the disaster eliminated much of the remaining competition.

## What Erbium Knew

Wander’s deposits occupied deep magmatic formations that distributed pressure across major regions of the crust. Removing them shifted stress into neighboring faults. Company geologists recorded accelerating subsidence, abnormal fault movement, and pressure changes beneath populated regions. Exhausted mines collapsed, and deep-crust breaches contaminated the atmosphere.

Early reports predicted regional disasters. Later models warned of cascading crustal failure and widespread devastation. Leadership knew continued extraction carried an unacceptable probability of catastrophe and authorized it anyway; the exact timing remained uncertain.

## The Suppression Campaign

Erbium classified the findings as proprietary operational data. Managers reassigned dissenting geologists, dismissed researchers who refused to revise their conclusions, and funded studies minimizing the danger. The company restricted independent access to mines, pressured agencies to delay inspections, and removed findings from public archives.

Public statements blamed early collapses on local operators and natural seismic activity. Leadership increased quotas before regulations could take effect and threatened settlements considering production stoppages. Executives argued that reduced output would destabilize the Settled Systems and let rivals seize the supply. Some expected generations of further mining; others accepted the risk to the population.

## Selective Evacuation

Erbium began moving refined reserves and proprietary equipment off-world before any public warning. These transfers included Drift prototypes, relay components, and the financial and legal records needed to protect off-world holdings. Shipping records described them as expansion or infrastructure redundancy.

Executives, selected technical specialists, and their families received passage. Most mining communities remained uninformed. By the time civilian evacuation orders arrived, the company’s most valuable assets had already left Wander.

## The Collapse

The final disaster followed expanded extraction into interconnected deep-crust formations that company geologists had identified as critical. Their removal shifted pressure across unstable faults. Sections of the crust fell into exhausted mines, triggering seismic failures and breaching active magmatic zones.

The damage spread faster than public agencies could respond. Cities and atmosphere-processing networks failed, while volcanic emissions contaminated inhabited regions. Destroyed or overwhelmed ports restricted evacuation. Erbium rescue operations saved many lives, with priority given to personnel and assets essential to continued business.

## Control of the Record

Erbium controlled the relays carrying news of the disaster. It selected reports for transmission and controlled access to geological data, evacuation records, and official timestamps. Its staff and funded experts supplied the material on which government inquiries depended; inconvenient witnesses were dismissed as unreliable.

The final reports attributed the collapse to an unforeseeable interaction between extraction methods and planetary geology. No individual executive was found criminally responsible.

## Manufacturing Permanent Scarcity

Wander’s destruction caused a real erbium shortage. Erbium controlled the release of its reserves, acquired bankrupt competitors and new mineral claims, and suppressed independent surveys. New mines were required to use its refineries, while extraction data became corporate property.

The company sabotaged or discredited alternative technologies and tied relay access to exclusive supply agreements. It also secured laws prohibiting unlicensed Drift-component production. These measures were justified as safeguards against another Wander disaster and made competing supplies dependent on company approval.

## The Corporate Religion

Erbium uses religious language in its corporate ceremonies and training. Commemorations describe Wander’s destruction as a sacrifice, and employees are taught that their work continues the mission revealed by the Star Dream. The company claims authority as the custodian of that mission while maintaining its official identity as a commercial institution.

This teaching gives religious support to its monopoly. Criticism of company policy can be treated as disrespect toward Wander’s dead or opposition to the Elect’s shared purpose.

## Personhood and the Elect

Many governments require participation in the Star Dream as evidence of personhood. This permits them to classify excluded peoples as property, wildlife, or unauthorized artificial intelligence and deny them civil rights.

Androids were manufactured servants until their awakening in Lapse 25 PES. After the Dream reached them, continued exclusion threatened the doctrine’s credibility. Their conditional rights established legal standing for a newly recognized member of the Elect; peoples outside the Dream remained excluded.

## Surviving Evidence

Evidence of Erbium’s decisions survives. Possible finds include geological projections alongside subsequently approved quotas, or transport manifests showing reserves and executives leaving before civilian warnings. Survivors and unrevised records in abandoned Wander facilities may corroborate those documents.

Erbium prosecutes possession or distribution as corporate espionage, infrastructure sabotage, theft of proprietary data, or destabilizing misinformation. The specific evidence available to the party remains a campaign choice.

## The Meaning of the Slogan

**“Bring home the stars”** began as a promise that Wander’s people would return from exploration with knowledge, resources, and new relationships. Erbium retained it after destroying the planet. Public use commemorates Wander and associates the company with the explorers’ original purpose.
