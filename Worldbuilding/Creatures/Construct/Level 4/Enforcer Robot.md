# Enforcer Robot

These sub-sapient robots staff checkpoints and gates and serve as expendable law enforcement throughout the Settled Systems. Cheap to manufacture and deadly in practice, each robot carries a copy of the Settled Systems Code Archive and directives to enforce intersystem law.
