```sf2e-stats
#### Big Wet Eye Blink `pf2:r` 
| ==Emotion== ==Mental== ==Visual==
**Source** [Galactic Ancestries pg. 95](https://2e.aonsrd.com/sources/28-galactic-ancestries)
**Frequency** once per day
**Trigger** A creature you're aware of critically succeeds on a Strike against you and would deal damage to you
---
 You blink your big wet eyes at the triggering creature, forcing it to pull back on its attack. The attacking creature takes a circumstance penalty to the damage of the triggering Strike equal to your level + 2. This penalty applies after doubling the damage for a critical hit. The attacker is then immune to Big Wet Eye Blink for 24 hours. 
```
