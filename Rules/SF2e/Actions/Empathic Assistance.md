```sf2e-stats
#### Empathic Assistance `pf2:1` 
| ==Emotion== ==Mental==
**Source** [Galactic Ancestries pg. 94](https://2e.aonsrd.com/sources/28-galactic-ancestries)
**Requirements** You are adjacent to a willing member of your pod who is currently frightened or affected by a harmful effect with the emotion or mental trait
---
You hold your affected pod member's hand to comfort them. If they are frightened, reduce their frightened condition by 1. If they are under the effect of an ongoing emotion or mental effect, attempt a counteract check against the triggering effect, using half your level rounded up as your counteract rank and your Perception modifier as the counteract modifier. On a success, you gain the effect for its remaining duration, and that pod member becomes immune to Empathetic Assistance for 1 hour. 
```
