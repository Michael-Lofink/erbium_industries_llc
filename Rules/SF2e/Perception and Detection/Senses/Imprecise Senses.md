**Source** [Player Core pg. 424](https://2e.aonsrd.com/sources/2-player-core)

Average hearing is an imprecise sense—it can't detect the full range of detail that a precise sense can. You can usually sense a creature automatically with an imprecise sense, but it has the [hidden](https://2e.aonsrd.com/conditions/22-hidden) condition instead of the [observed](https://2e.aonsrd.com/conditions/27-observed) condition. It might be undetected by you if it's using [Stealth](https://2e.aonsrd.com/skills/17-stealth) or in an environment that distorts the sense, such as a noisy room in the case of hearing. In those cases, you have to use the [Seek](https://2e.aonsrd.com/actions/10-seek) basic action to detect the creature. At best, an imprecise sense can be used to make an [undetected](https://2e.aonsrd.com/conditions/41-undetected) creature (or one you didn't even know was there) merely hidden—it can't make the creature observed.
