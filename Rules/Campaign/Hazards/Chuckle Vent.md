```sf2e-stats
# Chuckle Vent
## Hazard 1

==Electricity== ==Environmental==

**Source** Homebrew
**Complexity** Simple
**Stealth** 14
**Description** A crescent of pale mineral crust covers a pressurized fissure. Before an eruption, dust trembles, nearby comms crackle, and blue sparks crawl across the stones.

---

**Disable** DC 14 Survival (trained) to observe the vent from outside its eruption area for 1 minute and identify a safe lull, allowing the group to cross safely; or DC 17 Crafting (trained) to discharge the vent for 10 minutes using repair tools and conductive scrap (2 actions, performed adjacent to the crust). A failed check has no effect; a critically failed Crafting check triggers Eruption.

**Eruption `[reaction]`** **Trigger** A creature steps on the crust within 5 feet of the fissure, or the charged crust is broken or forcefully disturbed; **Effect** Gas and electrically charged grit erupt in a 10-foot burst centered on the fissure. Each creature in the area attempts a DC 17 Reflex save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.
**Success** The creature takes half of 2d6 electricity damage.
**Failure** The creature takes 2d6 electricity damage.
**Critical Failure** The creature takes double damage.

---

**Reset** The vent recharges after 10 minutes. Until then, it can be crossed safely. Breaking the crust remotely triggers the eruption normally. Ordinary attacks cannot destroy the underground vent.
```
