Your spacesuits have basic comm units built in, allowing you to communicate at planetary range (see the armor section of the tech trait and the first sentence of Comm Unit). I’m allowing brief, in-character communication without spending actions, provided it doesn’t produce a mechanical effect that normally requires an action. Call out a threat, ask for help, suggest a plan, or coordinate with each other.
All our social contract agreements about advising someone during their turn still apply. Whoever’s turn it is has the spotlight and makes their own decisions. Give them room to think and act, and keep suggestions brief. If the conversation starts dragging turns out, I reserve the right to say “stop the yap” and move things along.
The goal is to give your characters room to work together and build camaraderie. If you spot something useful that another character might miss, your character can point it out over comms. You’re allowed to help each other figure things out! DO NOT ABUSE THIS PLEASE
