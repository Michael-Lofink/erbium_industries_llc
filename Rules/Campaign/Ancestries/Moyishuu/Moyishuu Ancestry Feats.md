```sf2e-stats

| ==Moyishuu==
**Source** Adapted from [Galactic Ancestries pg. 150](https://2e.aonsrd.com/sources/28-galactic-ancestries)

---

Choose one cantrip from your ancestry's magic entry. You can cast that cantrip at will. A cantrip is heightened to a spell rank equal to half your level rounded up. This spell has the same tradition as listed in the feat entry.

---
**Options**: occult; *light*, *seek the stars*


#### Ancestral Darkvision <small class=sf2e-level>Feat 1</small>
| ==Moyishuu==
**Source** Adapted from [Galactic Ancestries pg. 150](https://2e.aonsrd.com/sources/28-galactic-ancestries)

---

You gain [darkvision](https://2e.aonsrd.com/rules/459-darkvision-and-greater-darkvision).


#### Ancestral Skills <small class=sf2e-level>Feat 1</small>
| ==Moyishuu==
**Source** Adapted from [Galactic Ancestries pg. 150](https://2e.aonsrd.com/sources/28-galactic-ancestries)

---

You have a keen interest in the origins of your people. You become trained in the skills listed in your ancestry's skills entry. If you would automatically become trained in one of those skills (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice.

You also gain the [Additional Lore](https://2e.aonsrd.com/feats/755-additional-lore) general feat for the [Lore](https://2e.aonsrd.com/skills/9-lore) skill associated with your ancestry (for example, androids have Android Lore and ysoki have Ysoki Lore).

---

**Options**: Piloting, Performance


#### Ancestral Weapon Familiarity <small class=sf2e-level>Feat 1</small>
| ==Moyishuu==
**Source** [Galactic Ancestries pg. 150](https://2e.aonsrd.com/sources/28-galactic-ancestries)

---

You gain access to all uncommon weapons with your ancestry trait. You have familiarity with weapons with your ancestry trait and with ancestral weapons. For the purposes of proficiency, you treat any of these that are martial weapons as simple weapons and any that are advanced weapons as martial weapons.  
At 5th level, whenever you land a critical hit with one of these weapons, you can add its critical specialization effect.

---

**Options**: [nano-edge rapier](https://2e.aonsrd.com/equipment/weapons/17-nano-edge-rapier), [singing coil](https://2e.aonsrd.com/equipment/weapons/61-singing-coil), [singing spear](https://2e.aonsrd.com/equipment/weapons/28-singing-spear)


#### Enhanced Senses <small class=sf2e-level>Feat 1</small>
| ==Moyishuu==
**Source** Adapted from [Galactic Ancestries pg. 150](https://2e.aonsrd.com/sources/28-galactic-ancestries)
**Prerequisites** a sense with a listed range

---

Your natural senses improve with careful practice or through the use of specific techniques. Increase the range of one of your senses by 10 feet if it's a [precise sense](https://2e.aonsrd.com/rules/455-precise-senses) or by 30 feet if it's an [imprecise sense](https://2e.aonsrd.com/rules/456-imprecise-senses).

---
**Options**: cosmicsense


#### Raise Crystalline Strands `pf2:r` <small class=sf2e-level>Feat 1</small>
| ==Moyishuu==
**Source** [Galactic Ancestries pg. 153](https://2e.aonsrd.com/sources/28-galactic-ancestries)
**Frequency** once per day
**Trigger** An enemy's Strike against you is a critical hit.

---

You position your fortified crystalline hair to absorb some of the blow from an otherwise lethal attack. The triggering attack deals only the amount it would deal on a hit, typically full damage in place of double damage. Any other effects caused by a critical hit still occur.

It takes a day for your shattered hair to regrow. Until it does, you take a –2 circumstance penalty to Diplomacy checks with other moyishuu.


#### First Dream Communion <small class=sf2e-level>Feat 1</small>
| ==Moyishuu==
**Source** Adapted from Super Soul!, [Galactic Ancestries pg. 153](https://2e.aonsrd.com/sources/28-galactic-ancestries)

---

Patterns carried from the First Dream allow your thoughts to cross the distance between separate minds. You gain limited telepathy.

You can communicate mentally with creatures within 30 feet. You can communicate only with creatures that share a language with you. This doesn't give you access to their thoughts, and it communicates no more information than normal speech would.


#### Uncanny Wonder `pf2:r` <small class=sf2e-level>Feat 1</small>
| ==Emotion== ==Moyishuu==
**Source** Adapted from [Galactic Ancestries pg. 154](https://2e.aonsrd.com/sources/28-galactic-ancestries)
**Frequency** once per 10 minutes
**Trigger** An enemy critically fails a melee Strike against you.

---

Your unusual timing and weightless movement leave your assailant unable to follow the exchange. The triggering creature is off-guard to the next Strike made against it by one of your allies before the start of your next turn.

---
**Special** The frequency of Uncanny Wonder is once per minute while you are in space or within the Drift.


#### Unfathomable Sorrow `pf2:r` <small class=sf2e-level>Feat 1</small>
| ==Emotion== ==Mental== ==Moyishuu==
**Source** Adapted from [Galactic Ancestries pg. 154](https://2e.aonsrd.com/sources/28-galactic-ancestries)
**Frequency** once per 10 minutes
**Trigger** An enemy causes you to become frightened or take persistent mental damage.

---

The weight of lives, voyages, and memories carried through the Second Dream rebounds against the source of your distress. The triggering creature becomes frightened 1. That creature can't reduce its frightened condition below 1 until the beginning of your next turn.

---
**Special** The frequency of Unfathomable Sorrow is once per minute while you are in space or within the Drift.


#### Unforgiving Fury `pf2:r` <small class=sf2e-level>Feat 1</small>
| ==Emotion== ==Moyishuu==
**Source** Adapted from [Galactic Ancestries pg. 154](https://2e.aonsrd.com/sources/28-galactic-ancestries)
**Frequency** once per 10 minutes
**Trigger** You critically succeed at a Strike.

---

Decisive action reinforces your determination and momentarily stabilizes your cosmic body. You gain temporary Hit Points equal to your Charisma modifier until the end of your next turn.

---
**Special** The frequency of Unforgiving Fury is once per minute while you are in space or within the Drift.

#### Unrelenting Joy `pf2:r` <small class=sf2e-level>Feat 1</small>
| ==Emotion== ==Moyishuu==
**Source** Adapted from [Galactic Ancestries pg. 154](https://2e.aonsrd.com/sources/28-galactic-ancestries)
**Frequency** once per 10 minutes
**Trigger** You're frightened or taking persistent mental damage.

---

The Second Dream remains full of unexplored possibilities. Reduce your frightened condition by 1. If you're taking persistent mental damage, immediately attempt a DC 15 flat check to recover from that persistent damage.

---
**Special** The frequency of Unrelenting Joy is once per minute while you are in space or within the Drift.


#### Read the Rhythm `pf2:1` <small class=sf2e-level>Feat 5</small>
| ==Auditory== ==Concentrate== ==Mental== ==Moyishuu==
**Source** Adapted from Absolute Empathy, [Galactic Ancestries pg. 154](https://2e.aonsrd.com/sources/28-galactic-ancestries)
**Prerequisites** trained in Diplomacy

---

You study a creature's cadence, posture, attention, and expectations, then misdirect it with a precisely timed shift in your own performance. Attempt to Feint against a creature. Use Diplomacy for this check in place of Deception, and compare your result to the creature's Will DC in place of its Perception DC.


#### Ancestral Magic <small class=sf2e-level>Feat 5</small>
| ==Moyishuu==
**Source** Adapted from [Galactic Ancestries pg. 150](https://2e.aonsrd.com/sources/28-galactic-ancestries)

---

Your ancestry has a powerful connection to one or more magical traditions.  
Choose one 1st-rank spell from your ancestry's magic entry. You can cast that spell as an innate spell once per day as a 1st-rank spell. If this is your 9th level ancestry feat, choose a 3rd-rank or lower ancestry spell as a 3rd-rank innate spell. If this is your 13th level ancestry feat, choose a 5th-rank or lower ancestry spell as a 5th-rank innate spell. If this is your 17th level ancestry feat, choose a 7th-rank or lower ancestry spell as a 7th-rank innate spell. This spell has the same tradition that is listed in the feat entry.

---

**Options**: occult; 1st *dream of home*, *mindlink*, *wisp ally*; 2nd *augury*, *translate*; 4th *flicker*, *translocate*; 5th *dreaming potential*; 6th *teleport*; 7th *interplanar teleport*, *retrocognition*

---

**Special** If you have multiple spells listed for your ancestry, you can select this feat more than once. If you do, you must select a different spell.


#### First Dream Recognition <small class=sf2e-level>Feat 5</small>
| ==Moyishuu==
**Source** Adapted from Mother Tongue, [Galactic Ancestries pg. 154](https://2e.aonsrd.com/sources/28-galactic-ancestries)

---

You recognize patterns shared by beings shaped by cosmic matter or the Drift. Whenever you meet a creature with the cosmic or drift trait in a social situation, you can immediately attempt a Diplomacy check to Make an Impression on that creature without first conversing for 1 minute. You take a –4 circumstance penalty to this check.

If you fail, you can converse with the creature for 1 minute and attempt a new check at the end of that time. When you attempt this second check, ignore the failure or critical failure from your first attempt.

You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to Perception checks and saving throws against creatures with the cosmic or drift trait.


#### Sonic Sync <small class=sf2e-level>Feat 5</small>
| ==Moyishuu==
**Source** Adapted from [Galactic Ancestries pg. 154](https://2e.aonsrd.com/sources/28-galactic-ancestries)

---

Your living crystalline hair distributes destructive vibrations across thousands of interlocking mineral strands. You gain resistance 5 to sonic damage.


#### Second Dream Improvisation <small class=sf2e-level>Feat 5</small>
| ==Moyishuu==
**Source** Replaces Wild Soulfeel, [Galactic Ancestries pg. 154](https://2e.aonsrd.com/sources/28-galactic-ancestries)

---

Your identity continues to develop through the experiences and disciplines you practice. During your daily preparations, choose Uncanny Wonder, Unfathomable Sorrow, Unforgiving Fury, or Unrelenting Joy. You gain the chosen feat until your next daily preparations.

You must choose a feat you don't already have. You can choose a different feat each time you make your daily preparations.

#### Second Dream Anchor `pf2:2` <small class=sf2e-level>Feat 9</small>
| ==Concentrate== ==Emotion== ==Mental== ==Moyishuu==
**Source** Adapted from Absorb Vibe, [Galactic Ancestries pg. 155](https://2e.aonsrd.com/sources/28-galactic-ancestries)
**Frequency** once per 10 minutes
**Range** touch
**Targets** 1 willing creature

---

You steady another person by grounding them in a specific memory, relationship, or experience that belongs to their present life.

Attempt to counteract one harmful emotion effect affecting the target. Use your Perception modifier for the counteract check and a counteract rank equal to half your level rounded up.

If you succeed, the target gains temporary Hit Points equal to your level. These temporary Hit Points last for a number of rounds equal to your Charisma modifier, with a minimum duration of 1 round.

---
***SFS Note*** Harmful effects that negatively affect a creature's emotions include an emotion effect affecting the creature or one of the following conditions: frightened or fleeing.


#### First Dream Awareness <small class=sf2e-level>Feat 9</small>
| ==Moyishuu==
**Source** Adapted from Grand Soul!, [Galactic Ancestries pg. 155](https://2e.aonsrd.com/sources/28-galactic-ancestries)
**Prerequisites** First Dream Communion

---

Your awareness reaches deeper into the cosmic patterns that preceded your embodied life. Your cosmicsense becomes a precise sense, and its range increases by 10 feet. This increase is cumulative with Enhanced Senses.

The range of the limited telepathy granted by First Dream Communion increases to the range of your cosmicsense.


#### Dream-Bound Companion <small class=sf2e-level>Feat 9</small>
| ==Moyishuu==
**Source** Adapted from Mascot Power!, [Galactic Ancestries pg. 155](https://2e.aonsrd.com/sources/28-galactic-ancestries)

---

A creature recorded in an inherited dream, constellation story, or childhood memory has begun answering your call. When you select this feat, choose *summon animal* or *summon fey*.

You can cast the chosen spell as a primal innate spell once per day. The spell is heightened to a spell rank equal to half your level rounded up.


#### Center the Second Dream `pf2:1` <small class=sf2e-level>Feat 13</small>
| ==Concentrate== ==Emotion== ==Moyishuu==
**Source** Replaces Center Your Emotions, [Galactic Ancestries pg. 155](https://2e.aonsrd.com/sources/28-galactic-ancestries)
**Frequency** once per 10 minutes
**Requirements** You have used Uncanny Wonder, Unfathomable Sorrow, Unforgiving Fury, or Unrelenting Joy, and the feat's frequency has not reset.

---

You pause and reaffirm the specific person you have become. Choose one of the qualifying feats. The frequency of the chosen feat immediately resets.

---
**Special** You can Center the Second Dream as a free action when the chosen feat corresponds to your heritage:

- **Court of Dance:** Unrelenting Joy


- **Court of Fields:** Uncanny Wonder


- **Court of Flames:** Unforgiving Fury


- **Court of Song:** Unfathomable Sorrow

  
  
#### Carry the Rhythm <small class=sf2e-level>Feat 13</small>
| ==Moyishuu==
**Source** Adapted from Empathic Conduit, [Galactic Ancestries pg. 155](https://2e.aonsrd.com/sources/28-galactic-ancestries)
**Prerequisites** Read the Rhythm

---

Your timing, voice, and body language carry across a battlefield with startling clarity. When you Feint using Read the Rhythm, you can do so at a range of up to 60 feet in place of only within melee reach.

If you succeed, the target is off-guard against both your melee and ranged attacks.


#### Second Dream Mastery <small class=sf2e-level>Feat 13</small>
| ==Moyishuu==
**Source** Replaces Soulfire, [Galactic Ancestries pg. 155](https://2e.aonsrd.com/sources/28-galactic-ancestries)

---

Your studies of the Four Courts allow you to emphasize a particular expression of your embodied life. During your daily preparations, choose one of the following Courts. You gain its benefit until your next daily preparations.

**Court of Dance** Your fly Speed increases by 10 feet.

**Court of Fields** You gain a burrow Speed of 10 feet. You can Burrow through earth, dust, sand, and similar loose material, though you can't Burrow through solid stone or metal.

**Court of Flames** You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to Athletics checks.

**Court of Song** You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to Perception checks.

---
**Special** If you have Dream-Bound Companion, a minion you summon using that feat gains the same benefit you selected.


#### Four-Court Eruption <small class=sf2e-level>Feat 13</small>
| ==Moyishuu==
**Source** Replaces Vibe Eruption, [Galactic Ancestries pg. 155](https://2e.aonsrd.com/sources/28-galactic-ancestries)

---

You can channel the emotional disciplines represented by all four Moyishuu Courts. You can cast *vibe check* as a 4th-rank occult innate spell four times per day.

Each time you Cast the Spell using this feat, choose fury, joy, sorrow, or wonder. Once you choose an option, you can't choose that option again until your next daily preparations.


#### Song of the Second Dream `pf2:2` <small class=sf2e-level>Feat 17</small>
| ==Moyishuu== ==Occult==
**Source** Replaces Song of Whimsy, [Galactic Ancestries pg. 155](https://2e.aonsrd.com/sources/28-galactic-ancestries)
**Frequency** once per day

---

Your voice, movement, crystalline resonance, or chosen form of performance opens the boundary between your finite life and the experiences carried from the First Dream. Roll 1d20 and consult the table below.

If an effect requires a saving throw, use the higher of your class DC or spell DC.

| d20 | Court | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | **Court of Flames** | All enemies within 30 feet must attempt a Will save. On a failure, a creature is confused for 3 rounds, or for 1 minute on a critical failure. While confused this way, its Strikes against its allies deal an additional 3d4 persistent bleed damage. |
| 4–6 | **Court of Dance** | You and all allies within 30 feet reduce your frightened conditions by 1, immediately end any persistent mental damage affecting you, and gain 25 temporary Hit Points until the end of your next turn. |
| 7–9 | **Court of Song** | All enemies within 30 feet become suppressed until the end of your next turn and must attempt a Will save. On a failure, a creature is slowed 1 for 3 rounds, or for 1 minute on a critical failure. |
| 10–12 | **Court of Fields** | All enemies within 30 feet become dazzled until the end of your next turn and must attempt a Will save. On a failure, a creature is stupefied 2 for 3 rounds, or for 1 minute on a critical failure. |
| 13–20 | **Chosen Court** | Select any one of the other four effects. |
```
