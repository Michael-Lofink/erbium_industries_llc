# Telepath Archetype Design

Design for [[Rules/Campaign/Archetypes/Telepath|Telepath]], adapted from [Eyeswide Detective](https://2e.aonsrd.com/archetypes/16-eyeswide-detective). The GM requested a free level-2 dedication for Ponderer, exemption from its dedication lock, a progression supporting a potential level-20 campaign, and a mechanics-first pass followed by setting flavor.

## Grant and advancement

Ponderer receives Telepath Dedication at level 2 as a bonus feat. This award imposes no requirement to take two more Telepath feats before selecting another dedication. He retains his ordinary level-2 class feat and can spend it on a different dedication whose prerequisites he meets. An existing restriction from another archetype does not prevent receiving this bonus award, but its normal restrictions still apply to future chosen dedications.

Only the dedication is awarded. This design does not add the full Free Archetype variant. Later Telepath feats cost normal class-feat selections unless the GM explicitly awards additional feats. None of the Telepath feats has the skill trait; Skill Paragon therefore supplies no extra Telepath selections.

The general archetype keeps the standard dedication restriction. Ponderer's award has a written individual exception in [[Campaign/Party/Jonah/Ponderer Telepath Advancement|his advancement note]].

## Power architecture

| Levels | Capability | Principal limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | Silent conversation and deliberate sharing of a brief impression | Shared language, 30 feet, willing disclosure |
| 4–6 | Better perception, a defensive reaction, brief hostile interference, a small group channel | Actions, reactions, short range, target immunity |
| 8–10 | Surface thoughts, communication across languages, a prepared attack, borrowed senses | Brief information, limited frequency, opportunity cost, consent for sensory access |
| 12–14 | A specific memory, anticipating an ambush, sensing minds, stronger shielding | Higher-level minds resist deep intrusion; senses require line of effect |
| 16–18 | Persistent long-distance communication and emergency protection of a linked group | Existing willing links; communication does not extend ability ranges |
| 20 | A temporary group state with precise mental awareness and modest defenses | Once per day, one minute, local range, no access to campaign-end mysteries |

The archetype has communication, perception, protection, and prediction. It grants no general spell slots, focus pool, universal truth detection, permanent control, teleportation, or automatic access to UNITY. Ponderer's personal cosmological significance remains in [[Campaign/GM Notes/Continuity/Annabelle the Disc and UNITY Brainstorm|the private brainstorming note]].

### DC progression

Use a dedicated telepath DC with a mental attribute chosen at dedication. Proficiency is trained initially, expert at level 9, and master at level 17. This supports a noncaster through level 20 without requiring a second spellcasting archetype. It improves only Telepath abilities, never the character's class DC, unrelated spells, or Ponder Unity. It does not grant spellcasting prerequisites or item access.

For a character starting with a +4 chosen attribute and raising it to +5 at level 10 and +6 at level 20, without an apex item:

This is an explicit homebrew progression. Published Eyeswide abilities use existing DCs or innate spellcasting. Full casters can develop superior proficiency, while the archetype's strongest effects remain limited by frequency, prerequisites, or duration.

## Source adaptation

Eyeswide Detective supplies the broad progression: communication, perceptive investigation, psychic interference, thought reading, attack prediction, ambush defense, and a later investigative insight. Its agency membership and Society training are replaced with a general psychic entry. The new progression adds consent-based links, shielding, and late-game options. The mystery-solving insight becomes mental detection, leaving investigation grounded in evidence the character can acquire.

The implementation is homebrew, not a renamed copy of the published archetype. See [Eyeswide Detective](https://2e.aonsrd.com/archetypes/16-eyeswide-detective), Starfinder Absalom Station pp. 132–133, for its original mechanics.

### Specific balance decisions

- **Dedication:** limited telepathy, trained Occultism, and a voluntary impression exchange. The source's expert Society and Streetwise package is removed. A single bonus feat provides useful expression without a combat attack or hostile mind reading at will.
- **Attuned Perception:** preserves the source's +2 circumstance scale, limited to mentally susceptible creatures in range and line of effect. It assists existing checks and does not reveal truth automatically.
- **Psychic Interruption:** keeps a Will-save combat role, with a shorter reaction-denial window. The target can spend an action to end stupefied. Every attempted target receives one hour of immunity. No stacked condition values and no repeated boss lock.
- **Read Thoughts:** permits a short snapshot once per 10 minutes with one-hour target immunity. Compared with the daily [mind reading](https://2e.aonsrd.com/spells/186-mind-reading) spell granted by Eyeswide, it allows more uses but removes ongoing reading, Intelligence comparison, and repeat sampling on a critical failure. Ponder Unity keeps its separate free-action niche.
- **Prediction:** the attack benefit expires at the end of the user's current turn, affects only their next Strike against the chosen creature, and uses normal multiple attack penalties. The setup costs an action and a check. An allied directive cannot multiply it.
- **Memory:** a minute-long activity, one daily attempt, one event, and incapacitation. The rank-5 mind probe spell in Player Core pp. 344–345 is an upper comparison: Delve Memory grants much narrower retrieval and cannot interrogate an entire biography.
- **Detection:** Mindsense is imprecise and respects solid barriers. Its level-20 precise version lasts only a minute. Neither identifies people through disguises nor reads their thoughts.
- **Defense:** bonuses are circumstance bonuses until the capstone's status bonuses; identical types do not add together. Shielding consumes the user's reaction and competes with other reactions. Hold Together is fortune and cannot stack with another fortune effect on the same roll.
- **Long range:** distant links carry intentional messages only. They cannot carry attacks, Read Thoughts, directives, or shielding over planetary distances.

## Ponder Unity integration

Keep [[Campaign/Party/Jonah/Ponder Unity|the existing feat]] unchanged. It remains a daily, free-action, touch-range ability using class DC, with audible repetition, incapacitation, its own temporary immunity, and its permanent-immunity consequence. Telepathy does not silently remove those traits or restrictions.

The original feat does not specify the outcome threshold of its additional Will save to reveal a secret, or precisely when permanent immunity begins. Before that result first matters, use this proposed ruling: the second save uses the same class DC and incapacitation adjustment; a failure or critical failure reveals one secret currently held by the target; either way, making that second save causes permanent immunity to Ponder Unity. This is a proposed clarification for GM review, not an edit to the existing feat. The secret reflects the target's knowledge and beliefs.

Read Thoughts and Delve Memory have independent named immunities. Ponder Unity's immunity applies to Ponder Unity. A successful use of one does not cancel another effect's immunity or restore expended daily uses. Multiple abilities can offer separate attempts, each with its own action, frequency, and information limits.

## Foundational checks and playtest priorities

- Verify each example progression against feat levels and prerequisites. The free award must not consume the normal level-2 selection.
- Verify DC arithmetic and that incapacitation compares a creature's level with the telepath's level for Delve Memory. It does not compare against feat level 12.
- A level-6 telepath uses Psychic Interruption on a boss: even on a successful save it briefly denies reactions, then that boss is immune for an hour. This reliable success effect is the principal encounter-balance watch point.
- Read Thoughts on a cooperative ally yields only a present surface thought. It supplies neither a map of every memory nor information the ally lacks.
- Read Thoughts against a resisting target cannot be rerolled every round: both the user's ten-minute frequency and target's one-hour immunity apply.
- Distant Channel does not allow a telepath aboard a ship to shield an ally kilometers away. Shared-channel communication is the only range exception.
- Borrowed Senses costs two actions, requires Sustaining, leaves the user's body blind and deaf to its surroundings, and offers no remote origin for attacks or effects.
- Mindsense notices a susceptible creature in darkness within 30 feet, but leaves it hidden and subject to the normal DC 11 targeting flat check. It detects nothing through a sealed wall.
- An immune construct cannot be read or predicted. A thinking android without mental immunity can be. Ancestry alone does not decide susceptibility.
- Mental Shelter and Anticipate Danger cannot both be used with the same single reaction. Fortune from Predictive Strike and another fortune effect never provides three attack dice.
- Level-20 awareness provides a powerful local team benefit for one encounter. It does not open UNITY, determine the Star Dream's origin, or compel any character's decision.

These are design checks. Combat performance and player experience still need table playtesting, particularly the level-6 interference and the level-18 and level-20 group defenses.

## Flavor direction

Use observable habits: an unspoken reply, a borrowed sensory impression, an approaching intention, a remembered detail. Fonqugon neural bodies can shift into a deliberate arrangement as Ponderer concentrates. This describes his own manifestation without making the archetype ancestry-exclusive.

The GM has approved a cryptic UNITY line in the general Telepath Dedication: “A part of the solution to UNITY. Establishes contact between separate minds. Further conditions remain unresolved.” The dedication's mechanical benefits remain as written. The meaning and in-world source of that line are open questions recorded in [[Campaign/GM Notes/Continuity/Void Calling Revision and UNITY Keys|Void Calling Revision and UNITY Keys]].

Ponderer's creation on Unity-22 and the other private backstory connections remain GM information. The archetype's general prerequisites apply to characters throughout the setting.
