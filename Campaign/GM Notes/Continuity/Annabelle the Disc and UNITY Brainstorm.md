# Annabelle the Disc and UNITY

Private working ideas from the November 9 discussion. Preserve the distinctions below when resuming this thread. Proposed explanations have not become events, revelations, or settled cosmology.

## Established material and current GM direction

- [[Campaign/Party/Gregory/8-Ball Scene|8-Ball's opening scene]] establishes a small metallic disc with a pale face, darker rim, and shallow handmade-looking pattern. The courier carries it; Annabelle takes it during the ambush and shoots Cowboy. Its function remains unidentified.
- [[Campaign/Party/Gregory/Secret Backstory|8-Ball's private backstory]] identifies Cowboy as an AnChris leader and the other component of 8-Ball as a corporate defector from Theosis. The defector's access and involvement remain unfinished.
- Annabelle is an Astrazoan. The GM is strongly considering her as Cowboy's longtime second-in-command, who supposedly defected from Erbium. Double agency, divided allegiance, or a later change of loyalty remain possible.
- AnChris performs acts of terrorism against Erbium. Members likely have personal grievances against the company. Their grievances and goals do not erase their methods or the consequences of their attacks.
- The GM prefers the disc to be a **key**. What it opens has not been selected.
- Ponderer was made on Unity-22 and is intended to be a key to something provisionally called **UNITY**. Its nature remains undefined. It need not resemble Starfield's Unity.
- The campaign could reach level 20. UNITY is a possible campaign endpoint, requiring a slow development across many adventures.
- In a later discussion, the GM approved cryptic “part of the solution to UNITY” text in the general Telepath and Void-Returned dedication entries. The meaning, source, and further conditions remain open. See [[Campaign/GM Notes/Continuity/Void Calling Revision and UNITY Keys|the revision and key-text note]] for the exact wording.

## Possibilities to revisit

Annabelle may know a secret about the universe that Cowboy came too close to discovering. Her defection could have been sincere with a concealed limit, or a discovery could have prompted renewed cooperation with Erbium. Neither explanation has been chosen. Her warnings, participation in AnChris, and decision to kill Cowboy should make sense together once her motives are settled.

The disc could open a sealed research installation associated with the Star Dream. In one proposal, Erbium removed personnel and equipment but left something it could not relocate. Cowboy sought a practical advantage against the corporation and misunderstood the full consequences of opening the site. Annabelle knew why it had been sealed. This is an assistant proposal, **not an approved destination or backstory**.

An earlier proposal made the disc a means of reaching somewhere excluded from ordinary Drift travel. The GM preferred a slower approach to the campaign's final mystery; do not assume the disc provides direct access to UNITY.

The disc and Ponderer can have different functions and become relevant at different stages. A physical key may grant access to an intermediate discovery; Ponderer's unusual mind may eventually enable contact or another decisive act. No particular relationship between these two functions is established.

Annabelle's visible injuries might relate to a prior encounter with the phenomenon. Their cause, timing, and meaning remain open.

## Pacing direction

Give nearer arcs concrete answers and consequences: reconstructing 8-Ball's history, finding Annabelle or the disc, discovering what it opens, and choosing what to do with the discovery. A resolved arc can reveal another connection without merely postponing every answer until level 20.

Ponderer's abilities can develop through play while the research behind his creation is gradually uncovered. Learning about Unity-22 does not automatically reveal his final purpose. His player retains control of his choices when that purpose becomes relevant.

## Open questions

- What did Cowboy believe the disc would let him accomplish?
- What did Annabelle believe would happen if he succeeded?
- Who or what held her deeper loyalty, and did that change?
- What did the corporate defector actually know or have access to?
- What does the disc open, and what useful intermediate story can happen there?
- What is UNITY, and what does being its key require Ponderer to choose or do?
