# Void Calling Revision and UNITY Keys

The GM requested a rapidly fatal true Void Calling, stage-1 transmission that includes asymptomatic Moyishuu, and infection that becomes dormant and undetectable in natural planetary atmosphere. The GM also approved cryptic UNITY text in the general Telepath and Void-Returned dedication entries.

## Current rules

[[Rules/Campaign/Afflictions/Void Calling|Void Calling]] is the working source. Exposure retains its multi-day onset and later hallucinations and airlock compulsion. True Void Calling has immediate onset and one-minute stages. It causes physical impairment followed by death and transformation. Its DC remains 27, and its virulent trait makes recovery difficult.

Entering vacuum with active Exposure can trigger the true form at any stage, including while wearing a spacesuit. On a successful initial save, retain Exposure; on a failure, replace it with the true infection. The vacuum trigger is limited to one initial save per 24 hours. Active disease timers continue aboard an air-filled ship outside a natural atmosphere.

Any stage-1-or-higher active carrier can transmit the slower Exposure form after eight hours aboard the same occupied vessel or habitat. Moyishuu retain an asymptomatic carrier state because of their cosmic trait. Cosmic carriers use daily recovery saves, including for the true form. Their own stage ceiling keeps failed saves at stage 1.

## Dormancy and diagnosis

Natural planetary atmosphere immediately suppresses stage effects and transmission. Save the stage and remaining interval; resume them on departure. Preserve pending virulent successes and onset time as well. A transformation countdown after death is paused by atmosphere, while the death itself remains an event requiring ordinary revival rules.

Dormant infection has no diagnostic signature. This applies to medicine, occult examination, magical disease detection, and the carrier-detection benefit of Calling Recognition. Old records can establish suspicion. A diagnostic result obtained in atmosphere is inconclusive.

Cures can remove a dormant infection if their normal targeting permits treating the subject. The GM tracks counteract results privately so a cure attempt cannot double as an atmospheric diagnostic test. A dormant carrier and a cured survivor are distinct states.

## Drift boundary

The GM confirmed that the Drift prevents new exposure. People believe it suppresses existing infection, but that claim remains uncertain. Before an infected creature enters the Drift during play, decide how that particular episode proceeds and use either paused-state bookkeeping or active progression consistently. This decision establishes observations for that episode; the cause and reliability of suppression remain open.

The earlier shipboard account that the Drift protects travelers therefore remains compatible with the current rules. Do not revise past transcripts, assign an infection to Reen, or decide that a reported knock proves a particular source.

## Timing checks

| Initial save and later results | Course of true Void Calling |
| --- | --- |
| Failure, then three failures | Stage 1 immediately; stage 2 at 1 minute; stage 3 at 2 minutes; death at 3 minutes |
| Critical failure, then critical failure | Stage 2 immediately; death at 1 minute |
| Failure, then two successes | Stage 1 immediately; first success holds stage 1; second success cures at 2 minutes |
| Critical failure, then critical success | Stage 2 immediately; stage 1 at 1 minute; further recovery is required |
| Enter natural atmosphere 40 seconds into stage 2 | Stage 2 and 20 seconds remain on record; symptoms and transmission are suppressed |
| Leave atmosphere after a week | Stage 2 resumes with 20 seconds until its next save |
| Cosmic carrier critically fails an initial save | Stage 1, asymptomatic; next stage save after one active day |
| A stage-2 patient temporarily gains cosmic for 1 minute | Harmful symptoms and the original timer pause for that minute; transmission remains possible outside atmosphere and the Drift |

The fastest one-minute death requires critical failures. Even the three-minute ordinary-failure course is lethal at low levels. Give players observable symptoms and the relevant treatment or escape options before an exposure becomes a forced consequence of a misunderstood rule.

## Treatment and archetype interactions

Treat Disease takes eight hours under Player Core p. 201. Emergency Grounding supplies a two-action intervention for the minute-long crisis, with +1 on success or +2 on critical success to the next stage save. The target can benefit from one attempt per stage interval across all caregivers. The interval continues during treatment.

Refuse the Call and later cosmic adaptations can pause an existing crisis while their cosmic trait lasts. Record the patient's original stage so the effect's expiry restores the correct condition. Permanent cosmic adaptation permits daily recovery attempts without further deterioration while it remains present.

[[Rules/Campaign/Archetypes/Void-Returned Archetype|Void-Returned]] now requires survival through stage 2 or higher followed by dormancy or cure. Its abilities persist in atmosphere. The patient's infection state determines transmission and relapse; taking the archetype has no curative effect.

[[Rules/Campaign/Ancestries/Moyishuu/Moyishuu|Moyishuu]] now points to the carrier rules. The public disease entry establishes that this ancestry can carry the infection; the identity and actual infection state of individual characters remain private.

## UNITY text

The GM approved these lines in the **general** dedication entries. They are original campaign flavor inspired by the supplied description from *So I'm a Spider, So What?* UNITY's nature remains open.

**Telepath:** “A part of the solution to UNITY. Establishes contact between separate minds. Further conditions remain unresolved.”

**Void-Returned:** “A part of the solution to UNITY. Preserves continuity through the Calling. Further conditions remain unresolved.”

The association is now visible in both rules entries. Mechanical benefits remain in each feat's rules. Who wrote the lines, how characters encounter this wording in-world, and what the solution requires remain undecided. The eventual roles of individual bearers and other characters are also open.

Ponderer's planned special relationship with UNITY remains in [[Campaign/GM Notes/Continuity/Annabelle the Disc and UNITY Brainstorm|the earlier brainstorming note]]. Preserve room for his decisions and the other characters' roles as the campaign develops.
