# Void Calling Revision and UNITY Keys

The GM requested a rapidly fatal true Void Calling, stage-1 transmission that includes asymptomatic Moyishuu, and infection that becomes dormant and undetectable in natural planetary atmosphere. The GM also approved cryptic UNITY text in the general Telepath and Void-Returned dedication entries.

## Current rules

[[Rules/Campaign/Afflictions/Void Calling|Void Calling]] is the working source. Exposure retains its multi-day onset and later hallucinations and airlock compulsion. True Void Calling has immediate onset and one-minute stages. It causes physical impairment followed by death and transformation. Its DC remains 27, and its virulent trait makes recovery difficult.

Entering vacuum with active Exposure can trigger the true form at any stage, including while wearing a spacesuit. On a successful initial save, retain Exposure; on a failure, replace it with the true infection. The vacuum trigger is limited to one initial save per 24 hours. Active disease timers continue aboard an air-filled ship outside a natural atmosphere.

Any stage-1-or-higher active carrier can transmit the slower Exposure form after eight hours aboard the same occupied vessel or habitat. A creature infected while cosmic begins at stage 1 after any onset. Outside natural planetary atmosphere, everyone’s recovery saves stop at stage 1. Disease-removal effects can cure an infection in either environment.

Gaining cosmic while already infected suppresses harmful effects and preserves the recorded stage. Normal intervals continue, and failures or critical failures hold the stage in either environment. Outside atmosphere, successful saves reduce the stage to a minimum of 1, applying virulent normally; at stage 1, intervals continue without rolling. In natural planetary atmosphere, recovery saves continue at stage 1 and can cure the infection. An infection acquired while cosmic stays capped at stage 1. If the trait ends, use the current stage and remaining interval, with atmospheric suppression applied where relevant.

## Dormancy and diagnosis

Natural planetary atmosphere immediately suppresses stage effects and transmission. Onset and stage timers continue, with saves resolved privately by the GM using the cosmic carrier exceptions where applicable. Recovery below stage 1 cures the disease. Departure applies the current stage’s effects and preserves its remaining interval and any pending virulent success.

A living patient who reaches true Void Calling’s stage 4 in atmosphere stays alive and continues saving each minute; failed saves hold stage 4. Departure while still at stage 4 causes death unless cosmic protection suppresses it. A body that was already dead remains dead, with its transformation countdown paused until departure.

Dormant infection has no diagnostic signature. This applies to medicine, occult examination, magical disease detection, and the carrier-detection benefit of Calling Recognition. Old records can establish suspicion. A diagnostic result obtained in atmosphere is inconclusive.

Cures can remove a dormant infection if their normal targeting permits treating the subject. The GM tracks counteract results privately so a cure attempt cannot double as an atmospheric diagnostic test. A dormant carrier and a cured survivor are distinct states.

## Drift boundary

The GM confirmed that the Drift prevents new exposure. People believe it suppresses existing infection, but that claim remains uncertain. Before an infected creature enters the Drift during play, decide how that particular episode proceeds and use either atmospheric suppression with continuing saves and recovery below stage 1, or the rules for travel outside atmosphere, consistently. This decision establishes observations for that episode; the cause and reliability of suppression remain open.

The earlier shipboard account that the Drift protects travelers therefore remains compatible with the current rules. Do not revise past transcripts, assign an infection to Reen, or decide that a reported knock proves a particular source.

## Timing checks

| Situation | Course of true Void Calling |
| --- | --- |
| Outside atmosphere: initial failure, then three failures, without cosmic protection | Stage 1 immediately; stage 2 at 1 minute; stage 3 at 2 minutes; death at 3 minutes |
| Outside atmosphere: stage 1, then two successful saves | Remains infected at stage 1 |
| Enter atmosphere 40 seconds into stage 2, then critically succeed | Save occurs 20 seconds later; stage falls to 1 and a new one-minute interval begins |
| In atmosphere: stage 1, then two consecutive successes | Recovery below stage 1 cures the infection; the GM records the result privately |
| In atmosphere: cosmic carrier at stage 1, then critically fail | Remains at stage 1; next recovery save occurs in 1 minute |
| In atmosphere: stage 3 without cosmic protection, then fail | Stage 4 is recorded; the patient remains alive and saves again in 1 minute |
| Depart atmosphere while still at stage 4, without cosmic protection | Death applies immediately; transformation follows in 1d4 rounds unless interrupted |
| Outside atmosphere: stage-2 patient gains cosmic for 1 minute with 30 seconds left, then critically succeeds | Stage falls to 1 after 30 seconds; when cosmic ends, stage 1 takes effect with 30 seconds left on its interval |

The fastest one-minute death requires critical failures. Even the three-minute ordinary-failure course is lethal at low levels. Give players observable symptoms and the relevant treatment or escape options before an exposure becomes a forced consequence of a misunderstood rule.

## Treatment and archetype interactions

Treat Disease takes eight hours under Player Core p. 201. Emergency Grounding supplies a two-action intervention for the minute-long crisis, with +1 on success or +2 on critical success to the next stage save. The target can benefit from one attempt per stage interval across all caregivers. The interval continues during treatment.

Refuse the Call and later cosmic adaptations suppress harmful stage effects while normal intervals continue. Record any improvement so the trait's expiry restores the current stage. Outside atmosphere, cosmic adaptation allows safe improvement to stage 1. In atmosphere, cosmic recovery saves can cure the infection through recovery below stage 1; failures and critical failures hold its stage. Treat Disease and grounding follow those recovery limits; disease-removal effects can clear the infection in either environment.

[[Void-Returned Archetype|Void-Returned]] now requires survival through stage 2 or higher followed by dormancy or cure. Its abilities persist in atmosphere. The patient's infection state determines transmission and relapse; taking the archetype has no curative effect.

[[Rules/Campaign/Ancestries/Moyishuu/Moyishuu|Moyishuu]] now points to the carrier rules. The public disease entry establishes that this ancestry can carry the infection; the identity and actual infection state of individual characters remain private.

## UNITY text

The GM approved these lines in the **general** dedication entries. They are original campaign flavor inspired by the supplied description from *So I'm a Spider, So What?* UNITY's nature remains open.

**Telepath and Void-Returned:** “A part of the solution to UNITY.”

The association is now visible in both rules entries. Mechanical benefits remain in each feat's rules. Who wrote the lines, how characters encounter this wording in-world, and what the solution requires remain undecided. The eventual roles of individual bearers and other characters are also open.

Ponderer's planned special relationship with UNITY remains in [[Campaign/GM Notes/Continuity/Annabelle the Disc and UNITY Brainstorm|the earlier brainstorming note]]. Preserve room for his decisions and the other characters' roles as the campaign develops.
