# Tuppie
==Unique== ==Tiny== ==Construct== ==Mindless== ==Robot== ==Tech== ==Minion==
**Recall Knowledge** DC 25 • Construct (Arcana), Robot (Computers or Crafting)
**Perception** +7 (darkvision)
**Languages** understands Talican but can't speak coherently
**Skills** Crafting +6, Medicine +8, Stealth +7
**Str** -3 **Dex** +3 **Con** +1 **Int** -5 **Wis** +4 **Cha** +0

---

**AC** 16; **Fort** +5, **Ref** +7, **Will** +6
**HP** 15; **Hardness** 2; **Immunities** bleed, death effects, disease, doomed, drained, fatigued, healing, mental, nonlethal attacks, paralyzed, poison, sickened, spirit, unconscious, vitality, void; **Weaknesses** electricity 3

**Integrated Medkit** Tuppie's internal compartments contain diagnostic sensors, sterile dressings, sealants, injectors, and other medical tools that function as a medkit. Tuppie can use Medicine actions requiring a medkit without carrying a separate one.

**Little Helper** `pf2:1` Tuppie positions tools, illuminates Tarn's work area, holds components in place, and feeds her diagnostic information. Tarn gains a +2 circumstance bonus to the next **Crafting** or **Computers** check she attempts before the end of her turn. Tuppie must be adjacent to Tarn or the object Tarn is working on.

**Medical Routine** Tuppie can perform **Treat Wounds** using its own Medicine modifier. Tuppie's treatment is technological and non-magical. It otherwise follows all the normal rules for Treat Wounds.

**Bedside Protocol** When Tuppie succeeds at Treat Wounds, increase the Hit Points restored by **2**. On a critical success, increase the Hit Points restored by **4** instead.

**Unreliable Tech** Once Tuppie is reduced to less than half its Hit Points, or immediately upon being damaged by a critical hit, it becomes permanently [glitching](https://2e.aonsrd.com/conditions/19-glitching) 1 until its Hit Points are fully restored, such as by being Repaired.

**Noncombat Programming** Tuppie has no Strikes and can't wield weapons. It won't deliberately harm another creature even if commanded to do so.

---

**Speed** 5 feet (hover), fly 25 feet

**Treat Wounds** (exploration, healing, manipulate) Tuppie spends 10 minutes treating one injured living creature. It attempts a Medicine check using the normal Treat Wounds rules.
