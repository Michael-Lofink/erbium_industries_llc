### Crash adjudication

The breach response successfully invited different approaches. Holding position, using an autograppler, and overriding the door all addressed the same immediate problem. 8-Ball's successful intervention altered the situation by stopping the decompression, and everyone retained a role in surviving the initial emergency.

Reen's landing used Arrest a Fall, requested privately by Mary Beth over Discord while he was disguised as Mordrun with a holoskin. The action permits an Acrobatics check or Reflex save, typically against DC 15 with allowance for a higher DC due to turbulence or other circumstances. The GM set DC 20; Mary Beth's result of 24 succeeded, so Reen took no damage from the fall. The public description of jumping concealed the actual action. The different DC and zero damage therefore have an explicit mechanical basis, and the earlier criticism of this difference is withdrawn.

The other characters faced the separately adjudicated crash saves at DC 25, with circumstance bonuses and additional hover mitigation for Tarn. The announcement of that DC came after rolls were underway. Future planning should address the lethality and available protections within this crash procedure while preserving distinct actions such as Arrest a Fall and their stated outcomes. The transcript alone omitted the private information needed to evaluate Reen's resolution correctly.

The main concern is the combination of difficulty and damage. Ponderer had 15 maximum HP. The proposed 30 damage on a failure equaled the instant-death threshold. His corrected result of 25 reduced that damage to 15, leaving him unconscious after the earlier 4 fire damage. Accepting the corrected modifier was necessary and appropriate. It also reveals that an accounting correction separated his survival from immediate death.

For perspective on the separate crash save, a total save modifier of +8 against DC 25 succeeds on a roll of 17 or higher, a 20% chance. This illustrates the lethality facing characters using that procedure. Reen's access to Arrest a Fall properly gives him a different resolution with its own success effect. GM Core's level-1 reference DC is 15; a catastrophe can exceed that, but its survival consequences require deliberate calibration.

Before a future set piece, decide whether death is an intended possible consequence. Set the degrees of success and damage before rolling, including how protective actions modify them. Explain enough of the danger that a player choosing to shield another person understands the personal cost. If the event establishes the campaign's survival premise, checks can determine injuries, separated equipment, damaged communications, or the distance between survivors, with survival established in advance.

Preserve the current outcome: all four PCs survived, the ship was destroyed, Drew died, and the rescues happened. No retroactive deaths or failed rescues are warranted by this review. The useful corrections concern current conditions and future procedure.

## 2. Clean session recap

Four personnel reached Erbium Industries' Frontier Extraction Division through very different circumstances. Their introductory scenes showed the lives and events that preceded their assignment.

At a crowded transit concourse, Mordrun Stronglimb was called for the Great Volunteering. He attempted to flee, dropped his coffee and badge during a collision, and was stopped by an older dwarven woman who urged him to report. Elsewhere in the concourse, a reader sitting above the crowd watched recruitment advertisements and heard the final announcement. He put on an armlet and left his perch. At service desk 18, a dwarf presenting himself as Mordrun passed through repeated failures of the visual records, identity archive, and biometric systems. The frustrated clerk authorized temporary credentials and obtained his written signature.

Ponderer of Oceanus met Naomi O. Regreqt and her son Connor during a clinical appointment. Naomi had been denied access to her husband for four rotations and was under financial pressure to resume service. Ponderer proposed using his mental abilities, suggesting that cooperation might help him obtain information. The contact exposed a remembered procedure involving Naomi, her son's disclosure, her husband's closure, and a question about Band 4. Observers watched the consultation through a mirror.

Tarn remembered her sixteenth birthday with Sobok, who baked a cake and introduced an old birthday-wish tradition. Their evening ended in an argument when he told her to abandon the ideas he had encouraged. The following day, her parents said she could no longer see him. They showed her an upgraded home in Crown's upper frame and explained that the household needed three employments on record. They asked her to enlist in a service they described as safe and compatible with her schooling.

In Kharosai, an android in a brown hat met a courier carrying a small metallic disc and escorted him to a transfer room. A vesk waited inside. An electrical discharge and gunfire interrupted the exchange. The android regained awareness on the floor and saw the courier dead. A masked woman he called Annabel retrieved the disc. Her exposed skin showed burns and other unusual damage. She shot away his reaching hand, referred to an earlier warning, and fired again with her rifle aimed at his head.

In the present, the FED transport carried the new crew through the Drift. Tarn administered a reintegration assessment to 8-Ball, who had little memory or understanding of his assignment. His recurring diagnostic notifications prompted a search for technical assistance, and he eventually disabled them. Ponderer and Mordrun completed their partner check and heard a knock from outside the hull despite being told that the Drift protected them from Void Calling.

The crew registered its issued equipment and divided shared tools. Ponderer took the medkit, Tarn accepted technical equipment, and 8-Ball received the assay wand. Their abrasive pilot handled the distribution. They also met Drew, a technician delighted to find listeners for his life story. Before the ship left relay coverage, the crew was instructed to record its authenticated PES stamp and maintain local records through its ghostboxes.

During travel, Mordrun questioned the pilot about the controls. Ponderer attempted to review former patients through legitimate channels and discovered that his closure deferment prevented access. Tarn spent time with Tuppy, her drone, while 8-Ball listened to Drew. Later, 8-Ball asked Tarn to be his friend; she agreed, and he also counted Tuppy as a friend. A character subsequently noticed a possible holographic glitch around someone entering the restroom area. The observation went uninvestigated when the ship was struck.

The impacts destroyed the forward section with the pilot and caused a dangerous breach. The crew held on and worked to close an obstructed door. 8-Ball overrode the mechanism, sealing the compartment before the damaged rear section tore free. As the remaining hab descended, he directed Tarn into the bathroom module and held its door shut. Tarn activated hovering support and asked Tuppy to help her. Mordrun braced himself against the descending hab; Ponderer abandoned his pressure suit to compress into a corner and anchor himself.

After impact, Mordrun regained consciousness among the wreckage. Drew was dead, and the other PCs were incapacitated. Mordrun authenticated the medkit with Ponderer's badge and followed its instructions to treat him. Tuppy treated Tarn inside the bathroom module. Finally, 8-Ball's internal companion activated an effect that restored him to consciousness at 1 HP. The session ended with all four PCs alive at an unidentified crash site, beyond relay coverage.
