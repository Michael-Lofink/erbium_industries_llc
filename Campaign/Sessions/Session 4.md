# Recap
*Hope (Title Theme), Justin E. Bell*

Alright team, let’s begin the debrief.

Last time, our team was recovering from its first encounter with the wildlife of The Last Grin when headlights appeared beyond the wreckage. As 8-Ball experienced an unwelcome interruption from somewhere in his memory, the others prepared to meet whoever was approaching. Their visitor introduced himself by shooting the emergency beacon and giving Ponderer thirty seconds to explain why he should remain alive. A promising start to local relations.

While PPPPP continued his introductions, his brenneri companion, Baron Bon Bortish, began helping himself to the remains of the ship. The team tried invoking Erbium’s authority, offering future profits, and making threats of its own. Eventually, 8-Ball helped secure an agreement, and our team climbed aboard the Grinrunner alongside the equipment that had, until very recently, belonged to them.

With Mordrun riding shotgun and the others crowded into the back, the journey provided an opportunity for medical attention, conversation, and the distribution of several days’ worth of rations in pursuit of friendship. Along the way, the team noticed that a cave previously visible on the landscape had disappeared. PPPPP identified it as the Quantum Cave, then became considerably less interested in answering questions.

Arriving at the detached cockpit, the team found dead electrovores, missing supplies, and an absent pilot. Tarn accessed the surviving systems and discovered records of missiles launched from the moon’s surface. Meanwhile, tire tracks and their escorts’ conspicuous lack of interest in the wreck suggested that this was a return visit. Tarn also began working on a promotion through badge forgery, demonstrating initiative under difficult circumstances.

These discoveries prompted a private discussion about their hosts’ intentions. Mordrun raised concerns about cannibals and bone cages, and the team considered diversions, commandeering the Grinrunner, and explosives. Tarn’s attempt to conceal a mine was noticed by Baron, who asked her to stop. She put it away.

Ponderer then took a more direct approach to understanding their new acquaintance. Reading Baron’s thoughts, he involuntarily announced, in Baron’s own voice, “I really hope they move in.” The conversation that followed revealed a settlement called Fresh-Breath, a community that had lived here for generations under the leadership of A. Sop. Its people survived largely on hunted electrovores, and Baron seemed sincerely interested in acquiring some new neighbors. The team agreed to continue the journey.

And now, as the Grinrunner rounds the rocks, we see Fresh-Breath crowded against the dark hillside. Blue-white lights mark its stairs and platforms, frost gathers beneath the roofs, and above the buildings stand two heavy gun emplacements, their barrels pointed toward the sky.

And that is where we will begin: “Fresh-Breath.”

# GM Background

## Fresh-Breath and OutCry IV

Fresh-Breath has roughly 100 residents, mostly human. Their ancestors hijacked **OutCry IV** during Wander's final days and escaped far into space. Generations of cannibalization supplied the systems that keep Fresh-Breath habitable.

Restoring the ship requires components now serving the settlement. Taking those systems would leave Fresh-Breath uninhabitable. Salvage from the party's wreck provides some parts, but falls short of a complete repair. The ship may become the party's home after escape.

**Possible components:** an environmental processor serving habitation; a power conditioner supporting heat and power; wiring and couplings recovered from the party's wreck.

## Nera Sop

Nera expects population growth to exhaust the electrovore food supply. She wants the party to persuade A. Sop to dismantle Fresh-Breath, repair the ship, and evacuate everyone to Settled Systems space. Despite the stories of Erbium's actions on Wander, she would choose Theosis over dying here without experiencing life elsewhere.

Her father still treats her as a little girl and dismisses her concerns. She suspects PPPPP's involvement in the strike. His paternity and A. Sop's responsibility for the attack are concealed from her; her use of **“my dad”** hurts PPPPP accidentally.

## A. Sop

A. Sop grew up hearing how Wander's sky cried and its earth roared during the collapse, and how people considered unimportant were abandoned. His ancestors escaped aboard the hijacked ship. These accounts underpin his fear of returning to Settled Systems space.

He prioritizes the colony's continued existence over the individual residents' prospects. He ordered the strike on the party's vessel. Offering them the stripped OutCry IV gets them out of Fresh-Breath and exposes them to the electrovores.

He initially refuses to dismantle essential infrastructure. Persuading him to evacuate will be difficult; the residents' destination, safety, and treatment by outsiders are possible concerns to address.

## PPPPP and Hare

A husband and wife presenting as Moyishuu stopped at The Last Grin on pilgrimage. A. Sop and the woman had an affair. She left him with a child, **PPPPP**, named after her husband, **PPPP**. Having grown up on the moon, A. Sop was unaware that Moyishuu can conceive only in the Drift; the woman was someone other than she appeared to be.

PPPPP's shapeshifting surfaced quickly. He was some form of impure astrazoan. A. Sop raised him while concealing their biological relationship. PPPPP became obsessed with earning his love and changed his appearance to resemble him more closely.

During a drunken fight between A. Sop and his wife, **Hare**, the paternity came out. PPPPP overheard and has kept his discovery secret. Hare locked herself in their room and died some months later.

PPPPP fears leaving Fresh-Breath will cost him his father. He despises Nera for the attention and recognition she received throughout childhood. He opposes evacuation and may urge A. Sop to kill the party.
