Last time, we got a peak into parts of the history of those who make up "Frontier Extraction Division 7000 142". Brushing past these tender moments, our team found itself on a standard issue F.E.D. Block ship sanctioned for F.E.D. team drop off and pickup, traveling through space. Their job was to excavate a frontier astral body that had shown potential Erbium deposits from deep space scans run at facilities located in the Low Plane. After approximately 67 offsets, the Block ship began to take damage from outside the ship, and the Pilot's final words to the team were "We've taken a hit. Seal your suits. Get clear of the aft passage. Gah, I love tobacco--" before the cockpit of the ship ripped itself from the hull. More blows were heard and the ship ripped further, until all that was left was the main hab, and the need for a landing strategy.

Our team using their unique skills, found ways to survive their impending doom as the main hab of the ship barreled towards the planetary body that had captured the remains of the ship in its atmosphere. And those methods of surviving didn't work! What a stupid, stupid team. Oh what, I'm not supposed to say that part? --Anyways, crash landing on a mysterious planet out in the middle of nowhere, our team licks it wounds in the debris unsure of what to do next.

In fact, at this very moment, as we zoom in on the crash site, and follow past the metal scrap laid bare, past the flames, and settle on the lavatory-hab that had been previously embedded in the main hab, but was now posted in the ground several feet away.
