# A. Sop's Office

**Session 4 · GM reference** — *Detroit City Ambient (Part 1), Michael McCann*

> [!gm-screen]
> > [!gm-scene] Scene
> > ```tabs
> > --- At the table (default)
> > ### The meeting
> > A. Sop receives the party with PPPPP beside him. PPPPP argues for killing them because they could expose Fresh-Breath or return with others. A. Sop is open to their departure under conditions of secrecy and permanent separation.
> > 
> > ### What A. Sop asks
> > Who expects their arrival? Have they transmitted their location? Who knew their route? What equipment survived?
> > 
> > ### His offer
> > A. Sop can be convinced to reveal **OutCry IV**, the ship his ancestors used to escape Wander. It was left at the mouth of the Quantum Cave. If the party can repair it, they can take it, provided they **keep Fresh-Breath secret and never return**.
> > 
> > ### Concealed from the party
> > The ship was cannibalized to build Fresh-Breath. A. Sop hopes the electrovores kill the party during their attempt to recover it.
> > 
> > --- Questions & pressure
> > ### Is the ship intact?
> > A. Sop evades questions about missing equipment. **Suggested excuse:** he has stayed away from the cave for years.
> > 
> > ### What dangers are there?
> > If pressed, he may disclose the electrovore breeding ground and Chuckle Vents. Another hunter can also provide this information.
> > 
> > ### Who shot us down?
> > A. Sop ordered it; PPPPP knows. **Suggested response under direct confrontation:** A. Sop admits authorizing fire and claims the approaching ship threatened the settlement.
> > 
> > ### What assurances do you want?
> > Secrecy about Fresh-Breath, departure, and a promise to stay away.
> > 
> > ### Optional escalation
> > PPPPP demands their transmitters or continued confinement. A. Sop can overrule him while offering the cave expedition.
> > 
> > --- Background
> > ### OutCry IV
> > Fresh-Breath has roughly 100 residents, mostly human. Their ancestors hijacked OutCry IV during Wander's final days. Generations of cannibalization supplied the systems that keep Fresh-Breath habitable.
> > 
> > Restoring the ship requires components now serving the settlement. Taking those systems would leave Fresh-Breath uninhabitable. Salvage from the party's wreck provides some parts, but falls short of a complete repair.
> > 
> > ### A. Sop's fear
> > He grew up hearing how people considered unimportant were abandoned during Wander's collapse. Those accounts underpin his fear of returning to Settled Systems space. He initially refuses to dismantle essential infrastructure; a proposed evacuation must address the residents' destination, safety, and treatment by outsiders.
> > 
> > ### The family secret
> > A. Sop is PPPPP's father and believes PPPPP is unaware. PPPPP learned the truth by overhearing A. Sop and Hare, and conceals that knowledge. Nera knows PPPPP as her father's hunter and right-hand man.
> > 
> > ### Nera's position
> > Nera expects population growth to exhaust the electrovore food supply. She wants the party to persuade her father to restore the ship and evacuate everyone. She suspects PPPPP's involvement in the attack; her father's responsibility and PPPPP's paternity are concealed from her.
> > ```
>
> > [!gm-npcs] Present NPCs
> > ### A. Sop
> > **Level 5 · Head of Fresh-Breath**  
> > AC **20** · Fort **+7** · Ref **+9** · Will **+12**
> > 
> > **Manner:** worn, prideful, grim.  
> > **Wants:** Fresh-Breath preserved; the party gone.  
> > **Knows:** he ordered the strike; OutCry IV is stripped.  
> > **Conceals:** PPPPP is his son. He believes PPPPP is unaware.
> > 
> > ---
> > 
> > ### PPPPP
> > **Level 3 · Hunter and right-hand man**  
> > AC **18** · Fort **+12** · Ref **+9** · Will **+6**
> > 
> > **Manner:** self-interested; seeks A. Sop's approval.  
> > **Wants:** his father's affection; Fresh-Breath to remain.  
> > **Knows:** A. Sop ordered the strike and is his father.  
> > **Conceals:** he learned his paternity.  
> > **Resents:** Nera's recognition and attention.
