# Recap
*Hope (Title Theme), Justin E. Bell*
Alright team, let's begin the debrief.

Last time, the team found itself on a barren moon out in unsettled space which we will refer to as, "The Last Grin". Stranded in the wreckage of the standard issue F.E.D. Block ship they were assigned to, team members Ponderer, 8-Ball, and Tarn were in desperate need of support. An unidentified system in 8-Ball sparked a revival procedure jump-starting fundamental operating systems. Meanwhile, Tarn, who crashed in the lavatory hab, was being stitched back together by honorary team member Tuppie. Finally, Ponderer, now a puddle of goo, was being studiously tended by Mordrun following the instructions of the FED Medkit to the letter.

8-Ball bravely chose to test the atmosphere, and Ponderer accidentally-bravely chose to test the atmosphere to which 8-ball being a taste-senseless freak didn't notice the sweet air that Ponderer felt filter through the membrane of his goo. The team, physically recovered, soon discovered the danger of "The Last Grin," that the atmosphere itself was poisoning them all with laughing gas, or nitrous as Mordrun noted.

Soon after, with some people devising plans to survive the toxic atmosphere, others deciding to tough it out, the team decided it'd be best to get their bearings and to ready for potential malevolent visitors. Along the way, 8-Ball spotted detachment points on the cockpit of the ship that had cleanly activated indicating a higher probability that the Pilot may have survived. Finally, Tarn deployed the FED Emergency Beacon unwittingly awakening the hungry electrovores burrowed in the moon.

After a brief but dangerous encounter with the native wildlife, our team takes a breath as the sound of wheels treading through the moon dust echoes in the distance.

And that is where we will begin, "Wait there's Life?"
