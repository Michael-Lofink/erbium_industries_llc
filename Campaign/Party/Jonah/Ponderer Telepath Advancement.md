# Ponderer Telepath Advancement

At level 2, Ponderer receives [[Rules/Campaign/Archetypes/Telepath#Telepath Dedication|Telepath Dedication]] as a bonus story feat. The award is planned; this note does not record a completed level-up or change the level-1 character sheets and printed cards.

## Exact award

- Grant Telepath Dedication in addition to his normal level-2 choices. If needed, waive its entry prerequisite for this award.
- Waive this dedication's requirement to take two additional Telepath feats before choosing another dedication. He can choose a different dedication with his ordinary level-2 class feat if he meets its prerequisites.
- This bonus award can be received despite an existing dedication restriction. It does not complete or waive restrictions imposed by a different archetype on later chosen dedications.
- Further Telepath feats use normal class-feat selections unless the GM explicitly awards additional feats. This is not an automatic Free Archetype feat at every even level.

## Level 2 benefits

He gains limited telepathy at 30 feet, or the range improvement described in the dedication if his current sheet already grants telepathy. He gains Share Impression and becomes trained in Occultism, or another skill if already trained in Occultism.

Choose Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma as the telepath attribute. Charisma is the recommended choice for his envoy build. Confirm the current sheet when leveling; with Charisma +4 at level 2, his telepath DC is **18**. This uses the archetype's own trained proficiency. It becomes expert at level 9 and master at level 17, applying only to Telepath abilities.

His first new uses are quiet conversation and a voluntary exchange of a brief remembered impression. He cannot silently use auditory envoy actions or Bon Mot through this telepathy. His normal class actions retain their existing rules.

## Relationship to Ponder Unity

[[Campaign/Party/Jonah/Ponder Unity|Ponder Unity]] remains unchanged: daily frequency, free action, touch range, class DC, audible repetition, incapacitation, and its own immunity rules. The new telepath DC does not replace its class DC.

The Telepath progression provides later development without making his ancestry feat a prerequisite for the general archetype. Read Thoughts at level 8 offers more frequent surface impressions; Delve Memory at level 12 adds a specific remembered event. Neither automatically reveals another person's entire history.

Before resolving Ponder Unity's additional save for a secret, consult the proposed clarification in [[Campaign/GM Notes/Continuity/Telepath Archetype Design#Ponder Unity integration|the design note]]. Its current wording leaves that save's threshold unspecified; the original feat has not been silently rewritten.

## Future choices

For a light investment, consider Attuned Perception at level 4, Read Thoughts at level 8, and Delve Memory at level 12, leaving the other normal class-feat selections available. These are suggestions for Jonah, not assigned choices.

For a protective development, begin with Mental Shelter and Shared Channel. The [[Campaign/GM Notes/Continuity/Telepath Archetype Design#Sample legal progressions|design note]] includes complete sample paths through level 20 and the costs of pursuing them.

## Private story boundary

Ponderer's eventual role in UNITY remains a GM mystery. The general Telepath Dedication now includes the approved cryptic line connecting the archetype to a “solution to UNITY.” His powers can manifest as growing control over his existing psychic sensitivity, with his smaller neural bodies settling into deliberate arrangements as he concentrates. His creation and intended purpose remain private story material.

Choosing a different archetype or declining later Telepath feats must not prevent his personal story from continuing. The level-20 Telepath capstone is a combat and perception ability; it does not define or unlock the campaign's endpoint.

See [[Campaign/GM Notes/Continuity/Annabelle the Disc and UNITY Brainstorm|Annabelle, the disc, and UNITY]] for the provisional story discussion and [[Campaign/GM Notes/Continuity/Void Calling Revision and UNITY Keys|the later UNITY text decision]]. Keep those notes and this private advancement note out of player-facing exports. The main [[Rules/Campaign/Archetypes/Telepath|Telepath rules]] include the approved cryptic line.
