# Session 2: action reminders

GM reference. The JSON decks contain player-safe rules summaries and no plot revelations. These cards summarize options; they do not grant feats or bypass training and equipment requirements.

## Opening reminder (about 90 seconds)

> Everyone starts with one Hero Point. You can spend it to reroll a check, keeping the new result, or save your points for heroic recovery if you're dying.
>
> Your character knows more than the actions printed on their class cards. Tell me what you want to accomplish and how; you don't need to remember the rule's name.
>
> While exploring, tell me what you're doing: checking suit sensors, searching, watching for trouble, or moving quietly. Analyze Environment checks environmental conditions your protection covers. Search looks for hidden things, and Scout gives the group an initiative bonus. Usually choose one ongoing activity at a time.
>
> You can ask what your character knows about a creature, machine, or situation with Recall Knowledge. Ask a specific question. You can also describe how you help another person and use Aid.
>
> If we have ten minutes and the right equipment, we can attempt repairs and medical treatment. And in a fight, Take Cover, Step, Demoralize, or helping an ally can be worth more than another attack.

Do not teach every card at once. Keep the shared deck on the table; hand out the relevant skill or vehicle cards when the situation arises.

## Reminders during the session

| Scene | Helpful prompt | GM distinction |
|---|---|---|
| Waking at the wreck | “Do you want to check your suit's environmental readings? Who is searching, and who is tending injuries?” | Analyze Environment does not automatically reveal the vent's electrical eruption or every hidden hazard. |
| Recovering trapped equipment | “The bracket has accessible fasteners. Who handles it, and can someone help?” | Your special suit-recovery check uses the consequences in your prep. Do not replace it with generic Disable a Device outcomes. |
| Damaged terminal or recorder | “Is the problem broken hardware, restricted access, or understanding what it displays?” | Use Repair, Hack, or Operate Device as appropriate. Preserve the basic clue promised by the scene. |
| An unfamiliar creature | “You can ask what your character knows before choosing your next action.” | Recall Knowledge needs a relevant skill and specific question. Visible feeding behavior can be described without a check. |
| PPPPP or Fresh-Breath | “Are you trying to improve their attitude, ask a favor, assess their behavior, or pressure them?” | Make an Impression, Request, Sense Motive, and Coerce have different effects. A normal conversation need not become a sequence of checks. |
| Grinrunner's controls | “Board, get to the controls, Take Control, then Drive. If it is moving, keep driving or Stop.” | Taking control does not bypass security or automatically displace an unwilling pilot. |

## Grinrunner reminder

The homebrew truck has Piloting DC 16, AC 13, Speed 35 feet, and collision 2d8 bludgeoning (DC 16). A 1-action Drive can move up to 35 feet with normal turns on a success; reckless 2- and 3-action Drives can move up to 70 and 105 feet in a straight line, with the 3-action version imposing -5 on its control check. Those are encounter distances, not long-distance travel rates. Board while moving uses Acrobatics or Athletics against AC 13. Its normal turning limit is 90 degrees per 20 feet traveled. Conditions can raise Piloting DCs.

Only one vehicle move action/activity can occur per round. The Drive inside Stunt is part of that activity, not permission for a second independent vehicle move. Stunt is printed as 1 action in Player Core 205 and includes Drive; its interaction with multi-action Drive and the duration of its AC bonuses are not fully spelled out there. Before using the optional Stunt rules, a practical table ruling is to use the 1-action Drive within Stunt and let its AC bonus last until the start of the pilot's next turn. That is a suggested ruling, not additional printed rules. Ordinary Board, Take Control, Drive, and Stop are sufficient for the first drive.
