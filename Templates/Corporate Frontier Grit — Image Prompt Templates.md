# Settlement and Facility Template

> Create a wide establishing view of **[settlement, extraction facility, station, colony, or worksite name]**, a **[facility type and approximate population or scale]** located in **[planetary environment, moon, asteroid, cavern, orbital location, or unusual system]**.
>
> The location was built for **[mining, refining, surveying, logistics, habitation, research, salvage, or corporate administration]** and currently functions as **[active facility, declining work camp, abandoned installation, independent settlement, emergency refuge, or contested site]**.
>
> Organize the environment around visible functional zones:
>
> - **[landing pad, docking area, or vehicle access]**
> - **[ore processing, drilling, refinery, or industrial machinery]**
> - **[worker housing, barracks, mess hall, or civilian district]**
> - **[storage containers, tanks, cargo stacks, and maintenance areas]**
> - **[administrative office, communications tower, or corporate terminal]**
> - **[hazardous, damaged, restricted, or unexplained area]**
>
> Architecture uses modular prefabricated structures, pressure doors, cylindrical tanks, stacked cargo units, exposed braces, gantries, ladders, service platforms, pipes, cable runs, and practical floodlights. Expansion should appear uneven, with newer modules attached to older infrastructure.

>
> Apply **[heavy, moderate, minimal, damaged, or painted-over]** Erbium Industries branding. Corporate markings remain geometrically clean even when the structures carrying them are worn.
>
> Environmental conditions include **[dust, wind, ice, low gravity, rain, toxic atmosphere, volcanic ash, mineral haze, darkness, or unusual stellar light]**. Use practical illumination from floodlights, vehicle lamps, windows, emergency beacons, and work areas.
>
> Include one restrained indication of local danger or mystery: **[sealed tunnel, unexplained mineral growth, abandoned equipment, distant structure, sensor array aimed at empty terrain, damaged quarantine barrier, or workers avoiding a particular area]**.

It survives in **[gravity, atmospheric pressure, temperature, radiation, terrain, available food, and other environmental conditions]**.
>
> Build its anatomy around practical adaptations:
>
> - **Locomotion:** [legs, gripping limbs, fins, membrane wings, burrowing plates, low-gravity propulsion, or anchored movement]
> - **Feeding:** [jaw structure, filtering organ, mineral scraping surface, proboscis, crushing plates, chemical digestion]
> - **Senses:** [heat detection, vibration sensing, polarized vision, pressure sensitivity, electrical fields, scent organs]

>

>
> Show evidence of how workers encounter it: **[ore dust on its hide, damage from industrial equipment, nesting in warm machinery, feeding on waste, following survey lights, attacking vibration sources, or avoiding corporate deterrent markers]**.
>
> **Presentation:** creature concept sheet showing **[full-body side view, three-quarter view, scale comparison, head detail, movement study, or habitat vignette]**. Use a plain background for reference art or a restrained environmental background for ecological context.

# Consistency Checklist

Before generating, specify:

- What is its function?
- Who manufactured, owns, or maintains it?
- What environment was it designed for?
- What has happened to it since manufacture?
- Where does the Erbium branding appear?
- What parts move, open, detach, or require maintenance?
- What should remain readable at thumbnail size?
- Is the output concept art, a game asset, a portrait, an icon, or a scene?
- Should the background be transparent, neutral, or environmental?
- Which details must remain consistent across future images?
