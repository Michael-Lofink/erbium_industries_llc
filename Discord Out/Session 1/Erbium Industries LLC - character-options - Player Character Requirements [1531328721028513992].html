Ancestral Paragon Variant Rule and the Skill Paragon Variant Rule:

--- When creating an ancestry paragon character, instead of starting with one ancestry feat and gaining another at 5th, 9th, 13th, and 17th levels, the character starts with two ancestry feats and gains another at every odd level thereafter (3rd, 5th, 7th, 9th, and so on) for a total of 11 ancestry feats. When creating a skill paragon character, after selecting the character's class, choose a specific skill. The character becomes trained in it. If they were already trained in it, they become trained in another skill instead of their chosen skill. At 3rd, 7th, and 15th levels, they gain an additional skill increase they can apply only to their chosen skill. They automatically gain all common general skill feats that specifically requires proficiency in the chosen skill as a prerequisite as soon as they qualify for those feats. If they already gain one of those feats (such as from a background or heritage), they instead gain Assurance for the chosen skill or, if they already have Assurance for that skill, a related Lore skill. Not all skills have the same number of feats, and some skill choices will end up granting more bonus feats than others. Characters with two or more fewer bonus Skill Paragon feats than any other character in the party gain their choice of the Additional Lore skill feat in a category related to their chosen skill, or the Assurance, Automatic Knowledge, or Experienced Professional skill feat in their chosen skill or a related Lore skill. --- (edited)

Backstory

As you begin working on where your characters came from make sure that you include an npc or group that you would do nearly anything for, an npc or group that's after you or you are after them or both, and a motivation to complete your service (I don't want any of you bailing on the plot now do I :P). I REQUIRE that your character have a theme song, it can be the inspiration for the character or the character can inspire the theme song either is fine. Any kind of song is fine: lyrics, no lyrics, 70's Jazz, Futurepunk; but I should be able to listen to the song and get an idea of who your character is. As mentioned previously, closer to game day we will be doing a cooperative writing project together. This will be a scene (from your backstory) with your character that we will write together, and on Session day there is a good chance we will act out this scene in tandem to stimulate intrigue about your character. This is not a character exposition dump, but something that establishes something about your character whether it be what they want, how they got here today, something that happened to them--essentially something pivotal you'd like to develop in narrative detail and be willing to share with the whole group. This is intended to be cooperative, we are building this together, please do not come to me with a finished idea. (edited)
